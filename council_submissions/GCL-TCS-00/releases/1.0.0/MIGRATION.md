New identifier: `GCL-TCS-00@1.0.0` (candidate pending G0–G9).

The charter is assembled from the seven protected 0.1.0 source parts. The explicit changes are version references from 0.1.0 to 1.0.0, the charter date from 2026-07-27 to 2026-09-05, and a conditional supersession statement. Policy, schemas, and templates receive the corresponding version-reference changes. Profile identifiers remain P01–P07; their bundle version becomes 1.0.0. Changing a version string does not establish conformance. Example template identities remain illustrative and must never be treated as review evidence.

There is no automatic authority inheritance from pilot readiness or earlier G0–G7 pilot records. The pinned predecessor evidence supports review of unchanged controls; reviewers must assess its applicability to this exact package. Historic candidate and pilot files remain intact.

Rollback before G9 means withdrawing this candidate without changing the historical 0.1.0 package. After any future G9 admission, supersession or withdrawal requires an explicit governed transaction retaining the released identities and review provenance. No release tag, publication or authority mutation is performed by this package.
