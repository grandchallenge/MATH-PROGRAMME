# CM5 R0: finite generation through the actual bar total

## Verified finite-column transfer

For a first-quadrant bicomplex of integer modules, if every column
homology on a fixed total-degree diagonal is finitely generated,
then the homology of the actual signed total complex in that degree
is finitely generated.

Use the filtration of the total homology by images of cycles supported
strictly before column r. In the next filtration stage, the top-column
coordinate of a total cycle is a vertical cycle. If its vertical
homology class vanishes, choose a vertical primitive. Insert that
primitive with the sign of the vertical differential, and subtract its
total boundary. All coordinates at and above the top column vanish,
and the total homology class is unchanged. The argument includes
column zero and vertical degree zero.

FiniteImage supplies the algebraic step over any commutative
Noetherian ring. For maps f and g with common domain, suppose the
image of the kernel of g under f is contained in a finitely generated
submodule P, and the target of g is finite. The image of f modulo P
factors through the image of g, so is finite. Its intersection with P
is finitely generated by Noetherianity. Therefore the image of f is
finitely generated. Apply this with f the map to total homology and g
the map to the top-column homology. The empty stage is zero, and the
stage after column n contains all degree-n homology.

## Application to the genuine Eilenberg–MacLane model

FiniteBar applies the theorem to the actual normalized reduced bar
total. Column zero vanishes; each positive column is homotopy
equivalent to the corresponding tensor power of the actual reduced
chains. The diagonal and next-object comparisons transport finite
generation to the next Eilenberg–MacLane degree. In particular
finite_next is a kernel-checked conditional induction step, with
precisely the required finite generation of tensor-power homology
on the given total-degree diagonal.

No spectral-sequence convergence claim, additional axiom, or
independent certification is used. Remaining inputs are the initial
circle-model finite homology and the finite-generation theorem for
tensor powers of the reduced free complexes (for example via finite
free replacements over the integers). These must be proved before
claiming finite generation of every stable coefficient.
