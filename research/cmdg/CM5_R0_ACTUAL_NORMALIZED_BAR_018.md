# CM5 R0: actual normalized bar construction and tensor support

## Formal disposition

The positive-degree bottom homology isomorphism is protected in
PR 3733. This successor formalizes the reduced bar object,
the exact normalization quotient, and the genuine tensor-complex
support bound. It does not claim the higher stable suspension
isomorphism yet.

The TensorSupport module sends the actual reduced Dold–Kan
complex to integer modules and constructs its tensor powers
using Mathlib's total tensor complex. If a complex begins in
degree n, its p-fold tensor power begins in degree pn.
Tensoring with the single complex of the tensor unit in degree p
makes the suspended layer begin in degree p(n+1). In particular,
for p at least two all these layers have zero homology below
degree 2n+2. These are actual chain-term vanishing proofs.

ReducedBasis identifies the actual augmentation kernel with the
free abelian group on nonzero group elements. The basis is given
by the reduced generators, each original generator minus the
identity generator. BarTupleQuotient constructs the split
surjection from the free group on tuples onto the free group on
tuples with no zero coordinate. Its kernel is exactly the
subgroup generated by tuples containing a zero coordinate.
The pointwise tensor basis comparison sends an actual tuple to
the tensor of its reduced generators and is natural for every
homomorphism, including ones that send a nonzero coordinate to
zero. The comparison induces an isomorphism of alternating
vertical chain complexes.

BarObject constructs the actual simplicial bar group using
edge coordinates. A tuple is integrated into vertex potentials
by partial sums. Pulling back along any ordinal map and taking
adjacent differences defines the simplicial map. The identity
and composition laws follow by telescoping. Each actual
degeneracy is proven equal to zero insertion, so the subgroup
generated by degeneracies agrees exactly with the tuple
quotient kernel.

NormalizedBar uses the actual reduced free group functor on
that simplicial bar object, then Mathlib's normalized Moore
complex. In positive horizontal degree its genuine Moore
projection factors through the tuple quotient. The inverse
comes from the nondegenerate representatives and the Moore
projection, with the degeneracies killed by the canonical
normalization projector. The resulting isomorphism is natural
in the group. Thus the actual normalized horizontal column,
after the alternating vertical differential, identifies with
the pointwise reduced tensor chains. The length-one column
identifies canonically with the original reduced Dold–Kan
complex.

BarTotal defines the actual bicomplex and its totalization,
with horizontal degree as the outer index. Its degree-zero
horizontal column is zero. The first column is identified
with the original reduced chains. It also defines the diagonal
of the actual bisimplicial bar group and its reduced chains.

## Exact remaining comparison

For the actual simplicial group X given by the Dold–Kan
Eilenberg–MacLane construction, the two tensor objects are
different constructions:

- the alternating chains on the pointwise tensor powers of
  the reduced free simplicial module;
- the total tensor powers of the alternating reduced chains.

A natural Eilenberg–Zilber equivalence (Alexander–Whitney and
shuffle maps with the chain homotopy identities) must identify
their homology. The support bound of TensorSupport cannot be
silently transferred across this missing comparison.

The next proof must also compare the actual normalized bar
total complex to the reduced chains of the actual diagonal,
and compare the diagonal bar group with the next Dold–Kan
Eilenberg–MacLane object by a genuine weak-equivalence route.
A strict simplicial isomorphism between the latter objects
is not presumed. The filtration argument must retain the
canonical first-column inclusion and the relevant two
adjacent degrees of vanishing. The result then yields
stable suspension, rather than only an isomorphism of
abstract groups.

These proofs use the pinned Mathlib revision and standard
Lean axioms; local checks do not constitute independent
attestation, protected admission, or MATHCERT. Higher stable
homology, its finite generation and lattice additivity,
pseudocoherence, and the final condensed comparison remain open.
