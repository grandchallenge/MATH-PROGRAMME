# CM5 R0: lattice pseudocoherence and coherent resolution (042)

## Result

The ordinary integer-lattice identity functor V is FP in every degree, with finite projectives generated by zero, free representables and binary biproducts. This is an unconditional Lean theorem on the actual lattice skeleton, not a finite-rank observation or an assumption of the Breen–Deligne resolution.

A single infinite augmented exact complex with these finite polynomial projectives has also been constructed. Its successive kernels are FP in every degree. This follows by Schanuel comparison for an arbitrary chosen finite-projective cover, rather than by assuming that independently constructed finite truncations agree.

## Proof spine

* Every finitely generated integral coefficient module has a finite free presentation, since its relation module is a finitely generated torsion-free module over the PID of integers. Transport through the stable matrix action gives natural short exact presentations of stable homology functors on the actual lattice category.
* Schanuel comparison and the split pullback construction give independence of cover, direct-sum and retract closure, extension closure and the degree-shifting cokernel rule for FP.
* The approximate-resolution lemma descends from the last required degree via the actual categorical cycles, boundaries and homology. Its degree requirements are explicit.
* The genuine reduced Eilenberg–Mac Lane lattice complex is augmented by the bottom homology comparison. Its higher augmented homology is naturally isomorphic to the corresponding stable functor; its terms are retracts of finite free representables.
* Strong induction on the FP degree uses the augmented complex at stage r+2 to obtain FP at degree r+1. The first augmented homology is zero; the other required homologies have lower FP degrees by the coefficient presentations.
* Recursive choice of covers constructs successive kernels and the differentials as each cover followed by the previous kernel inclusion. Schanuel comparison gives all FP degrees for each successive kernel. The augmentation is epic and exactness is proved at every term.
