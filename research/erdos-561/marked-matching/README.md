# Erdős #561 — marked matching deficiency closes the remaining conditional three-star cases

**Date:** 2026-11-19. The complete general mathematical argument is in `SHARP_SATURATED_MATCHING_DEFICIENCY_THEOREM.md`.

**New theorem:** In any finite simple graph of maximum degree at most an **even** integer `D`, a matching covers all but at most `floor(k/(D+1))` of its `k` vertices of degree exactly `D`. This bound is sharp on disjoint odd cliques `K_(D+1)`. The proof uses a classical designated-vertex Tutte matching theorem and an exact small-versus-large odd-component boundary count. No novelty of the classical matching criterion is asserted.

**Degree-four corollary:** In any graph with maximum degree ≤4 and ≤29 edges, a matching misses at most two degree-four vertices. Two explicit matching-based red/blue edge colourings then (A) avoid any red target containing a two-edge star and any blue target containing three disjoint four-edge stars, or (B) avoid three disjoint red two-edge stars and any blue target containing a four-edge star.

**Exact three-star effect:** The preceding protected-source parameter certificate contained **22 ordered** low-degree method survivors (11 up to exchanging red and blue). They split precisely as **4** cases solved by colouring A and **18** by colouring B. This leaves **zero method-survivors** among three-star target pairs whose largest component on each side has at least two edges, **conditional on the earlier unpromoted joint-three-star residual theorem and other paper-level reductions**. It is NOT an unconditional proof of Erdős #561; the central joint-residual theorem still needs rigorous independent mathematical adjudication. This package does not address the cases with one largest component of size 1 or unrestricted numbers of stars.

**Independent verification:** `verify_marked_matching_exact.py` compares an exact custom matching dynamic program with a completely separate odd-component deficiency enumeration for all 685 qualifying NetworkX graph-atlas types, 260 larger seeded graphs and disjoint sharpness examples from K5 to 4K5 (20 vertices). `verify_coloring_constructions.py` constructs both red/blue colourings using a different NetworkX matching implementation and directly verifies all 685 atlas graph types under both schemes and 330 target-specific exact-edge-count graphs. `verify_final_three_star_gate.py` checks the exact 4/18 partition of the complete 22-case input. `verify_chain_reconciliation.py` cross-compares every ordered pair from the earlier protected-source 75-,39-,25-,22-case lists down to zero.
