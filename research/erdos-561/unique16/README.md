# Erdős #561: uniqueness of the 16-edge subcubic obstruction

**Research result (2026-11-19):** The unique uncolourable simple graph with 16 edges and maximum degree at most 3 for red/blue degree caps (1,2) is two copies of a seven-edge five-vertex block connected by their one low-degree vertex to a common degree-two cut vertex.

The proof is unbounded and uses the published ordinary Lovász (g,f)-factor theorem, not an exhaustive finite graph database. A further theorem shows that **every** subcubic (1,2)-cap obstruction with at most 18 edges contains this same motif, plus at most two exterior edges. Its numerical tight-component classification has an independent exact 72-case check. This unique graph admits another colouring with only six red-active and six blue-active vertices, avoiding *any* pair of three-positive-star targets with largest star ≥2. It excludes **4** additional ordered small three-star cases *conditional on the earlier paper-level joint-residual theorem*. The 16-edge result closes 4 ordered cases; the 17/18-edge extension closes 10 more. Remaining programme shortlist: **25 ordered / 13 unordered** method-survivors, **all D4**, with no D3 survivors. The unrestricted conjecture remains OPEN.
