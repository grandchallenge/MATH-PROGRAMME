# EXT-015 REACH / UC048 — failed lifts for the actual completed weight model

**Candidate result.** In the differentiable module-sheaf model of 129, the actual completed weight sequence is not projectively cofibrant at any nonempty fixed profinite test. Its projections onto finite weight truncations are not fibrations, and the adjacent projections which drop an even weight are not fibrations. This includes the actual empty-open object and the constructed model on a nonempty open. The accepted replacement-based completed bar and Weiss proof remains available on its original domain.

## 1. Objects and a natural vacuum retract

The fixed-test coefficient functors and the actual product-weight objects $V_w^S(U)$, $V^S(U)$, and their tails are exactly (129.2)–(129.3). Take any nonempty profinite test $S$. Work in ZFC and choose one point of $S$ for the retractions. The parameter singleton evaluation below is a distinct functor from evaluation on $S$.

At every even weight $2k$ the scalar summand is $\hbar^k\mathbf C(-)(S)$ in cochain degree zero, by [135.6–7](SOURCE_CONTROLLED_COMPLETED_WEISS_135.md). At the empty physical open, the other summands vanish. For an arbitrary physical open, transport by the actual weight-preserving Wick isomorphism of 111/129, which conjugates the quantum differential to the arity-preserving classical differential. Inclusion and projection of the arity-zero slot are then cochain maps. Transport them back with the actual Wick isomorphisms. This constructs a chain retract at that weight; it does not assert that a naive arity projection commutes with the quantum differential. The real scalar unit on the smooth parameter sheaf with sections constant on $S$ has a retraction given by pullback to the chosen point of $S$ and the actual zero-vertex finite coefficient. The composition is the identity by [114](QUANTUM_UNIT_COMPARISON_114.md) and [126](COMBINED_PARAMETER_LIQUID_REALIZATION_126.md). The scalar retraction is real-linear, preserves the parameter module and connection, and is a cochain map. No multiplicativity premise is needed. It follows that the usual smooth-functions unit, in degree zero, is a retract of each even weight and of any full infinite tail, by choosing a single even weight in that tail.

## 2. The actual sequence fails projective cofibrancy

The parameter evaluation $\Gamma_*:A\to\mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb R}$ preserves colimits and monomorphisms. For the first fact, colimits of these module sheaves are given by sheafifying the underlying sectionwise colimit; the induced flat connection is the colimit connection, by linearity, the finite-projective one-form rule on each chart, and its naturality. On the parameter singleton the open-cover sheafification changes nothing. In the local-diffeomorphism presentation a nonempty covering of the singleton has a section, which gives the same assertion. The second fact follows from the exact section calculation in 129 §7, or directly from sectionwise kernels. In particular degreewise parameter evaluation preserves colimits, retracts, and monomorphic maps of complexes.

If the actual sequence in $\mathrm{Ch}(A)$ were projectively cofibrant, it would be a retract of a transfinite cell object built from generating free sequence cofibrations $P_j(B)\to P_j(C)$ as in 129 §7. Each base generating cofibration is degreewise monomorphic. Evaluation at the parameter singleton commutes with $P_j$ and these pushouts, transfinite colimits and retracts. The evaluated free maps are projective diagram cofibrations in $\mathrm{Ch}(\mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb R})$, since their base maps are monomorphisms in the injective base model there. Thus the evaluated sequence would be projectively cofibrant. This argument uses the valid generating-cell implication, without using the disputed C.5.6.1 converse.

The actual evaluated sequence has, by section 1 weight by weight, a cochain diagram retract

$$
G_j=\prod_{2k\ge-j}\mathbb R[0],\qquad
G_j\longrightarrow G_{j+1}\quad\text{by zero extension},\quad j\le0 .
\tag{UC48.1}
$$

Use the retraction onto the real unit from each even vacuum coordinate, with all other coordinates zero; these maps commute with the actual split tail transitions. Cofibrancy is closed under retracts. Here is a failed projective lift for this particular vacuum retract. Let $E=\bigoplus_{j\le0}P_j(G_j^0)$ in real vector-space diagrams with its evident epimorphism to $G^0$ summing the inclusions. It is onto at each index $i$ by the summand $j=i$. The augmented two-term diagram $[K\hookrightarrow E]\to G$ in degrees minus one and zero, with $K=\ker(E\to G^0)$, is an objectwise trivial fibration. Indeed its kernel at each index is a disk with identity on $K_i$. A degreewise monomorphism extends maps into that disk by extending the linear functional with values in $K_i$ in degree zero. The disk is injective as a complex of vector spaces. The levelwise epimorphism then has the required lifting by splitting off its injective kernel. These are the same literal model properties as 129 §6.2, now evaluated on the actual retract.

A projective lift of $0\to G$ would supply a diagram section $G^0\to E$. Its index-zero component in the summand labelled $j$ vanishes on $G_{j-1}^0$, by naturality and the definition of $P_j$. It therefore has finite-dimensional image, because $G_0^0/G_{j-1}^0$ is a finite initial block of even weights. The composite back to $G_0^0$ consequently has countable-dimensional image, as the sum of countably many finite-dimensional spaces. It cannot be the identity: sequences $(1,t,t^2,\ldots)$ on the even weights, indexed by $t\in\mathbb R$, are linearly independent by the finite Vandermonde determinant. This contradicts projective cofibrancy of the actual sequence. Its degreewise base monomorphisms remain the proved cofibrations of 129. The same argument works at the empty open using its exact scalar formula.

## 3. The actual projection kernels fail the fibrancy test

Let $\mathscr O(M)=C^\infty(M)$ with its ordinary flat connection. On the same real parameter site use the module sheaf $\mathscr J$ of arbitrary pointwise assignments of a full formal scalar Taylor jet:

$$
\mathscr J(M)=\prod_{a\in M}\widehat{\mathcal O}_{M,a}.
$$

Here each factor is the completed real Taylor algebra at $a$. The assignments have no cross-point regularity requirement. A smooth map pulls the jet at the image point back by Taylor composition. Multiplication by smooth functions uses their full Taylor jets. In local coordinates the connection differentiates a formal Taylor series in its formal variable at the point. This takes values in the formal Taylor one-forms, identified with $\Omega^1(M)\otimes_{\mathscr O(M)}\mathscr J(M)$ by the finite-projective one-form description of 123. Rules in different coordinates agree by the formal chain rule, including pullback under an arbitrary smooth map. Commutation of formal partial derivatives proves flatness and the ordinary Taylor Leibniz rule gives the connection identity. Descent for local diffeomorphism covers glues the data at each point because local diffeomorphisms identify the Taylor algebras. Hence this is an actual object of the checked module-sheaf category.

The natural full Taylor map $\mathscr O\hookrightarrow\mathscr J$ is monic and preserves the connection. It has no retraction. A retraction would be the identity at the parameter singleton, since $\mathscr J(*)=\mathscr O(*)=\mathbb R$. By naturality under all point inclusions, its value on an arbitrary assignment on $\mathbb R$ would be a smooth function with those assigned constant terms. Assign constant term one at zero and zero at all other points, with all higher terms arbitrary, for instance zero in the standard coordinate. This section of $\mathscr J(\mathbb R)$ has no smooth function with its constant terms. This tests naturality in the actual category, without relying on an endomorphism of bare point values.

Make an acyclic monomorphism by applying the disk in cochain degrees zero and one, with differential the identity, to $\mathscr O\hookrightarrow\mathscr J$. The map from the first disk to $\mathscr O[0]$ is the identity in degree zero and zero in degree one, a chain map. A lift against $\mathscr O[0]\to0$ would have in degree zero precisely the prohibited natural retraction. Thus $\mathscr O[0]$ fails the actual injective-model fibrancy condition. By section 1 it is a cochain retract of each even coefficient weight and of each full tail. Fibrancy is closed under retracts, so all those objects fail fibrancy. Lemma 129.2 now gives failure for the actual projections $V^S\to E_N$ for every $N$, and for $p_N:E_{N+1}\to E_N$ when $N$ is even. This witness does not decide each odd adjacent projection.
