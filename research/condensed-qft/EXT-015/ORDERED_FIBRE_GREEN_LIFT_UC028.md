- **A genuine collision.** Take $D=\delta_0(v_c)$ with a compact singleton cutoff, $f=1$ near zero with compact support, and $a_\epsilon(u,v_c)=\kappa_\epsilon(u-v_c)$ with cutoffs equal to one on the needed support. This tests the normalized Jacobian and a nonzero collision contribution. This is a control of the Green integral, before the two moment corrections and the $C_2$ subtraction.
- **Hidden variables and coupled regulators.** With one hidden $d$ remaining in $v$, a permitted example of the scalar is $\kappa_\epsilon(u-v_c)\kappa_\epsilon(u-v_d)P_\epsilon(v_c-v_d)$ with smooth compact cutoffs. These factors use one old integrated coordinate and do not split across the retained blocks. The scalar proof allows this full product, all old collision corners, and every fixed finite derivative before the physical projection. The remaining distributions are kept through the scalar integration and only then pushed forward in the admitted 108 form.
- **Original graphs outside the proof.** Near $u=v_c=0$ take $T=\eta(u,v_c)\delta(u+v_c)$ with a fixed cutoff equal to one near the joint origin. Its conormal is proportional to $(1,1)$ and therefore avoids the forbidden diagonal conormal. On a small interval of $u$, the slice is $\delta_{-u}$ in the other variable. The derivative of order $k$ in $u$ is, up to sign, $\delta^{(k)}_{-u}$, which lies in $H^{-s}$ only if $s>k+1/2$, by the Fourier norm of the delta derivative. No fixed finite $s$ gives every required derivative, so the original normal cone does not imply the coefficient hypothesis, even for a single valid distribution. Viewing $u-v_c$ instead as the slicing variable still translates the delta in the center and incurs the same unbounded derivative loss. The scalar pushforward theorem by itself gives no continuous factorization of this general mixed type.

The last control excludes an implication from the original cone condition to the additional smooth-family hypothesis. It gives no nonmembership evidence for the actual Green output of that graph or for any alternative representation. The remaining inference is a finite-output completed-source lift for arbitrary distributional dependence on the integrated block in the original 150.L domain.
