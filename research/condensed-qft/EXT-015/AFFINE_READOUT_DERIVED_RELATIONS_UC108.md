# EXT-015 follow-on / UC108 — derived relations of the affine collision readout

**Theorem.** For the renormalized free scalar on the line, use the admitted collision-moment algebra of a finite union of intervals and its affine readout. In the complete filtered joint liquid category the derived relative tensor of two affine inputs over that algebra is the completed affine algebra tensored with the exterior space of independent block relations. A block of size $b\ge2$ contributes its $b+1$ relations in weight $b$ and cohomological degree minus one. The formula is natural for inclusions and intertwines disjoint structure maps. In weight two a merger of two components into one adds the two triples of relation classes. A mixed product of singleton blocks stays in cohomological degree zero.

## 1. Actual algebras and the natural choice of relations

Fix $0<p\le1$, ZFC and the admitted Gaussian/Wick scale and whole-source quotient. Work in the complete filtered joint category of UC099 and UC105 with regulator scalar $K=\mathbf C$. Its monoidal product is the completed Day tensor. Put $B^\wedge=\mathbf C[[\hbar]]$ in total weight with $\hbar$ of weight two. On the physical domain of finite unions of open intervals, including the empty open, use the polynomial multiplication on the model $D(U)$ in UC093.1. UC093 identifies this structure on disjoint input regions with the renormalized Wick maps by its actual comparison. All same-open algebra products below refer to this explicit moment algebra.

For the underlying ordinary real polynomial model let $M_b(U)=\bigoplus_{I\in\pi_0(U)}\mathrm{Pol}_{\le b}(I)^*$ in weight $b$. These are the ordinary real forms of the moment generators. Write $X(U)=M_1(U)$ and $J(U)=\bigoplus_{b\ge2}M_b(U)$. Define real graded algebras

$$
D_{\mathbb R}=\mathbb R[\hbar]\otimes\mathrm{Sym}_{\mathbb R}(X\oplus J),
\qquad
P_{\mathbb R}=\mathbb R[\hbar]\otimes\mathrm{Sym}_{\mathbb R}(X).
\tag{UC108.1}
$$

Suppress $U$ in formulas within one region. Let $\pi:D_{\mathbb R}\to P_{\mathbb R}$ be the affine readout of UC093 §4. It is the identity on $\hbar$ and on singletons. Each generator $\xi\in M_b(U)$ maps into $\mathrm{Sym}^b_{\mathbb R}(X)$ by the dual of affine multiplication on its indicated component, with the normalized symmetric pairing of UC093. Let $\sigma:P_{\mathbb R}\to D_{\mathbb R}$ be its algebra section given by the singleton generators. Define

$$
\rho(\xi)=\xi-\sigma\pi(\xi),\qquad \xi\in J .
\tag{UC108.2}
$$

Replacing the higher block generators by $\rho(\xi)$ gives a polynomial isomorphism $P_{\mathbb R}\otimes\mathrm{Sym}_{\mathbb R}(J)\simeq D_{\mathbb R}$ over $P_{\mathbb R}$. The inverse takes each old higher generator to the new generator plus its specified singleton polynomial. The transformed augmentation $\pi$ is zero on the new generators. The section, readout and generator change commute with every inclusion: each block generator is carried by the dual restriction from its old interval to the containing interval, and affine multiplication commutes with restriction. This also covers an inclusion that merges components. Thus $\ker\pi$ is generated by the $\rho(\xi)$ and its first conormal quotient is $P_{\mathbb R}\otimes J$. The choice of ordinary coordinates on any interval is immaterial to this identification.

## 2. Computation of the completed derived tensor

Recall the following coefficient construction from UC102 and UC105. For a nonnegatively weight-graded real complex $V$, send its weight $a$ to the step $P_a(K\odot V_a)$ in the joint derived category, then take the coproduct in the complete filtered category. Here $\odot$ is the real copower on complexes from UC102 §3, built by basis coproducts with actual column-finite real maps. Denote the resulting functor by $\mathfrak F$. Real complexes split into cohomology and contractible pairs, so this construction carries quasi-isomorphisms to equivalences. It preserves coproducts and cofibers by its chain construction and exact coproducts, and hence preserves geometric realizations. It is symmetric monoidal by the real copower tensor rule, the step product (UC105.5) and separate cocontinuity. The copower rule identifies on pure weight complexes the tensor with the corresponding tensor of their real coefficient complexes. It follows on basis vectors by separate cocontinuity, and real matrices, total signs and the associated canonical maps give the compatibility with differentials and symmetry.

Under $\mathfrak F$, the algebra $\mathbb R[\hbar]$ gives $B^\wedge$, and $D_{\mathbb R},P_{\mathbb R}$ give precisely the completed algebras $D(U),P(U)$ and the completed affine readout. For these split weight models, a finite quotient of the coproduct takes the finite sum of its graded terms below the cutoff. Its limit is the actual weight product of that coefficient model by UC099. There is no commutation of an ordinary tensor with an infinite product in this argument. The functor carries the real two-sided simplicial bar to the completed two-sided bar, including both actions, and carries its realization to that realization. The latter computes the actual derived relative tensor over $D(U)$ by the monoidal construction of UC105 and the two-sided bar formula. [Lurie, Higher Algebra, Construction 4.4.2.7 and Corollary 4.4.2.15](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~lurie/papers/HA.pdf#page=576)

Here is the real computation and its natural comparison. Resolve $P_{\mathbb R}$ as a left $D_{\mathbb R}$ module by the Koszul complex with terms $D_{\mathbb R}\otimes\Lambda^q J$ in degree $-q$, where each exterior generator has its original weight and the differential sends $\xi$ to $\rho(\xi)$. To check exactness use (UC108.2), treating $P_{\mathbb R}$ as coefficient ring. In a basis of $J$ with polynomial variables $r_i=\rho(\xi_i)$ and exterior variables $e_i=\xi_i$, take $h_0=\sum_i e_i\wedge\partial_{r_i}$. With the Koszul differential $\sum_i r_i\,\iota_{e_i^*}$ its anticommutator is multiplication by polynomial degree plus exterior length. Each sum is finite on each monomial, and the operator preserves total weight. Divide by this integer on every positive eigenspace. The remaining component is $P_{\mathbb R}$ in degree and auxiliary degree zero, so the augmentation is a resolution. The terms are graded free and the construction works with all block weights, with finite-polynomial elements before completion.

There is a natural chain map from this resolution to the normalized left-module bar resolution $\mathrm{Bar}(D_{\mathbb R},D_{\mathbb R},P_{\mathbb R})$, sending a wedge to the signed sum

$$
d\otimes(\xi_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\xi_q)\longmapsto
\sum_{\tau\in S_q}\mathrm{sgn}(\tau)\,
d[\rho(\xi_{\tau(1)})|\cdots|\rho(\xi_{\tau(q)})]\otimes 1.
\tag{UC108.3}
$$

First faces give the Koszul differential, intermediate faces cancel in pairs by commutativity, and last faces vanish on the positive-length wedges by $\pi\rho=0$. In length zero the augmentation map is the same on both resolutions. The bar resolution is exact by the extra degeneracy inserting the unit on the left and has graded free terms after normalization. The comparison is a homotopy equivalence of graded left modules: the cone is a bounded-above exact complex of graded projectives, which splits successively from its top degree. Thus applying ordinary $P_{\mathbb R}\otimes_{D_{\mathbb R}}(-)$ to the comparison still gives a homotopy equivalence. The resulting Koszul differential is zero, and the resulting bar is the normalized complex of the two-sided relative bar. Weight is preserved throughout. In the two-sided bar the shuffle product concatenates the signed wedge comparison: shuffles and the internal permutations in (UC108.3) give each permutation of the concatenated list once with its sign. After applying the inclusion maps, termwise multiplication of the simplicial bars gives this product on realization via the shuffle map. The products are balanced over the shared vacuum by its action on the left affine end of every term. This gives the induced product in cohomology for disjoint input opens.

Consequently there is a natural equivalence in the completed category, with the following representative and its exterior multiplication on cohomology:

$$
P(U)\widehat\otimes^L_{D(U)}P(U)
\simeq
\mathfrak F\bigl(P_{\mathbb R}(U)\otimes\Lambda^\bullet J(U)\bigr).
\tag{UC108.4}
$$

Here each entry of $\Lambda^q$ lies in cochain degree $-q$. At a fixed weight the formula uses only finitely many weights and exterior lengths. The naturality and product assertions use (UC108.3) and the linearity of $\rho$ on generators. They do not require natural splittings of the bar resolutions. The unit on the empty open is $B^\wedge$ since there are no generators. The calculation retains the single vacuum parameter.

## 3. The two-interval test

Let $U=I_1\sqcup I_2$ consist of disjoint nonempty intervals and let $I$ be a single interval containing both. The first relation term is in weight two. In that associated weight the degree-minus-one cohomology is precisely $K\odot M_2(U)$, because each exterior generator has weight at least two and the weight-zero affine coefficient is $K$. The inclusion map is

$$
K\odot\bigl(\mathrm{Pol}_{\le2}(I_1)^*\oplus\mathrm{Pol}_{\le2}(I_2)^*\bigr)
\longrightarrow K\odot\mathrm{Pol}_{\le2}(I)^*,
\qquad (u_1,u_2)\longmapsto r_1^*u_1+r_2^*u_2 ,
\tag{UC108.5}
$$

where $r_i$ is restriction of a polynomial on $I$ to $I_i$. Both restriction maps are isomorphisms because a polynomial is determined by its restriction to an open interval. In the common basis dual to $1,x,x^2$ the map is $(u,v)\mapsto u+v$. Its kernel is exactly the antidiagonal triple and its image is the full target triple. The three target classes include the quadratic moment with the corrected normal derivative of UC092. Their representatives are the actual readout differences (UC108.2), with the original regulator scalar and the three pair-block moments of UC093.

A product of one singleton from each interval belongs to the singleton polynomial subalgebra fixed by $\sigma$. Its image after merging is the product of their two affine generator images, in degree zero. On the four-dimensional mixed real singleton tensor this is ordinary symmetrization onto the three-dimensional symmetric square after identifying both singleton bases. The antisymmetric direction then vanishes in degree zero. It yields no new degree-minus-one class, which by (UC108.4) has only genuine block relation generators at this weight. A further sign control occurs in weight four. For either one of the three common coordinate moment classes, its two separate interval copies have nonzero exterior product before merging, while the image is the wedge of that class with itself and vanishes. The relation map and its product are thus different computations from the ordinary cross-singleton multiplication.

## Scope and exact inputs

The computation applies to the moment algebras carrying the admitted Wick maps on the specified renormalized free-scalar class. The coefficient construction acts by actual joint liquid scalar maps, with the flat parameter connection and the existing source comparison. In particular the proof uses the UC093 map on the whole-source rapid quotient. The auxiliary relative tensor in (UC108.4) uses the stated same-open polynomial multiplication. The original support-valued degree-two comparison has its own objects and hypotheses.
