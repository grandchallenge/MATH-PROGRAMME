## 3. Relative derived comparison and completion

An occupancy has finitely many occupied intervals, so UC073 gives its actual finite free moment model of rank $\prod_I d_{\alpha(I)}$. The finite-interval homotopies act within the occupancy and can be fixed using only its occupied intervals. Adding unused intervals only inserts the scalar unit. Applying the finite occupancy projections and the same cochain identities on each interval gives a coproduct homotopy model in arity $n$, with its original coordinate support and parameter connection. Its section inserts the actual exterior products of the compact moments. [UC073 §§4–5](FINITE_PARTITION_TENSOR_UC073.md) [UC076 §3](ARBITRARY_SEPARATED_OPENS_INTEGRATION_UC076.md)

Every component of the union belongs to exactly one input because the inputs are clopen in the union. An occupancy with moment choices on the union is therefore exactly a tuple of occupancies with moment choices on the inputs. The product on those generators is the disjoint exterior product with the established graded signs and placement normalization. Adjoining the one common vacuum variable gives a coproduct of shifts of $B$ in cochain degree zero. This model is K-flat over $B$, because tensoring with it sends a complex to a coproduct of its grading shifts, and exact coproducts carry acyclic complexes to acyclic complexes. For the liquid-valued parameter sheaf, the exact coproduct is computed locally using the compact projective generators and manifold sheafification. The argument also holds for the directly constructed free parameter module sheaves over $B_S$. It needs neither flatness of the original distribution objects nor evaluation of a general derived tensor.
