## 3. Two concrete integration obstructions

### 3.1 The refined and ambient plot structures are not interchangeable

The oscillatory kernel family of 033 has admissible fibers and is smooth in the ambient strong distribution sense, while its delta contraction jumps from a fixed nonzero value to zero. If its identity lift into $C_{\mu,N}$ were smooth, composing with the smooth zero-scale contraction of 035/069 would give a smooth scalar function. This contradicts 033. Thus the inverse forgetful identity in the third row cannot be declared smooth.

This is a failure of a particular identity map of plot structures, not a proof that every derived comparison between unrelated models is impossible.

### 3.2 Collision counterterms lie outside the microlocal domain

After a nonzero smooth compact localization along a collision diagonal, its delta distribution has forbidden nonzero conormal directions in its wavefront; over points where the coefficient is nonzero it has every such direction. The defining D21 domain excludes those conormals. A nonzero finite normal derivative-delta jet is likewise singular with nonempty wavefront contained in that excluded conormal, so it also fails the defining admissibility condition.

Consequently a Gaussian renormalized sector containing such outputs does not embed into $C_{\mu,N}$ by the identity on distributions. This is not repaired by saying that both are distribution-valued. An integrated theory must actually extend the domain and specify its renormalized operations, parameter plots and transport; it cannot borrow the microlocal closure proof unchanged on the excluded strata.

The finite-state obstruction in 071 further shows that the stated insertion finite-part contract cannot be recovered from one fixed Laurent cutoff, even for a fixed nonzero insertion on its full polynomial module. It does not choose the required richer domain or prove global BV/QME.
