## 2. Actual complete source stages

Fix arity $n$, an interval $U$, a compact physical support $K\Subset U^n$, and a sufficiently small regulator interval $(0,e_0)$. Use the full collision resolution $X_n$ and blowdown $\beta_n$ of096.8. Only the boundary over $\epsilon=0$ enters the retained boundary; an upper regulator endpoint is treated by an ordinary collar.

Cover the compact resolved region over $[0,e_0]\times K$ by finitely many corner charts. Each extends to an open smooth coordinate neighborhood $\Omega_i$ allowing signed boundary coordinates. Fix global positive boundary defining functions $\rho_H$ on the resolved space; their local smooth extensions are used only to form normalized kernels. Choose a finite nonnegative pole vector $\mathbf a=(a_H)$ and write $\rho^{\mathbf a}=\prod_H\rho_H^{a_H}$.

On each $\Omega_i$ choose a closed conic set $\Gamma_i$ which avoids:

- the full conormal of every face and corner of the resolved positive quadrant;
- conormals of positive physical-regulator level sets in that quadrant.

Use separation from their limiting directions as well: at a zero-scale corner those limits lie in the span of its boundary conormals. This is a fixed-cone condition on each stage, not a pointwise reassignment to each parameter. Empty cones admit weighted smooth functions.

Let $E_\lambda$ be the subspace of $\prod_i\mathcal D'_{\Gamma_i}(\Omega_i)$ whose normalized components $v_i$ satisfy these linear conditions: the weighted distributions $\rho^{-\mathbf a}v_i$ agree on positive-quadrant overlaps, and their physical interior evaluation has support in $K$. Diffeomorphism pullback and multiplication by smooth units implement the overlap conditions. They are tested only where the boundary weights are nonzero. Data outside the positive quadrant may differ.

**Lemma99.1.** Each $E_\lambda$, with the induced product normal topology, is a complete Hausdorff locally convex space, and its condensed realization is liquid.

**Proof.** The new closed-cone theorem makes every factor complete. Overlap equality is the kernel of a continuous map into distributions on an open positive overlap. The support condition is the kernel of restriction to the complement of the specified physical support. These targets are Hausdorff; hence the conditions define a closed subspace of a finite product of complete spaces. No completeness of a quotient is used.

For a complete Hausdorff locally convex space, integration of a continuous profinite plot exists by finite clopen approximation, Cauchy in every seminorm. Bounded variation gives continuity on bounded measure balls; restriction to every smaller-exponent measure object gives the liquid criterion, as checked in096 and097. Thus $\underline E_\lambda$ is liquid. This does not make it projective or prove tensor flatness. $\square$

Transversality to positive regulator slices makes the actual evaluated family smooth in the regulator as a distribution-valued family; its test pairings are smooth scalar functions. Normal-topology continuity of pullback on each compact positive-regulator subinterval proves continuity of all these pairing maps on $E_\lambda$.

With physical compact kernels extended by zero to $\mathbb R^n$, define the ordinary envelope

$$
\mathscr E_n=
\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}_{\mathrm{Liq}_p}
\left(\underline{\mathcal S(\mathbb R^n)},
\underline{C^\infty((0,e_0))}\right).
\tag{99.1}
$$

Evaluation gives liquid maps $e_\lambda:\underline E_\lambda\to\mathscr E_n$. For currying, the test pairing is a condensed bilinear map, not an assertion of joint topological continuity of distribution/test duality. On each compact positive-regulator interval, noncharacteristic slicing and its derivatives are continuous into strong distributions with the fixed physical support. Duality with tests is hypocontinuous on bounded sets in both variables. Its uniform seminorm estimates give the same property with target $C^\infty$ on that interval. A continuous profinite plot into each factor has compact, hence bounded, coefficient images; splitting a difference into its two factor changes proves continuity of the paired plot in every target seminorm. This supplies a bilinear map of condensed modules, hence the liquid tensor/internal-Hom adjunction supplies the displayed map. The interval is exactly the one on which the families are defined.

## 3. Full formal face jets, with all weights retained

For a face stratum $F_I^\circ$ with vanishing boundary coordinates indexed by $I$, take every normal derivative of the normalized $v_i$ and restrict it to that stratum. The full corner-conormal exclusion makes each restriction noncharacteristic. The derivatives and restrictions are continuous in the fixed normal topologies. Encode them as

$$
z_I^{-\mathbf a_I}
\sum_{\alpha\in\mathbb N^I}
\frac{1}{\alpha!}
\left.\partial_{\rho_I}^{\alpha}v_i\right|_{F_I^\circ}
z_I^\alpha .
\tag{99.2}
$$

Boundary factors not in $I$ are invertible smooth functions on the stratum; their actual factors are included in the coefficients. Coefficients are distributions on the open stratum, not presumed smooth or Schwartz. Use all strata, including corners, rather than only the open faces.

These records form a liquid target $\mathfrak J_n$. Concretely, on each stratum chart use the product of complete distribution spaces for the Taylor coefficients, then the filtered union over componentwise finite Laurent pole bounds. Changes of boundary defining functions have the form $\rho'_H=u_H\rho_H$ with $u_H$ a smooth nonvanishing unit. Taylor substitution and the chain rule implement the change of normal frame: every individual coefficient depends on finitely many coefficients, tangent distributional derivatives and pullback by a stratum diffeomorphism. These are continuous linear maps. Compatibility on overlaps is an equalizer of those maps. Thus it defines a liquid module. Laurent transitions are monic; the formal parameters are retained and are not set to zero.

This construction is also valid using scalar pairings with all compact stratum tests to describe the coefficients. Equality of all such pairings is equality of the distribution coefficient. No assertion is made that every formal coefficient array is realized by a global mixed kernel.

The maps $j_\lambda:\underline E_\lambda\to\mathfrak J_n$ given by (99.2) are liquid. Increasing the displayed pole vector changes (99.2) by the corresponding formal monomial, leaving the meromorphic formal record unchanged. Chart and collar choices therefore have the specified comparison, rather than a graph-dependent extra label.

## 4. Image theorem on every profinite test

Put

$$
W_n=\bigoplus_\lambda\underline E_\lambda,\qquad
e=\sum_\lambda e_\lambda,\qquad j=\sum_\lambda j_\lambda,\qquad
\mathcal R_n=\mathrm{im}(e)\subset\mathscr E_n .
\tag{99.3}
$$

The index ranges over the admitted finite stages just described, in the chosen size universe. This is a categorical image in the liquid heart. In particular its positive-scale families are identified by actual test-pairing equality.

**Theorem99.2.** The full jet record descends uniquely to $\mathcal R_n$. More precisely,

$$
\ker(e)\subset\ker(j),\qquad
J:\mathcal R_n\longrightarrow\mathfrak J_n,\qquad
\mathcal B_n=\mathrm{im}(J)
\tag{99.4}
$$

are well-defined liquid maps and a liquid boundary image. They are independent of the chosen presentation by collar extensions. Their construction supplies the evaluation/jet kernel test for this explicit domain.

**Proof.** First take finitely many ordinary collar extensions whose evaluated interior families sum to zero. Enlarge their pole vectors to a common finite vector and compare them on a common positive corner chart. The sum of the normalized distributions vanishes on the positive interior. Face/corner transversality makes it a smooth function of the normal variables with distributional tangential coefficients, up to the boundary. Every one-sided derivative at every face and corner therefore vanishes. This proves zero of every coefficient in (99.2), including lower coefficients after pole cancellations. No boundary-supported delta is allowed by the fixed conormal exclusion.

Now let $T$ be any profinite test and $k\in\ker(e)(T)$. It is, by the kernel inclusion, an actual section of $W_n(T)$. The free liquid module on $T$ is compact in the abelian heart by091, so this section has only finitely many summands. Each summand is a continuous $T\to E_\lambda$ plot, since that complete locally convex realization is already liquid. At every point of $T$ the preceding ordinary-family argument gives zero formal jet record. In a common finite Laurent-bound stage, every coefficient is an actual continuous distribution-valued plot. Scalar test pairings separate it, so the entire record on $T$ is zero. This use of points concerns these concrete source plots and coefficient spaces; it is not point detection for arbitrary internal-Hom sections. It proves the kernel containment on every profinite test.

The abelian image factorization now gives the unique $J$ in(99.4). Changing extensions, normalized weights or charts while preserving the actual family changes a representative by an element of this kernel, and hence changes no boundary record. $\square$

### Profinite plots are stated honestly

A section of $\mathcal R_n(T)$ need not lift globally through the image epimorphism $W_n\to\mathcal R_n$. General profinite free objects are compact, not projective. Choose an extremally disconnected cover $S\to T$. Projectivity on that cover gives a lift; compactness gives finitely many complete stages there. Equality on the cover's kernel pair is equality of the actual evaluated families. Theorem99.2 ensures equality of their jets, and sheaf descent supplies the boundary section on $T$.

This describes the admitted plots: locally on a profinite cover, they have one finite collection of continuous fixed-cone collar extensions, with actual-family descent. It does not silently impose arbitrary-profinite projectivity or identify these plots with a weaker ambient subspace topology. For manifold parameters use locally finite-stage Bastiani-smooth extension coefficients. Continuous linear source maps preserve their derivatives, and relations of equal actual families preserve parameter derivatives and their boundary records. This gives only the specified restricted enrichment, not a universal enrichment of all liquid modules.

## 5. Required inputs actually lift

### Localized concentrated profiles

The uniform Schwartz stages of096 have weighted smooth lifts on $X_n$. In a normal chart $y=\epsilon u$, normalize by $\epsilon^{d+h}$ and retain $F(\epsilon,u)$. Where $|y|/\epsilon$ escapes, arbitrarily high Schwartz tail bounds absorb the finite rational factors introduced by every chart derivative. This proves smooth flat matching in the complementary charts, with seminorm bounds continuous in the original $P_d$ topology. This is the same estimate used for physical cutoffs in096, now with the finitely many blowup coordinate factors.

After physical compact localization, these are scalar smooth sections up to the corners. Smooth corner extension to signed collar coordinates is continuous linear: the scalar smooth extension with seminorm bounds ([Hanusch,2002.05125v2, Theorem1 and Application1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2002.05125v2)), iterated over finitely many faces and patched by a smooth partition of unity, has each output seminorm bounded by finitely many input smooth seminorms. Therefore these families lift continuously to stages with empty wavefront cone. This scalar smooth extension is not asserted to preserve a specified wavefront cone for arbitrary distribution-valued normal families; the R1 and jet extensions below use their actual geometric constructions instead.

A physical cutoff is part of this comparison. Multiplication by a cutoff constant near the collision need not be injective on the enlarged positive-scale $\mathcal C_d$: it can remove a nonzero regulator-flat tail. On the original finite-homogeneity $\Phi_d$ it is faithful near a collision patch. At each fixed $u$ equality holds for all sufficiently small $\epsilon$, forcing every finite Laurent coefficient to vanish. This is not an assertion of injectivity for all regulator-dependent profiles.

The map on the boundary is stronger. The local regulator-flat kernel maps to zero in every normalized global face jet: all regulator powers and all Schwartz tails are available to absorb each fixed chart pole. Conversely zero global jets imply zero local full Taylor profiles on the front-face chart with finite $u$. By the profinite Borel epimorphism of096, the induced map from $\mathcal N_d$ into the local boundary image is monic. Centre variables carry specified compact smooth coefficients; fixing a nonzero such coefficient gives this injection on each collision patch. This is an explicit local comparison, not a claim that an uncut relative kernel has compact physical support.

In particular the boundary is nonzero. An all-physical-moments-zero profile remains a nonzero full normal profile here. No physical Laurent series has replaced the actual boundary.

### The regular R1 sector

A fixed compact normal R1 sector is a closed-support subspace of $\mathcal D'_\Gamma(U^n)$, hence complete by the new input and closedness of support. It therefore has a liquid realization; take the filtered union over the same fixed supports and admissible closed cones, retaining finite-stage profinite plots. Proposition96.2 pulls every such sector continuously to the resolved space, including face and corner restrictions. Thus it provides actual continuous collar-extension source maps. The limit of a positive-regulator slice conormal at a corner lies in the corner conormal span, already excluded by the same proof. The output cones can consequently be chosen to satisfy the source-stage conditions.

The resulting maps are

$$
L_{\mathrm{R1}}(U)\hookrightarrow\mathcal R_n
\xrightarrow{J}\mathcal B_n .
\tag{99.5}
$$

The first arrow is monic because its evaluated family is the original constant-in-regulator kernel. It preserves the original locally normal manifold plots, through their actual fixed-sector lifts. This is an underlying liquid/DVS comparison, not a comparison of derived field duals or a new Weiss theorem.

The composite in(99.5) is also monic. On the side boundary away from collisions its value is the original $T$. If it vanishes, $T$ is supported in the finite union of collision diagonals. A nonzero distribution supported on a smooth stratum has nonzero conormal wavefront there, by its finite normal-jet representation. Choose a maximal nonzero support stratum and apply that observation; it contradicts the R1 exclusion of every partial-diagonal conormal. Descending through the finite strata proves $T=0$.
