### Closed-sector completeness check

Dabrowski–Brouder, [Corollary 25](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1308.1061), proves completeness of the normal-topology distribution space with a fixed closed wavefront cone. The sector with support in one fixed compact is a closed subspace: convergence in the normal topology implies distributional convergence, and pairing with every test supported outside that compact remains zero. Thus each fixed-support closed-cone sector is complete. This does not pass completeness or regularity to their ordinary global inductive union; 075 proves the failure of that separate assertion.

### What the equivalence actually compares

It does not identify this restricted positive model with the positive-scale theory containing every compact distribution. It also does not identify the normal plots with the ordinary global LCS plots. Those stronger identifications are not substituted into the R1 verdict.
