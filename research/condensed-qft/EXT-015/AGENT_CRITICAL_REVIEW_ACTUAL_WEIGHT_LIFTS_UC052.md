## Projective sequence obstruction

At fixed $S$, evaluation at the parameter singleton is a different functor. Colimits of the underlying module presheaves use the sectionwise module colimit. On each chart the finite cotangent idempotent is compatible with that colimit, so the compatible connections induce a flat connection there. Sheafification inherits this connection by restriction and gluing. At the parameter singleton, open covers have an identity member. In the local-diffeomorphism presentation a nonempty cover splits over the singleton, so matching sections likewise give the original value there. Thus parameter singleton evaluation preserves colimits. It also preserves monomorphisms by sectionwise kernels. The same facts apply degreewise to complexes and then to diagrams. This reasoning needs no exactness assertion for evaluation on the original profinite test. [123 §3](RETAINED_FAMILY_DIFFERENTIABLE_LIQUID_AUDIT_123.md) [129 §7](SOURCE_CONTROLLED_PROCOCHAIN_APPLICABILITY_129.md)

In the genuine projective model, a cofibrant sequence is a retract of a transfinite cell object with free generating base cofibrations. The free diagram $P_j$ is zero before $j$ and takes the same base complex at every index at or after $j$. The evaluated generating base maps are degreewise monomorphisms, hence cofibrations in the injective model on vector-space complexes. Their free diagrams are projective cofibrations by the evaluation adjunction and objectwise definition of trivial fibrations. Parameter point evaluation commutes with these free diagrams, their pushout attachments, transfinite colimits and retracts. Consequently projective cofibrancy of the original sequence would imply projective cofibrancy of its evaluated sequence and of the even-vacuum diagram retract (UC48.1). [129 §§6.2, 7](SOURCE_CONTROLLED_PROCOCHAIN_APPLICABILITY_129.md)

For that retract $G$, the sum of free diagrams $E$ maps onto $G^0$ at each index, since the summand born at that index maps by the identity. The kernel $K$ gives an augmented two-term complex $[K\hookrightarrow E]\to G$. Its objectwise kernel is the identity disk on $K$ in degrees minus one and zero. The disk is an injective complex of vector spaces: a chain map into it is determined by its degree-zero component, which extends along a degreewise monomorphism by linear extension. The objectwise epimorphism therefore splits off this kernel and has the right lifting property against every base cofibration. This proves the required objectwise trivial fibration. The splittings need only be objectwise for that test.

A diagram section would give a degree-zero section at the terminal index $0$. Its component in the summand born at $j$ kills the image of $G_{j-1}^0$ by naturality, since that summand vanishes at $j-1$. This component factors through a finite even-weight block. Summing back through $E_0$ therefore gives a composite with countable-dimensional image. The even-vacuum product contains the uncountable linearly independent family of sequences of powers, with every finite independence test witnessed on its initial coordinates by the Vandermonde determinant. Thus the composite cannot be the identity. This shows the actual evaluated diagram could not have arisen from a projectively cofibrant source diagram. The actual tail inclusions still satisfy the base degreewise monomorphism criterion. [UC048 §2](ACTUAL_PROJECTIVE_SEQUENCE_OBSTRUCTION_UC048.md)

## Actual disk obstruction for projection kernels

The formal Taylor assignment sheaf $\mathscr J$ has independent jets at all parameter points. A smooth parameter map pulls back the jet at the image point by Taylor composition. Local diffeomorphisms induce invertible maps on these formal local algebras, so compatible pointwise assignments glue on the stated covers. The algebraic cotangent tensor can be checked by representing $\Omega^1(M)$ as a finite idempotent summand of a free smooth module. The induced idempotent on the product of local Taylor algebras has image the product of formal one-form modules. Thus differentiation in each formal variable takes values in the actual cotangent tensor required by the category. Formal differentiation commutes with coordinate change by the formal chain rule, acquires the Taylor Jacobian under arbitrary smooth pullback, and has commuting partial derivatives in every chart. It also takes the Taylor expansion of a smooth multiplier to the expansion of its ordinary derivative. These facts verify naturality, flatness and the module connection rule without requiring regularity across assignment points. The Taylor map $\mathscr O\hookrightarrow\mathscr J$ is a monomorphism preserving the connection. A retraction would be the identity on the singleton and would therefore assign to every arbitrary Taylor assignment a smooth function with those constant terms, by naturality along point inclusions. Constant terms supported at one point of the line contradict even continuity. The identity disks in degrees zero and one on this monomorphism give an acyclic cofibration. The map from the first disk to $\mathscr O[0]$ is the identity in degree zero, and any extension to the second disk would give the impossible retraction. This is a failed lift in the actual module-sheaf category. In any kernel containing this retract, the obstruction persists by composing the disk map with the retract inclusion and any putative lift with its projection.
