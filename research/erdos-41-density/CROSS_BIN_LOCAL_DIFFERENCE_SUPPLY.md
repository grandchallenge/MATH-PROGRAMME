# Erdős #41: density-forced local outer differences and the cross-bin correlation gate

**Mathematical status.** Proved conditional finite-combinatorial consequence of hypothetical persistent critical B3 density, with exact multiplicity accounting. **Not** a proof of the additional cross-correlation lower bound, and **not** a solution of Erdős #41.

## 1. Density forces a localized outer difference reservoir

An integer B3 set has unique unordered three-summand sums with repetition. Write $A(t)=|A\cap[1,t]|$, and assume **hypothetically**

$$
A(t)\ge c\,t^{1/3}\quad\text{for every sufficiently large }t,
\qquad 0<c\le1.
\tag{DENS}
$$

Finite triple counting gives $A(t)<3t^{1/3}$. Set $R=(6/c)^3$, and $W=A\cap(N/R,N]$. For every sufficiently large $N$,

$$
|W|\ge(c/2)N^{1/3}.
$$

Fix the integer $J=\lceil R^2\rceil$, independent of $N$. Partition the real interval $(N/R,N]$ into J consecutive coarse intervals of equal length. One contains a subset $Z\subseteq W$ of size

$$
\boxed{m:=|Z|\ge {c\over2J}N^{1/3}},
\qquad
\boxed{\mathrm{diam}(Z)\le {N\over J}\le {N\over R^2}.}
\tag{COARSE}
$$

Next partition that coarse interval into $B=\lceil N^{1/6}\rceil$ equal fine intervals $Z_b$ (empty bins permitted). Each fine interval has diameter at most $N^{5/6}$. By B3 triple counting **inside any translated interval of diameter D**, an s-element B3 subset satisfies

$$
\binom{s+2}{3}\le 3D+1,\qquad s^3\le18D+6.
$$

It follows that for $N\ge1$, every fine-bin occupancy satisfies

$$
s_b:=|Z_b|\le (18N^{5/6}+6)^{1/3}
\le 24^{1/3}N^{5/18}<3N^{5/18}.
\tag{FINE}
$$

For sufficiently large $N=N(c)$, (COARSE) and (FINE) ensure $\max_bs_b\le m/2$. The ordered pairs with their two endpoints in **different fine bins** then number exactly

$$
D_{\mathrm{inter}}=m^2-\sum_b s_b^2
\ge m(m-\max_b s_b)\ge \frac12m^2.
$$

Half these ordered pairs are positively oriented. Because B3 implies B2, **every positive ordered difference of two distinct elements is unique**. Thus each such pair contributes a different integer h.

**Theorem 1 (density-sensitive localized outer-difference supply).** Under (DENS), for every sufficiently large $N$ there is a set $\mathcal H_N$ of *actual positive outer differences*, generated by elements in separate fine bins of the same coarse interval, such that

$$
\boxed{\mathcal H_N\subseteq (W-W)\cap[1,N/R^2],\qquad
|\mathcal H_N|\ge {c^2\over16J^2}N^{2/3}.}
\tag{LOCAL}
$$

This is a lower bound on **distinct arithmetic differences**, not the number of representations with duplicates. Its fixed positive constant depends only on c. It is a genuine consequence of the *persistent* lower-density hypothesis; an isolated dense finite B3 prefix does not imply it at all large N.

The estimate goes beyond merely counting all differences across the entire outer interval. It forces order $N^{2/3}$ distinct outer differences **inside the relatively short inner-compatible window** $[1,N/R^2]$.

## 2. Exact cross-bin correlation identity and compatibility with inner scales

Choose any disjoint inner B3 blocks $X_i,X_j\subseteq A\cap[1,N/R^2]$, below W. Let $P_i=\{a+b:a,b\in X_i\}$, and write

$$
R_{ij}(t)=|\{(p,p')\in P_i\times P_j:p-p'=t\}|.
$$

Define the centered mixed-lag fibers with **all outer centers W**,

$$
f_i^W(d)=|\{(p,z)\in P_i\times W:p-2z=d\}|,
\qquad
O_{ij}(W)=\sum_df_i^W(d)f_j^W(d).
$$

The previously admitted exact correlation identity says

$$
\boxed{
O_{ij}(W)=\sum_{h\in(W-W)\setminus\{0\}}R_{ij}(2h)
}
\qquad(i\ne j).
\tag{CORR}
$$

There are no repeated-difference coefficients in (CORR), because B3 implies Sidon uniqueness of every nonzero ordered difference. In particular, (LOCAL) yields the **exact, nonnegative arithmetic sub-sum**

$$
\boxed{
O_{ij}(W)\ge\sum_{h\in\mathcal H_N}R_{ij}(2h).
}
\tag{GATE}
$$

The cross-bin difference set has *many* genuine h-values in the correct magnitude range: every $|2h|\le2N/R^2$, as do all pair-sum cross-differences $p-p'$ of inner points in $[1,N/R^2]$. Thus a failure of (GATE) to force overlap cannot be blamed merely on a lack of outer differences of compatible order of magnitude.

**However, $|\mathcal H_N|\gg_c N^{2/3}$ is insufficient to prove $\sum_{h\in\mathcal H_N}R_{ij}(2h)>0$.** Distinct subsets of the same interval can avoid each other, even when both contain many values. A lower bound on cardinality alone is not an intersection theorem. We have no density-sensitive result proving that the actual cross-pair sums $P_i-P_j$ hit twice these outer differences.

The missing lemma is now exact and falsifiable: under (DENS), select **which** inner blocks, and prove a sufficiently large lower bound for

$$
\sum_{h\in\mathcal H_N}R_{ij}(2h)
$$

after summing over an explicitly specified *nonredundant* complete collection of inner-block pairs. It must survive finite critical B3 families and the sparse infinite B3 examples already tested. There is no mathematical justification at present for claiming such a positive lower bound.

## 3. Relation to the previous no-overlap selection theorem

The preceding [density-localized zero-overlap theorem](DENSITY_LOCALIZED_ZERO_OVERLAP.md) narrows the outer-center set to a single fine bin Y. Inner lag intervals can then be made pairwise disjoint and the same-bin terms vanish. The complete outer W, by contrast, must contain polynomially many populated fine bins under (DENS). Theorem 1 strengthens this by producing $\gg_c N^{2/3}$ distinct **inter-bin differences at inner-compatible magnitudes**, all inside one short coarse interval.

That condition does **not** say these differences intersect any given inner cross-pair spectrum. The identity (CORR) cleanly separates the two mathematical obligations: (a) abundant actual outer differences, now proved conditionally, and (b) excess intersection with inner pair-sum differences, **open**.

## 4. Executable evidence and proof status

Exact tests should verify: ordinary B3 triple counting in an arbitrary translated interval; unique nonzero positive ordered differences; the partition count $m^2-\sum s_b^2$; the resulting distinct short differences; and the exact (CORR)/(GATE) identity on genuine finite B3 fixtures. A non-B3 set with repeated differences is a required hostile control. Rational parameter fixtures verify the exponents and constants but are **not evidence that the infinite density hypothesis is realizable**.

A pinned Lean algebraic receipt may check simple constants, but neither the hypothetical existence of a positive-density infinite B3 set nor the universal conditional theorem is claimed to have been formalized end-to-end in Lean.

**Disposition:** localized $\Omega_c(N^{2/3})$ distinct outer-difference supply is proved conditionally. Cross-scale collision from these outer differences is **not** proved. Erdős #41 remains **OPEN**; no Solve/MATHCERT promotion, novelty priority, or publication is asserted.

Relevant context: C. Táfula, *Infinite Sidon-type sets for zero-sum linear forms*, *Monatshefte für Mathematik* 211 (2026), 315–327, DOI: 10.1007/s00605-026-02211-4. The odd-order problem is identified as open; the paper's gap hypotheses and pairwise-distinct ordered tuple convention are not interchangeable with the repetition-permitted B3 setting here.
