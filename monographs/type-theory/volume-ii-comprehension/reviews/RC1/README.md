## Independence boundary

A review produced by the composing process, an internal audit, CI, regression testing, rendering inspection, or machine-assisted checking alone does not satisfy Gate 8. The final mathematical judgment must be attributable to a genuinely distinct reviewer who declares independence and conflicts.
