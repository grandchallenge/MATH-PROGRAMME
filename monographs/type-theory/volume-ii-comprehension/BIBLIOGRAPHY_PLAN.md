# Bibliography Plan — Volume II: COMPREHENSION

## Primary-source priorities

- Per Martin-Löf: early papers/notes on intuitionistic type theory and the 1984 *Intuitionistic Type Theory* record.
- N. G. de Bruijn: Automath and dependent formal language sources.
- John Cartmell: generalized algebraic theories and contextual-category work.
- Thierry Coquand and Gérard Huet: Calculus of Constructions.
- Peter Dybjer: inductive families / inductive definitions in type theory.
- Sources for early comprehension-category terminology and categorical semantics of dependent types.

## Modern expository anchors

- Nordström, Petersson, and Smith, *Programming in Martin-Löf's Type Theory*.
- Martin Hofmann, dependent-type syntax/semantics surveys.
- Bart Jacobs, *Categorical Logic and Type Theory*.
- Robert Harper, *Practical Foundations for Programming Languages*.
- Selected Agda/Idris/dependent-programming sources for indexed-data examples, used as programming evidence rather than historical priority evidence.

## Historical/date claims requiring verification

1. chronology of Π/Σ terminology and notation;
2. relation among contextual categories, categories with families, and comprehension categories;
3. provenance of inductive-family terminology;
4. Russell-style versus Tarski-style universe terminology;
5. exact publication/circulation dates when citing foundational lecture notes.

## Citation discipline

Historical attribution will be strengthened during the publication pass. This plan is not itself evidence for a priority claim. Where the manuscript says “first,” “introduced,” or assigns a date, the bibliography audit must contain a checked source.
