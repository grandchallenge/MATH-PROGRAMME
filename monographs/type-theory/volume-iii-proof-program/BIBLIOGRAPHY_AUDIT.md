## Attribution policy applied

1. “Curry–Howard” is treated as a later collective name for a historical lineage, not a single-origin event.
2. The manuscript distinguishes type assignment, formulae-as-types, normalization/reduction, later control interpretations, and parametricity rather than attributing them all to one source.
3. Gentzen/Prawitz cut/normalization results, Kripke soundness, strong normalization, and Reynolds parametricity are identified as imported theorem families where the volume does not supply a complete independent proof.
4. No novelty, priority, patentability, or “first” claim is made.
5. Where circulation/publication history is materially complex (especially Howard and proceedings records), the manuscript uses conservative bibliographic wording.

## Gate-5 disposition

**PASS — scholarship closure for the internal development manuscript.** Independent mathematical review remains separate.
