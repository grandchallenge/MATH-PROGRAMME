# MATHSOLVE Routing Policy

## Binding invariant

> Every Grand Intellect mathematical struggle, including every MATH-PROGRAMME campaign, is governed through MATHSOLVE or explicitly exempted. Full lineage is preserved in GitHub now and projected into AETHER later. Every resulting mathematical claim is handed to MATHCERT.

## Institutional allocation

- MATH-PROGRAMME charters campaigns, fixes scope and policy, and controls programme promotion.
- MATHFORGE supplies audited sources, discovery records, candidate mechanisms, and exact exploratory artifacts.
- MATHSOLVE owns theorem spines, route selection, proof obligations, exact screens, failed routes, restricted targets, and certification handoffs.
- MATHCERT owns certification, rejection, qualification, and proof-debt status.
- INTELLECT governs the constitutional lifecycle.
- GitHub is the present authoritative record. AETHER is a future semantic projection and must not be represented as authoritative before conformance.

## Required record

Every active campaign carries either:

1. a route pinned to an exact MATHSOLVE commit, manifest path, and Git blob identity; or
2. a reviewed waiver with bounded scope, approving authority, reason, risk, and review date.

Silence is not a waiver.

The route also records:

- native or retrospective coverage;
- current repository placement;
- Solve Work Package identifiers;
- exact MATHFORGE provider input;
- MATHCERT state and issue where available;
- promotion blockers.

## Repository placement

A retrospective manifest may register historical Solve-owned work that remains embedded in MATH-PROGRAMME. It preserves the source repository and commit and records migration debt.

Retrospective registration does not authorize continued misplacement. After registration, future theorem-spine, proof-attempt, route-selection, failure-ledger, restricted-target, or mathematical mechanism work must occur in MATHSOLVE unless a waiver is approved.

MATH-PROGRAMME may retain programme-facing summaries, policy records, promotion records, documentation, and stable links to provider artifacts.

## Promotion gates

The executable gate in `ci/validate_mathsolve_routing.py` applies to:

- WP00;
- WP01;
- WP02;
- restricted-target selection;
- mechanism work;
- mathematical claim promotion;
- integration.

A missing route or waiver fails closed.

Programme-embedded retrospective work may be registered at WP00, but it cannot advance to a future Solve-owned stage while remaining embedded.

A claim cannot be promoted or integrated unless its MATHCERT state records a complete handoff outcome or explicit proof debt. `pending` and `partial` are not promotion states.

## Authority transition

1. GitHub authority: repository commits, manifests, reviews, CI, issues, and artifact identities are authoritative.
2. AETHER shadow projection: GitHub lineage is projected without changing authority.
3. Dual-record conformance: disagreement fails closed.
4. AETHER semantic authority: institutional state and derived readiness may move to AETHER while GitHub remains authoritative for repository artifacts.

## Binding maxim

> No Grand Intellect mathematics without a Solve route.  
> No Solve route without lineage.  
> No mathematical claim without a Cert handoff.  
> No promotion by implication, omission, or repository accident.
