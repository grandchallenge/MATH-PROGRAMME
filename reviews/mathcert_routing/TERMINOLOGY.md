# MATHCERT routing terminology

- **Handoff ready:** MATHSOLVE has produced a complete content-addressed packet. MATHCERT has not necessarily accepted it.
- **Submitted:** MATHCERT has acknowledged intake. No adjudication has been issued.
- **Adjudicated:** MATHCERT has issued `certified`, `qualified`, `rejected`, or `proof_debt` with an exact output identity.
- **Positive disposition:** `certified` or `qualified`.
- **Route registry:** The MATHCERT portfolio record at `governance/certification_routes.json`.
- **Packet identity:** The MATHSOLVE handoff path and Git blob recorded by MATH-PROGRAMME.

Do not use `ready`, `submitted`, policy-green, merged, promoted, reviewed, or documented as synonyms for mathematically certified.
