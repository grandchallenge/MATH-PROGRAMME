# Claim boundary

This review package establishes repository identities, artifact identities, route state, and lifecycle gate semantics.

It does not establish the truth of a mathematical proposition. It does not convert a MATHSOLVE packet into a MATHCERT disposition. It does not authorize a claim about an open problem.

Only a content-addressed MATHCERT output with an adjudicated state may close the Cert disposition gate. Only `certified` or `qualified` may support positive claim promotion.
