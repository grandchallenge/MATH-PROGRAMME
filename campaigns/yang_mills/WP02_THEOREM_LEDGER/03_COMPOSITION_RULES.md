# YM-WP02 Composition Rules

## Match before composition

A theorem interface may be composed only when the intended argument matches its:

- spacetime dimension;
- gauge group and rank regime;
- regulator and lattice action;
- finite- or infinite-volume convention;
- coupling regime;
- observable class;
- normalization and physical units;
- quantifiers and direction.

A result that differs in any field is an analogue or a new bridge obligation, not an interchangeable premise.

## Composition states

- `COMPOSABLE_*`: usable only within the suffix-qualified scope.
- `NONCOMPOSABLE_*`: unusable until every `residual_hypothesis` and corresponding debt item is discharged.
- `OPEN_NOT_COMPOSABLE_AS_A_PREMISE`: the terminal target may not be assumed.
- `UNVERIFIED_COMPLETE_SOLUTION_CLAIM`: documentary intake only.
- `COMPOSABLE_AS_STATUS_ONLY`: establishes institutional status, not mathematics.

## Forbidden implicit bridges

The following transitions require explicit theorem records:

1. finite volume to infinite volume;
2. fixed lattice spacing to continuum;
3. dimensionless gap to physical mass;
4. reflection positivity to complete OS reconstruction;
5. selected correlator decay to full spectral exclusion;
6. strong coupling to the continuum critical trajectory;
7. perturbative asymptotic freedom to nonperturbative existence;
8. two or three dimensions to four dimensions;
9. large `N` to finite `N`;
10. gauge-fixed auxiliary fields to the positive physical sector.

## Source-state rule

A `NEEDS_THEOREM_BODY_AUDIT` or `UNVERIFIED_COMPLETE_SOLUTION_CLAIM` source cannot support a theorem-grade premise. Repository presence, publication, citation count, numerical agreement, or manuscript wording does not change that state.
