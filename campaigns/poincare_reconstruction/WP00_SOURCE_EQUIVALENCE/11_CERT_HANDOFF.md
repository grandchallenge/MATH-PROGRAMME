# PC-WP00 MATHCERT handoff

## Purpose

The first certification slice checks the terminal logical and combinatorial structure of the Poincaré proof without pretending to formalize Ricci flow, canonical neighbourhoods, surgery existence, or finite extinction.

```text
imported geometric theorem interfaces
  -> finite surgery-history object
  -> connected-sum classification
  -> fundamental-group discharge
  -> S^3 conclusion.
```

## In scope

1. Factor labels for spherical space forms and `S²`-bundles over `S¹`.
2. A finite event log for continuation, splitting, permitted component disposal, and extinction.
3. Validation of live-component identities, ancestry, acyclicity, and extinction.
4. Reconstruction of a connected-sum factor expression by induction over the history.
5. Interpretation of factor expressions as free-product expressions through an explicit imported topology interface.
6. Proof that a trivial free-product expression has no nontrivial finite or infinite-cyclic factors.
7. Identification of a spherical factor with trivial deck group as `S³`.
8. Negative fixtures for malformed histories, hidden deletions, live components at extinction, and hidden nontrivial factors.

## Out of scope

- Ricci curvature and Ricci flow definitions;
- short-time existence;
- Perelman entropy and reduced volume;
- `kappa`-non-collapsing;
- `kappa`-solution classification;
- canonical neighbourhoods;
- geometric surgery construction;
- finite-extinction analytic estimates;
- any claim of a complete formal proof.

## Proposed interface

Schematic only:

```lean
inductive PCFactor
| spherical (deck : FiniteGroupLabel)
| s2xs1
| twistedS2Bundle

inductive SurgeryEvent
| split (parent left right : ComponentId)
| discard (component : ComponentId) (factor : PCFactor)
| continue (before after : ComponentId)
| extinct
```

### C0 — abstract factors

Specify factor labels and their group interpretations.

### C1 — history well-formedness

Validate unique live identifiers, acyclic ancestry, ownership conservation, permitted terminal labels, and extinction only with no live components.

### C2 — factor reconstruction

Prove by finite induction that each component carries a connected-sum expression over permitted factors. The statement that actual Ricci surgeries induce these events remains the imported `PC-L007`/`PC-L008` interface.

### C3 — group interpretation

Interpret

```text
spherical(Gamma) -> Gamma
s2xs1 -> Z
twistedS2Bundle -> designated nontrivial group
connected_sum -> free_product.
```

A syntactic free-product surrogate is acceptable initially only when labeled as a surrogate rather than van Kampen formalization.

### C4 — simply connected discharge

Given the imported equality between the manifold fundamental group and the interpreted expression, prove

```text
pi_1(M)=1
  -> no sphere-bundle factors
  -> every deck group is trivial
  -> every spherical factor is S^3
  -> the connected-sum expression reduces to S^3.
```

## Risks

1. Connected sum may be absent from current formal manifold libraries.
2. Fundamental groups are defined up to isomorphism, complicating rewriting.
3. Concrete free-product group infrastructure may be expensive.
4. A syntactic surrogate certifies less and must be labeled precisely.
5. Connected-sum neutrality with `S³` may lack library support.
6. A fully certified history validator does not certify the geometric surgery-to-history correspondence.
7. Imported theorem interfaces must carry provenance and `UNFORMALIZED_IMPORT` status.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each theorem references a `PC-*` spine node and claim ID.
- Every imported assumption has visible source provenance.
- No assumption packages the final Poincaré conclusion.
- Positive fixtures cover direct extinction and nested splits.
- Negative fixtures fail closed on malformed histories and nontrivial factors.
- Public wording states exactly which layer is certified.
- “Formal proof of the Poincaré conjecture” is prohibited while the analytic chain remains imported.

## First task

Formalize the finite factor-expression discharge:

> For an expression generated by spherical factors labeled by finite deck groups and sphere-bundle factors labeled by nontrivial groups, triviality of the interpreted free-product expression forces every factor label to be trivial and excludes all sphere-bundle factors.

This advances `PC-L012` and claim `PC-WP00-C006`. It does not certify the geometric decomposition theorem.
