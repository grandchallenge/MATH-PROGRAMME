# PC-WP05 — solved-problem archival publication readiness

## Decision

```text
READY_FOR_QUALIFIED_SOLVED_PROBLEM_ARCHIVAL_PUBLICATION
```

The decision applies to the integrated reconstruction dossier, not to a new theorem or full formal proof certificate.

## Required public framing

The archive must be introduced as a governed reconstruction of a solved theorem. It may state that:

- the Poincaré conjecture is a solved classical theorem;
- Perelman completed Hamilton’s Ricci-flow programme through three primary preprints;
- Morgan–Tian supplies a complete detailed proof following those preprints;
- Kleiner–Lott supplies a detailed reconstruction of Perelman I and II;
- GCL has normalized the dependency structure, audited recurrent false-proof patterns, encoded the finite surgery-history layer, and kernel checked a bounded evaluator conditional on explicit imported event relations.

## Permitted description

> A source-normalized and adversarially audited reconstruction of the Hamilton–Perelman finite-extinction proof route, with executable topology-history fixtures and a Lean-checked finite backward evaluator. The analytic Ricci-flow and manifold-level topology theorems remain explicitly imported from the classical literature.

## Mandatory qualification box

Every public landing page must state:

1. this is not a new proof;
2. the Ricci-flow analysis is not formalized in the repository;
3. JSON history validity does not prove a geometric history exists;
4. the Lean certificate is conditional on `ImportedEventRelation`;
5. exact line-by-line source concordance remains incomplete;
6. no novelty or priority is claimed.

## Evidence suitable for publication

- canonical theorem and solved-status audit;
- explicit Top/PL/Diff bridge;
- route and dependency DAGs;
- fifteen false-proof fixtures;
- nineteen theorem interfaces with source and correction records;
- exact finite surgery-history schema;
- two positive and twelve negative executable histories;
- Lean kernel checking of finite evaluator properties;
- final source-concordance, category and non-circularity audits;
- claim-level trust matrix and retained proof-debt register.

## Retained debts compatible with publication

The archive may publish while retaining:

- line-by-line cross-source concordance;
- exact parameter equivalence across all reconstructions;
- independent analytic proof checking;
- manifold-level connected-sum and van Kampen formalization;
- verified JSON-to-Lean parsing;
- full Ricci-flow formalization.

These debts are compatible only because the public claim is explicitly bounded.

## Publication blockers

Publication must be withdrawn or corrected if any public artifact:

- calls the package a new proof or proof of an open problem;
- says Perelman’s analytic proof has been formalized or independently verified;
- treats Kleiner–Lott as a detailed proof of Perelman III;
- uses the two assertions excluded in the introduction to Perelman II as established inputs;
- hides the category bridge;
- infers topology from extinction without the surgery-event theorem;
- treats WP03 or WP04 as establishing event existence;
- removes the source-concordance or proof-debt qualification.

## Archival recommendation

Publish the dossier as a versioned research archive with immutable claim and source manifests. Keep further analytic formalization, alternative-proof comparison, and strengthened certification as separately admitted future campaigns rather than silently extending `PC-WP05`.
