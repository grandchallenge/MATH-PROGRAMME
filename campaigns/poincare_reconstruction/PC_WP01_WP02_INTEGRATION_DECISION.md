The WP02 crosswalk gives governing dispositions for the material correction classes identified at WP00:

1. ancient `kappa`-solution inaccuracies in Perelman I are governed by corrected Perelman II statements and checked against Morgan–Tian/Kleiner–Lott;

3. the sketched eventual globally smooth-flow assertion is not consumed;
4. finite extinction is sourced to Perelman III and Morgan–Tian, not Kleiner–Lott.

Quotation-level discrepancy extraction remains archival debt, but no known correction lacks a theorem-consumer disposition.
