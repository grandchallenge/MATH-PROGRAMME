# OZ-RT-BZ-T3-011-F

T3-011-E closes the entire frozen single-channel polynomial multiplier algebra. F therefore changes exactly one structural dimension: it admits the smallest genuinely mixed quadratic multiplier `x_c x_d G` on two distinct coordinate axes.

The same-coordinate pairs among `n1`, `n2`, and `n3` are excluded because they reduce to the E-closed `n^2 G` class. The admitted unordered pairs are exactly `(n1,k1)`, `(n1,l1)`, `(n2,k1)`, `(n2,l1)`, `(n3,k1)`, `(n3,l1)`, and `(k1,l1)`.

For each pair, F retains each endpoint's existing candidate bank and scalar namespace. It constructs the commuting iterated difference `Delta_c Delta_d(x_c x_d G)`, certifies the direct four-term identity against the two-channel discrete product rule under the common-denominator expanded `Q[n,k,l]` semantic normal form, and then evaluates the endpoint's frozen normalized cokernel witness.

The response being nonzero is not sufficient. The decisive event is the first exact nonzero normalized cokernel pairing. If two semantically equivalent mixed-response representations receive different witness pairings, F terminates at `MIXED_CHANNEL_SEMANTIC_FUNCTIONAL_AMBIGUITY`.

No square term, higher degree, arbitrary bivariate polynomial, multiple-lift combination, support/harmonic enlargement, rational prefactor, recurrence search, correction-layer work, proof, or promotion is authorized.
