# BSD-WP03 — Handoff gate

WP03 is complete only when the following are simultaneously true:

- the individual-certificate schema rejects numerical-only and universal claims;
- the database-experiment schema rejects unbounded or unfrozen populations;
- the formal-interface registry imports no open BSD proposition as an axiom;
- the claim-promotion policy has no path from finite computation to universal BSD;
- all positive and adversarial fixtures replay;
- Agent Council and independent Referee review are complete;
- Programme policy checks pass on the exact promoted head.

Even after promotion, WP04 remains closed until separately authorized.

A future WP04 candidate must state:

1. the exact restricted theorem target;
2. every WP02 theorem interface imported;
3. every WP01 false-proof fixture it must bypass;
4. every WP03 certificate, experiment, or formal interface used;
5. why the target is not a finite-data restatement or a disguised form of BSD.

WP03 does not select that target.
