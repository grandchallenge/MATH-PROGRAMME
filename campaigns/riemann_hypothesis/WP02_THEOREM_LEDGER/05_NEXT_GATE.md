# RH-WP02 next gate

WP01 and WP02 are internally complete but require repository review and CI before promotion.

After promotion, the campaign still may not begin unrestricted mechanism generation. The next gate requires:

1. a route-by-route prior-art and claimed-proof audit;
2. selection of one exact bridge node from the theorem ledger;
3. a statement of the missing theorem in locked notation;
4. a barrier profile and smallest falsifying test;
5. a support route: continuum proof, exact finite verification, interval certificate, or formalization;
6. an explicit explanation of why the target is narrower than RH but advances `RH-R030`.

The certified computational substrate may be opened only for a bounded certificate attached to a named theorem interface. Broad zero searches, unconstrained operator fitting, and numerical optimization of sampled equivalent criteria remain closed.
