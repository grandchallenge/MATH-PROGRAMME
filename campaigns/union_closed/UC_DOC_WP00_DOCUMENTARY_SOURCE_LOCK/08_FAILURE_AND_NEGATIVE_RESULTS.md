# Failure and negative-result analysis

| Route | Why plausible | Smallest obstruction | What it rules out | What it does not rule out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pair every set missing `x` with a set containing `x` | works for singleton member | union map collisions | unrestricted naive injection | more structured injections |
| Use total incidence count | Reimer gives a strong lower bound | support-size denominator | average-only proof | additional structural use of closure |
| Compress to a canonical family | successful in extremal set theory | closure/frequency may not be preserved | unproved compression monotonicity | audited class-specific compression |
| Enumerate larger universes | exact and falsifying | no unbounded reduction | computation-as-general-proof | bounded classification theorems |
| Basic entropy growth | broke the dimension-free barrier | Bernoulli/golden-ratio barrier | same-form route to one half | richer coupling or uniformity arguments |
| Minimal counterexample restrictions | many local theorems exist | restrictions need not conflict | “highly constrained implies impossible” | a complete contradiction theorem |
| Accept a claimed proof by title/abstract | rapid current awareness | unchecked reductions and quantifiers | status change without review | independent proof audit |

Recent complete-proof claims are retained as adversarial inputs for a future source audit, not as documentary theorem support.
