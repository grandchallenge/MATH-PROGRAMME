## 2. Lay executive companion

Frankl's conjecture asks whether every finite nontrivial family of sets closed under union contains an element appearing in at least half of the family. The statement is elementary; the universal counting bridge is not.

This source lock converts the mature Union-Closed campaign into a release-class documentary source without publishing a web edition prematurely. The monograph explains set-family closure, abundance, powerset sharpness, small exact ledgers, complement and lattice forms, Reimer's theorem, entropy bounds, formalized restricted results, hybrid certificates, current literature, false-proof risks, and the programme's exact claim boundary.

The source refresh includes primary material through 2026-07-27. Newly located complete-proof claims remain `NEEDS_AUDIT`; they do not alter the programme's open status.

## 3. Canonical scope and claim boundary

The documentary concerns the finite set-family statement

```math
for every finite nontrivial union-closed family F,
there exists x in support(F) with 2 * frequency_F(x) >= |F|.
```

Included viewpoints:

- finite set families and frequency profiles;
- complement/intersection-closed duality;

- Gilmer/Sawin/Yu/Cambie/Alweiss-Huang-Sellke/Liu entropy terrain;
- exact finite replay and Lean-checked restricted results;
- current proof-claim audit obligations.

Excluded promotions:

- necessary minimal-counterexample conditions to nonexistence;
