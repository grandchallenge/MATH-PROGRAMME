# HC-WP00 MATHCERT handoff

## Purpose

Certify the identity and logic of Hodge-related claims without pretending that current theorem-prover libraries contain a faithful end-to-end formalization of smooth projective complex varieties, Chow groups, Hodge structures, and cycle-class maps.

The detailed implementation plan and schema are maintained in:

- `grandchallenge/MATHCERT#23`
- `docs/hc_claim_schema_and_certification_boundary.md`
- `schemas/hc_claim_record.schema.json`

## In scope

1. Validate machine-readable Hodge claim records.
2. Reject coefficient drift `Q -> Z`.
3. Reject geometry drift `smooth projective -> compact Kahler`.
4. Reject rationality loss `Hdg^(2p)(X,Q) -> H^(p,p)(X)`.
5. Reject cycle-object drift to motivated, topological, or analytic substitutes without a bridge.
6. Reject effectivity and quantifier strengthening/weakening.
7. Check the finite statement lattice and implication labels.
8. Certify the abstract equivalence between cycle-map surjectivity and rational generation under a visible factorization interface.
9. Certify the dimension-at-most-three implication under provenance-bearing imports for Lefschetz `(1,1)` and hard Lefschetz.

## Out of scope

- a proof of the universal Hodge conjecture;
- hiding imported Hodge decomposition, Chow groups, or cycle classes as axioms;
- presenting a finite-dimensional surrogate as complex algebraic geometry;
- certifying numerical periods as algebraic cycles;
- formalizing a neighboring conjecture and reporting it as the classical theorem;
- a claim that mathlib lacks all relevant components; the recorded statement is only that the integrated required stack was not found in the bounded audit.

## Imported theorem interfaces

Every conditional abstract theorem must visibly name:

- Hodge decomposition;
- rational structure and conjugation compatibility;
- cycle-class map and factorization through rational equivalence;
- Lefschetz `(1,1)`;
- hard Lefschetz as an isomorphism of rational Hodge structures;
- algebraicity of the hyperplane class.

The theorem result must carry `CONDITIONAL_ABSTRACT_CERTIFICATION`, not `FORMALIZED_HODGE_THEOREM`.

## Negative fixtures

Required schema/test failures:

```text
coefficient_ring: Q -> Z
geometric_category: smooth_projective_variety -> compact_kahler_manifold
input_predicate: rational_Hodge -> complex_type_p_p
cycle_object: CH_p_tensor_Q -> motivated_correspondences
quantifier_scope: every_variety_every_class -> very_general_variety
conclusion: rational_combination -> effective_irreducible_cycle
implication: algebraic_to_Hodge -> Hodge_to_algebraic marked THEOREM
```

## Formalization stages

### C0 — schema validator

Load canonical and mutated claim records. Completion: valid classical record passes; every mutation fails with the intended diagnostic.

### C1 — statement relation graph

Represent relation labels and prove no path silently derives algebraicity from Hodge-locus, absolute, motivated, or Tate nodes without a conjectural bridge.

### C2 — generator equivalence

Build an abstract quotient/image model and certify

```text
surjective cycle map <-> target generated by images of irreducible cycle generators.
```

### C3 — boundary-case logic

Under imported interfaces, certify the logical reduction

```text
Lefschetz (1,1) + hard Lefschetz -> codimension n-1
boundary codimensions -> dimension <=3.
```

### C4 — provenance enforcement

A source with `THEOREM_BODY_PENDING` may support metadata or a bounded status statement, but not an exact historical hypothesis absent from the audited text.

## Acceptance criteria

- No hidden axioms or placeholders in public proof counts.
- Every assumption has a source ID and audit state.
- Negative fixtures are versioned and replayable.
- The public result status distinguishes semantic certification from continuum mathematical proof.
- The universal target remains `OPEN`.

## First task

Implement the schema validator and the six core semantic mutations. This discharges statement-hygiene debt without depending on unavailable Hodge/Chow foundations.
