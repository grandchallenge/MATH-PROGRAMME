The uniform position law follows from interval PNT and bounded Riemann approximation, not independence of shifted totients. The endpoint prime p is N+L+1 and both original phase starts are in the required block. The terminal difference sign follows from the exact rolling recurrence, with its dropped coefficient a multiple of Q. The empirical L2 inequality centers e and p, so its variance estimate is valid even for completely dependent values. Chord estimate uses a central arc; the ordered-pair variance identity includes the diagonal, and triangle inequality gives factor nine for max of two variances. The explicit cofinal lower bound is weak and applies to terminal-anchored sparse populations, not the WP09-M cofactor aggregate.
