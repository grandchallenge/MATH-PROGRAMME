## Exact-tree correction

The parent CM4 audit correctly observed that the pinned tree has no dedicated `Mathlib/Condensed/Derived` subtree, but that fact is not the operative blocker. The pin contains:

- general derived `Ext`;
- positive-Ext vanishing for injective second arguments and projective first arguments;
- an abelian structure on sheaf categories and additive sheafification;
- global sections as the right adjoint to the constant-sheaf functor, with comparison to evaluation at a terminal object and to limits;
- sheaf cohomology expressed as `Ext`;
- Čech complex infrastructure;
- discrete condensed modules modeled by locally constant maps and naturally identified with constant sheaves;
- ordinary condensed epimorphism characterizations;
- generic internal projectivity and a light-condensed specialization;
- clopen refinement for profinite spaces; and
- enough projectives in the category `Profinite`.

The formal probe `CMDGCondensedCM4P3Audit.lean` checks these interfaces directly at compile time and intentionally asserts no P3 theorem.

## Narrowed missing implication

The bounded audit did **not** locate a theorem that assembles the available ingredients into the required statement for arbitrary profinite `S`. In particular, it did not locate:

- higher `Sheaf.H'` vanishing for the required discrete integral coefficient object on arbitrary profinite `S`;
- injectivity or another acyclicity theorem for that discrete integral coefficient sheaf sufficient to invoke the available injective-Ext vanishing;
- projectivity of the free abelian sheaf representing evaluation at arbitrary profinite `S`;
- an ordinary-`CondensedMod` analogue sufficient to convert internal projectivity into the required CM4 vanishing;
- a Čech-to-derived comparison plus zero-dimensional acyclicity theorem sufficient for this target; or
- a complete underived reduction from protected P2-E to the actual `CondensedMod.IsSolid` solidification-precomposition isomorphism.

This is a bounded exact-tree result, not a claim of global mathematical nonexistence.

## Why the available ingredients do not close P3

`Ext.subsingleton_of_projective` eliminates positive Ext once the first argument is projective. `Profinite.projectivePresentation` gives enough projectives in the category of profinite spaces. These facts do not imply that the free abelian sheaf generated by the representable of an arbitrary profinite `S` is projective in the abelian sheaf category.

Dually, `Ext.subsingleton_of_injective` eliminates positive Ext once the coefficient object is injective. The pin does not establish that the discrete integral coefficient sheaf needed here is injective or otherwise acyclic, so this route also stops before the required profinite/discrete vanishing theorem.

The pinned global-sections API identifies global sections as a right adjoint and, where applicable, with evaluation at a terminal object or a limit of the underlying presheaf. Those general descriptions provide the correct derived-functor architecture but do not prove exactness of the relevant sections functor on arbitrary profinite objects with the required discrete coefficients.

Likewise, the Čech complex machinery and finite disjoint clopen refinements provide the expected ingredients for a zero-dimensional proof, but the audited pin does not expose a ready Čech-to-derived comparison plus acyclicity theorem that closes this specialization.

The light-condensed theorem `LightCondensed.internallyProjective_iff_tensor_condition` is recorded only as proof architecture. It is not authority for ordinary `CondensedMod`.

## Reopening condition

P3 can move only after one of two machine-replayable results is supplied at the exact pin (or a separately governed revised pin):

1. a profinite/discrete acyclicity or projectivity theorem in a form composable with the CM4 RHom calculation; or
2. a complete underived reduction transporting the protected P2 measure/dual equivalence through the actual `Condensed.profiniteSolidification` map and proving the defining Yoneda precomposition morphism in `CondensedMod.IsSolid` is an isomorphism.
