# OAI-26 family 376: Transport constructions and quantitative estimates

## The question in plain language

The family includes mathematical constructions that move prescribed boxes using carefully controlled flows, together with a forced-fluid computation construction. A later manuscript edition also makes quantitative claims about projecting forces on a torus and about how derivatives decay in time. A theorem about existence of a flow is not automatically a theorem with those stronger numerical bounds.

## A small example

For a periodic vector field $g$ on a flat torus, its divergence-free projection can be written as

$$
Pg=g-\nabla\phi,\qquad \Delta\phi=\nabla\cdot g,
$$

with a mean-zero convention for $\phi$. Take the pure gradient field $g(x)=\nabla\sin(x_1)$. Choosing $\phi(x)=\sin(x_1)$ gives $Pg=0$. This illustrates what the pressure-correcting projection removes. It does **not** establish a general high-order Sobolev bound or uniform time-decay control.
