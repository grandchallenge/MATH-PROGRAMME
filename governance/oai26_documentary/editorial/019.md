# OAI-26 Family 019: The local p-adic section conjecture and global consequences

**Editorial status: DRAFT_UNACCEPTED. Mathematical status: NO_CERTIFICATION.** This article describes the claims in the pinned OAI-26 source overview. It is not an independent mathematical proof review.

## The question in plain language

A curve can be studied by its points, or indirectly by recording how its finite algebraic covers fit together. The latter information is encoded by the arithmetic étale fundamental group. Grothendieck's section conjecture asks whether the group-theoretic information can recover rational points, particularly for curves of genus at least two.

Family 019's publisher claims a positive answer for curves over finite extensions of the p-adic numbers. It also claims a global result for certain modular curves over the rational numbers.

## A small example

Think of a finite covering of a curve as several overlapping copies, locally arranged above it. The étale fundamental group records the compatible symmetries of all finite covers. A rational point provides a way to identify a section of the associated arithmetic fundamental-group sequence, up to a suitable conjugacy relation.

The conjecture's difficult direction is the converse: showing that every relevant section comes from a rational point. Merely constructing a section from an existing point is not a proof that all sections arise this way.

The analogy is explanatory only; it does not establish the conjecture.

## What is known

The immutable OAI-26 publisher overview, Family 019, attributes the following assertions to its two source manuscripts.

**Local claim.** For every smooth, proper, geometrically connected curve of genus at least two over a finite extension of the p-adic field, rational points correspond bijectively to conjugacy classes of sections of the *full arithmetic étale fundamental group*. Both the genus restriction and the use of the full arithmetic group are part of the claimed statement.

**Global claim.** Grothendieck's section conjecture is claimed for the modular curves X_0(N) and X_1(N) defined over the rational numbers, provided their genus is at least two. This is not a publisher claim of the section conjecture for every curve over the rational numbers.

The source catalogue links two papers: *Étale covers with a prescribed exterior sheet*, dated September 24, 2026, and *The p-adic section conjecture*, dated October 6, 2026. Their respective roles within the proof chain cannot reliably be derived from the titles alone.
