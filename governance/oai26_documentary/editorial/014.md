## The question in plain language

The Langlands programme seeks relationships between arithmetic representations, automorphic forms, and geometric objects. A geometric Langlands correspondence describes certain categories of sheaves on spaces associated with a curve by means of dual-group data. A Ramanujan-type theorem places strong bounds on local components of automorphic representations. Arthur parameters add a further layer of structure to spectral data.

Family 014 combines these distinct topics. The indexed overview reports results in positive-characteristic geometric Langlands, local temperedness for specified global function-field representations, and Arthur enhancements of specified cuspidal excursion parameters. Each statement has its own domain and assumptions.

## A small example

A correspondence between two mathematical categories is not merely a matching of individual objects: it must also preserve their relevant morphisms and constructions. Conversely, a bound on local spectral parameters at one place does not automatically prove the analogous bound at every place.

Imagine a global representation with local information attached to each prime or place of a function field. A claim at all **unramified** places leaves open ramified places unless a separate theorem addresses them. Similarly, assuming a finite-level decomposition to construct an Arthur enhancement differs from proving the decomposition in that same theorem.

These distinctions explain why the eight papers cannot be summarized responsibly by the single phrase “Langlands is proved.”

## What is known

The pinned publisher overview claims the following bounded assertions:

- A *restricted* geometric Langlands equivalence for connected reductive groups on smooth projective connected curves over an algebraic closure of a finite field, under four stated Lie-theoretic characteristic hypotheses. Over arbitrary algebraically closed fields of positive characteristic, further assumptions require the characteristic to be very good and not to divide the order of the Weyl group.
- Ramanujan at **every place** for globally generic cuspidal representations of split adjoint absolutely simple exceptional groups over global function fields, with no characteristic or ramification-depth restriction in the indexed statement.
- Ramanujan at **every unramified place** for cuspidal representations of split adjoint absolutely simple groups that have a generic unramified component.
- Assuming the finite-level Ramanujan–Arthur decomposition, global Arthur enhancements of occurring cuspidal excursion parameters for split connected semisimple groups at full finite level. The indexed claim says diagonal specialization recovers the parameters across the entire Weil group, including inertia.

The last assertion explicitly depends on a finite-level decomposition hypothesis; it is not presented here as an unconditional proof of every enhancement. The catalogue's remaining tame eigensheaf, marked-point, Frobenius-structure, and rationality papers may have distinct technical roles that require exact theorem-level assessment.
