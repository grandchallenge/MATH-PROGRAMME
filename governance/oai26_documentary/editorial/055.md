## The question in plain language

Bridgeland stability conditions organize objects in a derived category into stable and unstable classes. Family 055 addresses a particular Gepner symmetry on quintic threefolds and prescribed large-volume central charges on threefolds with trivial canonical bundle.

## A small example

A smooth quintic threefold is a degree-five hypersurface in complex projective four-space. The Gepner claim includes a specific phase shift of 2/5; an arbitrary stability condition is not enough. Likewise, a nearby large-volume central charge does not establish an assertion about exact charges.

## What is known

The pinned OAI-26 overview attributes to the first manuscript a numerical Bridgeland stability condition with phase shift 2/5 for every smooth complex quintic threefold. It attributes to the second numerical stability conditions for all smooth projective complex threefolds with trivial canonical bundle, at every sufficiently large volume, using exact ordinary and square-root-Todd central charges. These are distinct assertions with different hypotheses; neither has been independently checked here.
