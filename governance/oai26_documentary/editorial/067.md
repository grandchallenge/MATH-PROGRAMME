## The question in plain language

A Fano manifold is a smooth projective variety with positive anticanonical bundle. Its tangent bundle records the directions in which one can move. A tangent bundle is *nef* when it satisfies a numerical nonnegativity condition. Campana and Peternell conjectured that Fano manifolds with nef tangent bundle are highly symmetric: they should be rational homogeneous spaces.

Family 067 concerns the six-dimensional case of this classification problem.

## A small example

Projective space is homogeneous: its points can be exchanged by algebraic symmetries. Grassmannians, whose points represent linear subspaces of fixed dimension, are other examples of homogeneous spaces. The conjecture says that the combination of Fano positivity and nef tangent directions is rigid enough to force this symmetry; mere numerical nonnegativity does not make the symmetry obvious.

## What is known

The pinned September 25, 2026 manuscript *The Campana–Peternell conjecture in dimension six* claims that **every smooth connected complex projective Fano sixfold with nef tangent bundle is rational homogeneous**. Its 27-page PDF abstract also gives a consequence for connected compact Kähler manifolds with nef holomorphic tangent bundle: when the complex dimension minus maximal irregularity of finite étale covers is at most six, the ordinary universal cover splits as a rational homogeneous manifold times a complex affine factor of that maximal-irregularity dimension.

The table of contents identifies arguments using minimal rational curves, pseudoindex four and five, characteristic-class constraints, Grassmannian recognition, and an appendix labeled an exact arithmetic certificate. The existence of that appendix is not evidence that its computations have been independently validated here.

The assertion is restricted to dimension six for the Fano classification; it does not establish the conjecture in every dimension.
