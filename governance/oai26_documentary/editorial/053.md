## The question in plain language

Moduli spaces of stable pointed curves collect algebraic curves with marked points, including degenerations that make the space compact. Inside their intersection theory, *tautological relations* express when combinations of geometric cycles are equivalent to zero.

Pixton developed a remarkable collection of such relations. The original *completeness* conjecture asked whether the relations he proposed account for all tautological relations of the relevant kind. Family 053's publisher claims a counterexample.

## A small example

A set of formulas might generate many known relations without generating every true relation. To disprove completeness, it is not enough to find a new way to write an old relation: the new relation must genuinely lie outside the span generated by the proposed list.

There are also different settings for interpreting relations. The rational Chow ring tracks algebraic cycles up to rational equivalence. Rational cohomology is a related but potentially coarser invariant. A counterexample to a completeness assertion in Chow does not automatically settle every version of a cohomological claim without further argument.

## What is known

The pinned OAI-26 overview attributes to *A high-arity counterexample to Pixton completeness in Chow* (September 24, 2026) the construction of a tautological relation on a moduli space of stable pointed curves that **vanishes in rational Chow**, and hence in rational cohomology, yet **lies outside Pixton's original relation span**.

The overview presents this as disproving both the original Chow and rational-cohomological completeness formulations. The adjective *original* matters: this is not a source claim that all conceivable strengthened or revised generating systems also fail.

The overview does not specify the moduli-space genus, number of markings, codimension, explicit relation, or the linear-independence argument establishing nonmembership in the original span. Those require the actual manuscript.
