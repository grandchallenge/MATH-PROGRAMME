## The question in plain language

A function field consists of rational functions on an algebraic variety, rather than ordinary numerical fractions alone. A reconstruction theorem asks whether a carefully chosen collection of invariants determines the original field, up to a specified notion of equivalence.

Family 009 claims that surprisingly compressed algebraic information can recover function fields of transcendence degree at least two over algebraically closed constants. The information includes low-degree Milnor K-groups modulo a prime and their multiplication, as well as related Galois data.

The exact meaning of *recover* is essential: depending on the characteristic, the source synopsis distinguishes recovery of a perfect closure from recovery of the original field.

## A small example

Suppose a field contains variables $x$ and $y$ and all rational expressions in them. The field remembers the ways rational functions add, multiply and divide, including which elements are constants.

Milnor K-theory records multiplicative information using symbols. In degree one it recovers the multiplicative group of nonzero field elements, while degree two records symbols such as

$$
\{a,b\},
$$

subject to relations including the Steinberg relation $\{a,1-a\}=0$ whenever both entries are nonzero. Knowing only two separate groups is less informative than knowing their product pairing: the pairing tells us which degree-one elements interact to produce degree-two symbols.

The motivating question is whether those compressed operations still encode the field itself. This analogy illustrates the reconstruction problem; it does not prove the claimed result.

The publisher's synopsis claims recovery from degree-one and degree-two Milnor K-groups modulo a prime $\ell$, together with the multiplication between them. It states that when $\ell$ differs from the characteristic, these data determine the perfect closure and constants; in equal characteristic, the accompanying work recovers the original field and its named base.

A related claimed result concerns reconstruction from abelian-by-central pro-ell Galois data for primes away from the characteristic. Such a Galois quotient retains more than a bare abelian group but less than the full absolute Galois group.
