# OAI-26 family 068: anticanonical nonvanishing under smooth semipositivity

## The question in plain language

A complex projective variety has a canonical bundle describing its highest-dimensional holomorphic differential forms. Its dual, the *anticanonical bundle*, plays a central role in classification. A basic question is whether a positive tensor power of this bundle possesses a nonzero global section.

Family 068 reports that a particularly strong metric positivity assumption answers yes in every dimension: if the anticanonical bundle admits a **smooth Hermitian metric with nonnegative curvature**, some positive tensor power has a nonzero section. This is called *anticanonical nonvanishing*.

## A small example

For a smooth complex projective variety $X$, the canonical divisor is $K_X$. Its anticanonical divisor is $-K_X$. The nonvanishing conclusion can be written

$$
H^0(X,-mK_X)\neq 0\quad\text{for some integer }m>0.
$$

Here the left-hand side is the space of global holomorphic sections of the indicated bundle. Nonzero means at least one section exists; it does **not** assert that this bundle is base-point-free or that every positive tensor power has a section.

Smooth semipositivity is also more specific than numerical nefness: the premise requires a **smooth** Hermitian metric with semipositive curvature, not just a numerical inequality on curves or an arbitrary singular metric.

## What is known

The protected Family 068 catalogue identifies seven manuscripts, all dated September 26, 2026, addressing metric positivity, anticanonical indices, invariant sections, twisted differentials, descent, controlled boundaries, and related technical steps.

The pinned overview asserts: for **every smooth connected complex projective variety** $X$ whose anticanonical bundle admits a **smooth Hermitian metric of nonnegative curvature**, there is **some positive integer $m$** with a nonzero global section of $-mK_X$.

The indexed theorem is not limited to any fixed dimension. But the claim does not automatically imply a uniform bound on $m$, semiampleness, effectivity of $-K_X$ itself, or the same result under merely singular or integrable metric hypotheses. Those would require separate source-checked theorems.

The seven supporting manuscript titles identify substantial auxiliary geometry, including klt pairs and torus quotients. Those auxiliary topics must not be treated as unrestricted extensions of the headline theorem without examination of the exact PDFs.
