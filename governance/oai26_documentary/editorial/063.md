## The question in plain language

A *Fano manifold* is a smooth complex projective variety whose anticanonical divisor is ample. The *Picard number* counts independent numerical divisor directions, while the *pseudoindex* is the smallest positive intersection of the anticanonical divisor with a rational curve. The generalized Mukai conjecture asks how large these two measures can be together relative to the dimension, and describes the equality case.

## A small example

On a product of r projective spaces of the same positive dimension d, the total dimension is rd, the Picard number is r, and the pseudoindex is d+1. Therefore the quantity rho times (iota minus one) equals rd, exactly the dimension. The conjecture claims that **only** such products can attain equality.

## What is known

The pinned September 24, 2026 paper *The generalized Mukai conjecture* states in **Theorem 1.1** that a smooth connected complex projective Fano manifold of **positive dimension** n, Picard number rho and pseudoindex iota satisfies

$$
\rho(\iota-1)\le n.
$$

It further claims equality **if and only if** the manifold is isomorphic to the product of rho copies of projective space of dimension iota minus one. The pseudoindex is the minimum anticanonical degree among irreducible rational curves, **not** the Fano index (the divisibility of the anticanonical class).

The introduction describes a proposed route using small quantum multiplication by divisor classes, independent curve degrees, point-descendant estimates, and an equality analysis. These are descriptions of the manuscript's proof plan, not separately validated mathematics.
