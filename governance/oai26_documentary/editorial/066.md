# OAI-26 family 066: bounded klt complements for Fano contractions

## The question in plain language

A Fano-type variety has strong positivity properties associated with its anticanonical divisor. In the minimal model program, one often studies a *pair*: a variety together with a boundary that records permitted singular behavior. A *complement* replaces or enlarges that boundary so the resulting log-canonical divisor becomes torsion in a controlled way.

Shokurov's bounded-complement philosophy asks whether the multiple needed to obtain such a relation can be bounded uniformly using a few coarse parameters, rather than depending on every geometric detail of the variety. Family 066 reports bounded **klt** complements, with crucial qualifications on coefficients, singularities and the underlying contraction.

## A small example

Let $(X,B)$ be a pair with canonical divisor $K_X$ and boundary $B$. A complement introduces a boundary $B^+$ such that a positive integer multiple of the adjusted adjoint divisor is linearly trivial, with suitable conditions on singularities.

The distinction between *log canonical* and *Kawamata log terminal* (klt) is essential: klt demands stricter control of discrepancies. An **index bound** means that one integer from a uniformly bounded range suffices, which is stronger than an assertion that some unbounded integer exists for each example.

A *Fano contraction* is relative to a map onto a base. A complement asserted **near every base point** is local on that base; it should not automatically be described as a single global complement over the entire base.

## What is known

The protected Family 066 catalogue identifies two manuscripts, both dated September 25, 2026: *Bounded klt complements for Fano contractions* and *Uniform Cartier sections for Fano type contractions*.

The pinned publisher overview claims a finite-rational-coefficient form of Shokurov's bounded-klt-complement conjecture for **epsilon-log-canonical complex Fano-type pairs** with **nef anti-log-canonical divisor**.

Separately, for **epsilon-log-canonical Fano contractions over any algebraically closed characteristic-zero field**, the overview claims klt complements **near every base point**, with index bounded only by the dimension and a fixed positive rational epsilon.

The finite coefficient restriction, nefness, field, characteristic, epsilon-log-canonicity and relative locality are material. The overview does not justify extending the result to arbitrary real coefficients or uniform global complements on every contraction.
