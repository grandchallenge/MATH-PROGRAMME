## The question in plain language

The geometric Langlands programme relates mathematical descriptions of bundles on a complex curve to descriptions associated with a dual algebraic group. Its *quantum* deformation asks whether suitable categories of twisted differential equations for a group and its Langlands dual agree after an inverse transformation of the twisting parameter. A category here records both objects and structure-preserving maps between them. An equivalence of categories is stronger than a mere correspondence between selected examples: it must preserve the full mathematical structure.

## A small example

For a simply laced group, the lacing number is one, so the shifted parameter in the manuscript is sent from $c$ to $-1/c$. For a nonsimply laced root system the lacing number $r$ is two or three, and the partner parameter is $-1/(rc)$. The hypothesis $c\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb Q$ excludes rational values but includes non-real complex values. These expressions describe the manuscript's parameter convention, not a numerical algorithm for constructing the correspondence.

## What is known

The October 4, 2026 manuscript *Global quantum geometric Langlands at irrational level* states an unramified de Rham quantum geometric Langlands equivalence for **every connected simple complex algebraic group** $G$, **every smooth projective connected complex curve** $X$, and **every shifted level** $c\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb Q$. Its main theorem asserts an equivalence of presentable complex-linear derived DG categories

$$
D_c(\operatorname{Bun}_G(X)) \simeq D_{-1/(rc)}(\operatorname{Bun}_{G^\vee}(X)).
$$

where $G^\vee$ is formed using the dual **full root datum** and $r$ is the lacing number. The source specifies determinant-line twisting, uses the given global forms rather than quietly passing to adjoint or simply connected forms, and includes **all connected components** of the bundle stacks. A theta characteristic and dual pinnings enter the normalization of the construction.

This is a claimed equivalence of *full twisted D-module categories*, not a theorem merely about their finite-dimensional objects, a comparison of Grothendieck groups, or a Betti equivalence. The claimed range is irrational shifted levels. It does not include a statement covering all rational or critical levels.

### The argument described by the manuscript

The manuscript's introduction presents two principal comparison mechanisms. First, an irrational-level Whittaker–Poincaré or Plancherel identity $J_sR_s\to\mathrm{Id}$ is claimed on the entire twisted D-module category. Its proof invokes a finite Whittaker test, a filtered deformation argument and uniform weight bounds to transfer a vanishing statement from the ordinary-level setting. Second, local Kac–Moody and Whittaker pairings are compared over *families of marked points*, including collision diagonals. The source proposes extension through collision strata, reconstruction of maps of hearts, and derived realization.

The final section uses arbitrary-level localization, a global unipotent-period/Ran comparison and two Whittaker identities to construct inverse functors. The central-torsor and component compatibility is part of the claimed construction. These are source-reported proof steps, **not independently replayed GCL lemmas**.
