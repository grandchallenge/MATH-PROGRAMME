## The question in plain language

Two apparently different moduli spaces can describe related geometric information about a complex algebraic curve. One space parametrizes Higgs bundles; another parametrizes representations of its fundamental group. Nonabelian Hodge theory relates them, but the two spaces carry different ways of organizing cohomology.

The P = W conjecture predicts that a filtration built from a geometric map on the Higgs side, the *perverse filtration*, matches the *weight filtration* from mixed Hodge theory on the character-variety side.

## A small example

Think of the same vector space equipped with two increasing sequences of nested subspaces. Even if the underlying vector spaces are isomorphic, these two sequences need not coincide. A P = W result says that, under a particular identification, corresponding stages agree with a precise shift in indices.

The conjecture compares sophisticated filtrations on *all* rational cohomology groups, not merely dimensions or Betti numbers. In Family 043 the publisher emphasizes that *variant cohomology* is included, rather than silently excluded.

## What is known

The pinned OAI-26 overview attributes to the September 24, 2026 manuscript *P=W in composite rank for fixed determinant* a result for smooth coprime fixed-determinant, trace-free Higgs moduli spaces and their character varieties over smooth projective complex curves of genus at least two.

For composite ranks the overview asserts the full rational-cohomology filtration equality: the kth perverse stage corresponds to both the weight levels indexed 2k and 2k+1. It explicitly includes variant cohomology. The overview says that, together with previously known prime-rank theorems, this establishes the fixed-determinant P = W conjecture in every **coprime** rank.

The coprimality, fixed-determinant, trace-free and genus assumptions are substantive. The statement should not be generalized to noncoprime singular moduli spaces or arbitrary character varieties without additional arguments.
