# OAI-26 family 059: Counterexamples to Zariski's multiplicity conjecture

**Editorial status: DRAFT_UNACCEPTED pending protected admission. Mathematical status: NO_CERTIFICATION.** This document checks the statements in pinned PDFs; it does not certify their proofs.

## The question in plain language

The *multiplicity* of a hypersurface at a singular point is the degree of the lowest nonzero term of a local defining equation, when that equation is reduced. Zariski asked whether the shape of a hypersurface near a point, viewed up to a homeomorphism of the surrounding space, determines its multiplicity.

The two Family 059 manuscripts claim counterexamples: germs with different multiplicities but indistinguishable embedded topology.

## A small example

Near the origin, an equation beginning in degree two has multiplicity two; an equation whose first nonzero terms have degree three has multiplicity three. Distinct multiplicities are easy to identify algebraically, but it is much harder to know whether their zero sets have the same topology *inside* the ambient complex space. Abstract homeomorphism of the zero sets alone would be weaker than the ambient homeomorphism required here.

## What is known

The pinned September 24, 2026 paper *Ambiently homeomorphic isolated hypersurfaces of multiplicities two and three* states that it constructs **reduced hypersurface germs with isolated singularities** of multiplicities **two and three** in a common complex affine space of dimension divisible by eight. Its abstract also claims that the defining function germs are topologically right equivalent and that the examples bear on Arnold's corank question. The paper does not assert these examples lie in complex dimension four.

The pinned September 27, 2026 paper *Ambiently homeomorphic isolated hypersurface germs in C4 with multiplicities four and five* makes a separate **four-variable** claim in Theorem 1.1: reduced convergent holomorphic germs with isolated critical points, multiplicities **four and five**, and a homeomorphism of the corresponding ambient pairs. Its introduction describes using Milnor open books, integral Seifert forms and a classification result to compare their embedded links.

Taken as source claims, either construction would negate the embedded form of Zariski's multiplicity conjecture. The two pairs have different ambient-dimension constraints and should not be combined into a purported two-versus-three example in complex four-space.
