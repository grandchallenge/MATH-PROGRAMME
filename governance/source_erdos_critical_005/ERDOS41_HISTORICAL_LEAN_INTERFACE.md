# Compiled historical (B_3) source interface

Lean 4.33.0 compiled `Erdos41HistoricalSourcePredicates.lean` successfully on CHAD. It provides three *distinct* propositions over arbitrary predicates `A : Nat -> Prop` (so not restricted to finite ambient carrier): `historicalB3` (all nonempty sums of at most 3 **distinct** elements, up to permutation), `modernMultisetB3` (all sums of exactly 3 elements, including repetitions), and the weak `fixedDistinctTripleB3` (exactly 3 distinct elements). Theorems `historical_implies_fixed` and `modern_implies_fixed` are both fully compiled, no `sorry`. The first uses Lean axioms `propext` and `Quot.sound`; the second has no axioms.

The historical predicate is grounded in the scanned Erdős 1980 survey pp.98–99, using Sidon's original 0/1 coefficients and cardinality ≤3. The modern predicate matches the semantic meaning of repeated size-3 multisets. Both implications to the weak predicate are straightforward and *proved*, but the converses are false. Infinite counterexamples in protected PR #2692 show the historical and strong modern conditions are mutually incomparable, not alternate names for one formal theorem.

The interface is **not yet** a theorem inside the external `FormalConjectures.ErdosProblems.41` / Mathlib environment, and it does not establish definitional or propositional equivalence with the upstream `NtupleCondition` term.
