# GCL-PAAR-001 — Assertion provenance, auditability and reproducibility

## Objective

Reconcile and audit GCL's EXISTING, standing assertion-integrity invariants. Do not invent another governance framework, start specialists automatically, require separate GitHub identities, or force human per-item approvals. Specialists volunteer; GCL truthfully records availability, custody, returns, reviews, certifications and admissions.

The six-repository AUDIT_BASELINE.json is a BOUNDED SAMPLE, not a certification of the entire GCL estate.

## Invariants to verify against existing authority

1. **Provenance.** Every material scientific, operational, governance, administrative or public assertion identifies original source, producer/agent execution, authorized transport identity, UTC time, immutable source revision and relevant evidence artifacts. Reported theorems, finite computations, formal statements, independently proved claims and certified results have distinct classifications.
2. **Auditability.** State transitions bind predecessor, authenticated evidence, exact protected authority, role-appropriate independent review and successor. Mutable Issues, Projects, consoles and published pages do not silently supersede protected source. Dossiers and assignments are not approvals.
3. **Reproducibility.** Computational evidence provides code/revision, deterministic inputs and seed, environment/toolchain, command, raw output/expected digest, finite-domain boundaries and independent verifier as appropriate. Mathematical evidence exposes explicit assumptions, argument or checked proof, theorem interface and unresolved obligations.
4. **Independence.** Role/independently produced work provenance matters; transport login alone neither proves independence nor requires one login per role. No candidate self-certification or fabricated reviewer return.
5. **Fail-closed integrity.** Stale, duplicate, contradictory, fabricated, untrusted, tampered or unavailable evidence cannot promote a claim or state. Maintain historical lineage and permit unrelated valid work.
6. **Accurate lifecycle.** AVAILABLE, CLAIMED, RETURNED, REVIEWED, ADMITTED, formally CERTIFIED and publicly PUBLISHED are not synonyms. An unaccepted published draft is not editorial V2 admission. Lack of worker participation is not a system malfunction.

## Audit work packages

PAAR-A: Inventory every active GCL assertion producer, consumer, authority, policy contract, status mirror, proof/published output, and exact immutable source. Include CONTROL, MATH-PROGRAMME, MATHSOLVE, MATHCERT, INTELLECT, MATHFORGE and all other active GCL systems. Separate ENFORCED, DOCUMENTED_ONLY, PARTIAL, UNKNOWN, and NONCONFORMANT.
PAAR-B: Independently sample end-to-end lifecycles (unclaimed work, successful return, revise/rejection, protected admission, mathematical certification boundary, public status). Reconcile live GitHub Project/Issue state to protected ledgers and deployed representations.
PAAR-C: Inject *in-memory or sandbox-only* adversarial negatives against EXISTING validators: stale exact heads, signature/source hash drift, duplicate returns, forged independent review, changed theorem scope, unverifiable computation, expired receipt and contradictory mirror. No production corruption.
PAAR-D: Maintain difference register with exact reproduction, expected authority, real observed output, impact, owner and minimal correction. Govern any substantive fixes as separate protected PRs with regression tests. Avoid new machinery unless a reproduced enforcement gap cannot be closed with existing controls.
PAAR-E: GCL accountable assurance agents independently assess the exact-source assurance matrix, record bounded exceptions, and specify replay cadence through EXISTING scheduled controllers. Separate publication certification applies only when a claim is submitted for certification.

## Required specialist outputs

- Cross-repository authority/control matrix pinned to exact protected commits and Git blob SHAs.
- Evidence chain graph, boundaries and consumers for all material assertion classes.
- Reproducible positive and negative tests with raw logs, code, toolchain, input, expected output hashes and limits.
- Distinct PASS / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN / FAIL for each control and observed claim; never convert inaccessible evidence into a pass.
- Scoped minimal remediation work items and protected PRs only when justified by reproduced nonconformance.
- Final accountable GCL internal audit dossier and exact-head protected assurance receipt, with external certification review only when a publication claim requires it.

## Acceptance and authority boundary

The workstream may be marked CLOSED only after the enumerated active estate is covered or a formally approved scope/exclusion is explicit; all material control mappings are evidenced at protected revisions; negative tests exercise genuine validators; discrepancies are corrected or retained as identified blocking exceptions; authority/mirror states reconcile; GCL accountable independent audit roles review the factual evidence; and the final receipt is protected and reproducible. A second external independent integrity reviewer is not a general prerequisite; publishing certification has its own review rules.

This is NOT a claim that GCL governance needs rewriting. It creates no certification authority, no source or worker status transition, no new credentials, and no V2 admission. Unfinished volunteer work remains truthfully AVAILABLE.
