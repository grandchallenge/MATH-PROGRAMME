## #517 OPEN — Fejér/Pólya exceptional-value problem

Original P. Erdős, *Some unsolved problems* (1961), printed p.250 IV.8, https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1961-22.pdf#page=30, original web PDF screenshot visually inspected. For f(z)=Σ_(k≥1)a_k z^(n_k), a_k≠0, strictly increasing positive n_k with n_k/k→∞, ask EVERY complex value attained infinitely often. No finite-order assumption. Fejér 1908 (Math Ann 65, 413–423) proves Σ1/n_k<∞ ensures every value at least once; Biernacki 1928 (Paris thesis) gives infinitely often under SAME Fejér summability; Pólya 1929 (Math Z 29, 549–640) treats finite-order entire gap functions under unbounded gaps. Original complete proofs Fejér/Biernacki/Pólya not independently inspected; historical claims grounded in original Erdős 1961 and maintained problem source. Maintained https://www.erdosproblems.com/517 edited Dec29 2025 stays OPEN; March2026 Murai1983 comment lacks a located precise resolving theorem.

Great Picard at infinity gives ALL values infinitely often except at most ONE exceptional value for a transcendental entire f. The genuinely open residual is to rule out any such exception for arbitrary infinite-order Fabry-gap entire functions, not to test finitely many values numerically. An exceptional value attained only finitely often admits representation f−w=P e^g by dividing finitely many zeros then taking entire logarithm of nowhere-zero function. This is a reduction, not a proof of incompatibility with gaps.

Exact illustrative sequence n_k=k bit_length(k), k≥1, with bit_length(k)=floor(log2 k)+1: strictly increasing, n_k/k→∞. On each dyadic block 2^j≤k<2^(j+1), reciprocal sum ≥1/(2(j+1)), so Σ1/n_k DIVERGES even though Fabry holds. Thus Biernacki's Fejér result does NOT imply the conjecture. f(z)=Σ z^(n_k)/(n_k)! is entire of finite order≤1 by domination by exp(|z|), hence covered by the Pólya partial case; NOT a counterexample. e^(z^m) misses zero but exponent ratio n_k/k=m finite, so also NOT a counterexample. tests/test_erdos_compendium_0517_fabry_values.py verifies exact bit lengths up to k1024, ten dyadic blocks, factorial-subseries domination with Fraction, and omission-example gap failure. Four focused tests PASS. Analytic inference of full infinite-block divergence is written out, finite tests not equated to infinite proof.
