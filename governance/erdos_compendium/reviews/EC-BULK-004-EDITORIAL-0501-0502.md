## #501: special logic status and 2026 primary formal source

Erdős *Some unsolved problems* (1961), printed p.243, II.9, primary https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1961-22.pdf#page=23 visually inspected. Two distinct questions: infinite independent set for arbitrary BOUNDED sets A_x of Lebesgue OUTER measure <1, and an independent TRIPLE if each A_x is closed of measure <1. Independence requires both x notin A_y and y notin A_x for every distinct x,y; no self-loop condition. Erdős–Hajnal proved arbitrarily large finite families but not infinite one.

## #502: theorem resolution but exact extremum unresolved

Original Erdős 1961 printed p.244 Coxeter paragraph, https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1961-22.pdf#page=24 visually inspected. Original asks how many d-dimensional points FORCE >2 distinct distances; source explicitly retracts Erdős's false claimed earlier polynomial proof. The 1983 Bannai–Bannai–Stanton *Combinatorica* 3(2), 147–152 DOI 10.1007/BF02579288 proves |A|<=C(d+s,s) for s-distance sets; full 1983 paper body uninspected. FULL, primary alternative proof inspected: Fedor Petrov and Cosmin Pohoata, *A remark on sets with few distances in R^d*, Proc. AMS 149 (2021) 569–571, arXiv:1912.08181v1 https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.08181 . All THREE page images inspected: Theorem 1.1 p1, polynomial inertia lemma Theorem 1.2 p2, derivation p3. For s distances polynomial p(x,y)=product_j(δ_j²-||x-y||²) yields positive scalar diagonal evaluation matrix on A, inertia |A|. Orthogonal complement of degree<=s polynomial values has codimension at most C(d+s,s). Total-degree<=2s+1 bivariate polynomial bilinear form vanishes on that complement; positive definite subspace intersects trivially, so positive inertia <=C(d+s,s). Thus |A|<=C(d+s,s). This explains proof fully, but is not a Lean kernel replay.

For d>=3, all weight-two 0/1 vectors in R^{d+1} have C(d+1,2) members, lie in affine hyperplane sum=2 of dimension d, and realize exactly squared distances 2 and4. Lower bound C(d+1,2); upper bound C(d+2,2). Exact maximum M_2(d) is NOT known for general d, although original polynomial threshold question settled. In small d=1,2 the weight-two example lacks both distances. Current new upstream Formal Conjectures 502.lean has by sorry plus external reported Lean source, pinned FC-GDM-85F86371 lacks 502.lean.
