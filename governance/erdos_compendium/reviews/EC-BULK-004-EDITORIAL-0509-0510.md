## #509: monic lemniscate disk covers

Elementary independently verifiable lemmas: f=z-a gives one unit disk; for f=(z-a)(z-b), |z-a||z-b|<=1 implies one factor<=1, hence two unit-radius disks suffice, total2; repeated roots one disk. ## #510: dated 2026 exponent drift and two opposite extremal bounds

Original P. Erdős (1961), printed pp.247–248, IV.2, https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1961-22.pdf#page=27 and #page=28, **both PDF pages visually inspected**. Ankeny/Chowla first ask large negative excursion; Chowla's square-root challenge appears across pages. For any N-element A of DISTINCT POSITIVE integers, f_A(theta)=sum cos(a theta), K(N)=inf_A[-min f_A]; conjecture K(N)>=c√N (N sufficiently large). Source variant A⊂Z includes negative and zero terms with changed multiplicity/constant; do NOT blindly equate. K(N) is a lower bound on guaranteed excursion; construction giving small excursion is an UPPER bound on K(N). Positive f_A(0)=N and zero mean yield negative point for each A individually, NOT K(N)>0 uniformly just from continuity.

Dated PRIMARY sources: Ruzsa (2004) K>=exp(c sqrt log N), full proof not reread. B. Bedert arXiv:2509.05260v2 (September 2025), original PDF pp1–2 visually inspected: K>=N^(1/7-o(1)); his updated July 24 2026 v3 arXiv primary HTML https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.05260: K>=N^(1/5-o(1)). Exact classical Sidon-difference identity: B={2^j-1:0<=j<k}, each positive difference unique since 2^j-2^i has 2-adic valuation i and odd factor 2^(j-i)-1. A={positive differences}, |A|=k(k-1)/2. P(z)=sum_{b∈B}z^b. Laurent identity P(z)P(z^-1)=k+sum_{a∈A}(z^a+z^-a). At z=e^{iθ}, yields F_A(theta)=(|P|²-k)/2>=-k/2 and K(C(k,2))<=k/2, extends via padding by <k additional frequencies to K(N)=O(√N).
