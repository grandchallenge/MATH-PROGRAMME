## #545 — OPEN eventual maximum Ramsey number at fixed edge count

Original P. Erdős/R. L. Graham, *On partition theorems for finite graphs*, Infinite and Finite Sets, Keszthely 1973, Colloq Math Soc János Bolyai 10 (1975), pp515–527, esp p526; original complete scan NOT retrieved, same for Erdős *On some problems in graph theory, combinatorial analysis and combinatorial number theory*, Graph Theory and Combinatorics (1984) pp1–17 p11. For m=binom(n,2)+t, 0≤t<n, compare every isolate-free m-edge graph G with H(n,t)=K_n plus one vertex adjacent to t clique vertices, extra isolated vertex omitted if t=0. The literal all-m comparison FALSE for small m (site confirms m2..5 and m7..9 exceptions), but the eventual all-large-m comparison remains OPEN. Original intended asymptotic qualifier needs exact 1975/1984 originals before attributing it directly to Erdős/Graham verbatim.

Original Benny Sudakov *A conjecture of Erdős on graph Ramsey numbers*, Adv Math 227 (2011), pp601–609 DOI 10.1016/j.aim.2011.02.004, COMPLETE author original https://people.math.ethz.ch/~sudakovb/erdos-ramsey-problem.pdf, printed p602 independently visually inspected. Explicitly states triangular m=binom(n,2) case K_n maximizes Ramsey number among all isolate-free graphs remains hard and unresolved, and Theorem1.1 proves only R(G)≤2^(250√m) for all m-edge graphs without isolates. That weaker exponential-scale result solves DIFFERENT problem #546, not sharp comparison to specific R(H(n,t)). Older Alon/Krivelevich/Sudakov 2^(O(√m logm)) general bound and bipartite improvement do not settle #545.

Elementary FULL bounded counterexample: for m=2, n=2,t=1 colex H=P3, R(P3)=3 by pigeonhole on three edges of K3. G=2K2 (matching size2) has R(2K2)=5: explicit K4 coloring red star centered at one vertex, blue triangle on leaves has no monochromatic disjoint pair; every K5 red/blue coloring contains monochromatic matching2 since each edge color class without disjoint edges is pairwise intersecting and has ≤4 edges (star/triangle), while K5 has ten edges. Thus exact 5>3 contradicts universal all-m, not eventual infinitely-large conjecture. Sudakov theorem is too coarse for comparison.
