## Exact statement and normalization
For integers k>=2 and l>=3, does some finite simple graph G of clique number at MOST l, i.e. with NO K_(l+1), have the EDGE-RAMSEY property that EVERY colouring of existing graph edges in k colours contains a monochromatic clique K_l? YES in classical literature. This is graph EDGE coloring, not vertex coloring. For target clique K_l, induced vs arbitrary monochromatic host copy distinction is immaterial; for noncomplete F the Nešetřil–Rödl 1976 stronger theorem preserves induced-copy structure. A trivial complete host K_R(k; l) fails forbidden K_(l+1), and K_l itself cannot force a monochromatic K_l under k>=2 and l>=3 (color its edges nonmonochromatically).
