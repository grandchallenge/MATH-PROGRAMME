## Exact statement and normalization
Fix integer k>=4 and graph order n>=k. f_k(n) is maximum integer m for which an EXACTLY k-chromatic finite simple graph G of n vertices has EVERY ODD SIMPLE CYCLE of length >m (equivalently maximum odd girth minus 1), with finite girth because any nonbipartite graph contains an odd cycle. Erdős–Gallai ask f_k(n)=Theta_k(n^(1/(k-2))). This is PROVED in classical literature by Kierstead–Szemerédi–Trotter 1984, matching upper and lower growth with constants depending on FIXED k; do not replace odd girth with unrestricted graph girth, chromatic >=k when exact k intended, or n^(1/k) for exact k bound without parameter shift.
