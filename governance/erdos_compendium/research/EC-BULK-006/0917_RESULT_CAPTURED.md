## Exact statement and normalization
For k>=4 define f_k(n) maximum edges in n-vertex finite simple graph G with chromatic number exactly k and EDGE-CRITICAL: deletion of ANY EDGE lowers chromatic number. Three parts: (A) f_k(n) >>_k n² positive quadratic density? (B) f_6(n)~n²/4? (C) for all fixed k>=6, f_k(n)~0.5*(1−1/floor(k/3))*n²? Source semantics important: edge-critical graph permits isolated vertices, whereas later Luo–Ma–Yang paper studies FULLY k-critical (every proper subgraph lowers chi); an edge-critical graph stripped of isolates yields fully critical core, while adding isolates preserves edge criticality. Do not equate exact maxima at fixed n without this reconciliation.
