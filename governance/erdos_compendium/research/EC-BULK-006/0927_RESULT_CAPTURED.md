## Exact statement and normalization
g(n) = MAXIMUM over ALL finite simple graphs G of n vertices of NUMBER OF DISTINCT SIZES of *INCLUSION-MAXIMAL CLIQUES* (not all complete subgraphs, not just one maximum clique). Maximal clique C is complete and NOT properly contained in any larger complete subset. Erdős [Er66b][Er71,p101][Er69b] conjectured g(n)=n−log_2(n)−log_*(n)+O(1) with log_*n # iterations of logarithms to below fixed threshold. Historical 1966 iterated-log lower construction suggested necessity. DISPROVED by Joel Spencer 1971: g(n)>=n−log_2(n)−O(1); Moon–Moser1965 proved g(n)<=n−floor(log_2(n)) for n>=4, hence ACTUAL g(n)=n−log_2(n)+O(1). The difference log_*n tends infinity and cannot be swallowed O(1). If clique incorrectly interpreted as ANY complete vertex set, possible sizes 1..omega(G), maximizing g(n)=n via K_n and conjecture nonsensical.
