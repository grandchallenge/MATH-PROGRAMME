## Exact statement and normalization
For N>=2, maximize M(N)=sum_{a in A}1/a over A subset {1,...,N} such that no DISTINCT a<b in A satisfy b=at with integer t>1 and least prime factor p_min(t)>a. Erdős asks for the asymptotics of M(N)/log N. This is weaker than ordinary primitivity: divisibility is forbidden only when all prime factors of the quotient exceed the smaller member. Erdős, 'Some Extremal Problems in Combinatorial Number Theory' (1970), printed p.128, Section 2, equations (24)-(27) and subsequent maximum question, https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1970-21.pdf . The original defines p(t) as the LEAST prime factor on printed p.123. The modern https://www.erdosproblems.com/858 matches the quantified finite-N optimization after taking t>1 and avoiding undefined spf(1).
