## Exact statement and normalization
For any fixed integer k>=0 and FINITE simple graph G, suppose EVERY subgraph H with m vertices has independence number alpha(H)>= (m−k)/2. Then chi(G)<=k+2? YES, proved Jon H. Folkman 1970. All subgraphs vs only induced subgraphs is equivalent for this hereditary alpha LOWER bound: given same vertex set, deleting edges can only increase alpha. Original condition equivalently max_{H induced subset G} (|V(H)|−2alpha(H))<=k. Precise auxiliary potential rho(H)=|V(H)|−2alpha(H)+2, including empty H rho=2. Folkman theorem chi(G)<=max_H rho(H). No clique/planarity restrictions. Not the separate Erdős #924 Folkman monochromatic-edge-clique theorem.
