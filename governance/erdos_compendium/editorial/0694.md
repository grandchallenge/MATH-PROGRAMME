## The question in plain language

For a totient value v in the image of Euler's φ, let f_min(v) and f_max(v) be the least and greatest positive m with φ(m)=v. Erdős asks for the largest ratio f_max(v)/f_min(v) among such values v≤x. The maintained catalogue marks the problem SOLVED (externally LEAN-reported) and gives asymptotic (e^γ+o(1))loglog x, attributed to a GPT-5.5 Pro proof directed by Liam Price. This does not settle the separate Carmichael conjecture about a singleton totient fiber.

## A small example

The positive solutions of φ(m)=4 are exactly m=5,8,10,12, so f_min(4)=5, f_max(4)=12 and the ratio is 12/5. This can be checked by factoring numbers up to a suitable bound; ruling out larger m uses elementary totient inequalities and is not just a finite exploratory scan.
