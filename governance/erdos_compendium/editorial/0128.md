## The question in plain language

An **induced subgraph** is formed by selecting vertices and keeping every edge between them. Erdős and Rousseau asked whether a graph $G$ on $n$ vertices must contain a triangle if **every** induced subgraph on at least $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ vertices has more than $n^2/50$ edges.

Equivalently, does every triangle-free graph admit a set of at least half its vertices spanning at most $n^2/50$ edges? This is the **sparse-half conjecture**. The bound is normalized by the original $n$, not by the chosen subset's size. The strict inequality and the floor convention affect finite cases.

## A small example

A five-cycle is triangle-free. Replacing every vertex by $t$ mutually nonadjacent vertices and every cycle edge by a complete bipartite graph gives a triangle-free graph on $5t$ vertices. Its carefully chosen half-sized subsets have edge counts of order $n^2/50$. This blow-up explains why the conjectured constant is sharp if the assertion holds.

One must search over **all** eligible vertex subsets. Checking only randomly chosen halves could miss the sparsest one; checking a non-induced subgraph would permit deleting edges and change the problem.

## What is known

The universal threshold $1/50$ remains **OPEN/FALSIFIABLE**. Erdős, Faudree, Rousseau and Schelp (1994) established a weaker universal criterion, with $50$ replaced by $16$. Alexander Razborov's primary [2022 journal article](https://doi.org/10.1070/SM9615), *More about sparse halves in triangle-free graphs*, proves that a triangle-free graph always has a half-sized subset spanning at most

$$
\frac{27}{1024}n^2
$$

edges, versus the conjectured $n^2/50$. Since $27/1024$ exceeds $1/50$, this is progress, not the desired universal inequality.

Razborov separately proves the conjectured threshold for graphs of girth at least five, for independence number at least $2n/5$, and for strongly regular graphs. These are restricted classes; they do not collectively cover all triangle-free graphs.

## GCL's current state

The pinned and maintained [Erdős #128](https://www.erdosproblems.com/128), last edited October 2025, label the exact conjecture **FALSIFIABLE**. The original source task is [#1397](https://github.com/grandchallenge/MATH-PROGRAMME/issues/1397). The primary Razborov publisher abstract and constants were checked, but his full flag-algebra argument was not independently reconstructed.

The original issue contains an authenticated [RESULT/1 source report](https://github.com/grandchallenge/MATH-PROGRAMME/issues/1397#issuecomment-6165429624). This is unadjudicated source evidence only, not independent mathematical certification or editorial admission.

## What remains

A bounded task is to enumerate balanced blow-ups of $C_5$ for $t=2,4$, list every vertex subset of size at least $\lfloor5t/2\rfloor$, and calculate its induced-edge count exactly. Acceptance: graph is triangle-free, the minimum induced count is reported with an explicit subset, and comparisons distinguish equality from strict inequality at $n^2/50$. Stop at finite sharpness fixtures; no universal theorem follows.
