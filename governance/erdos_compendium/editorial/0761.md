## The question in plain language

Define χ(G) as graph chromatic number, ζ(G) as the cochromatic clique-or-independent partition number, and δ(G) as the maximum, over orientations of G's edges, of the minimum number of colours needed so that NO colour class contains a directed cycle. Erdős–Neumann-Lara ask whether large χ forces large δ; Erdős–Gimbel ask whether large ζ forces the existence of a subgraph with large δ. Both are OPEN in the maintained source. A positive answer to the second would imply a positive answer to the first using the known #760 relation.

## A small example

Any orientation of a forest is acyclic, so δ(G)=1 for every forest even when G has edges and χ(G)=2. For a triangle K3, orient the three edges cyclically: one colour creates a directed three-cycle, but two colours suffice; hence δ(K3)=2. A transitive orientation of K3 is acyclic, illustrating why δ maximizes over orientations. These small graphs do not settle whether δ can stay bounded while χ or ζ grows without bound.

## What is known

The dichromatic number of a directed graph measures partition into acyclic vertex classes. The maintained #761 describes the undirected graph parameter as 'for any orientation there exists a k-colouring into acyclic classes', i.e. a worst-orientation bound; confusing this with an easier 'there exists an orientation' parameter would trivialize the question by orienting edges according to a vertex order. The source notes #760 connects χ to ζ on a subgraph and attributes the two questions to Erdős–Neumann-Lara and Erdős–Gimbel [ErGi93]. No source-grounded general solution or protected FC #761 theorem was found.
