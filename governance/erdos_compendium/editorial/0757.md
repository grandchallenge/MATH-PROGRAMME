## The question in plain language

Let A be a finite set of n real numbers with the property that every four-element subset B satisfies |B−B|≥11, counting zero and BOTH signs of differences. Erdős and Sós asked for the best universal c such that A contains a Sidon subset of size at least c n, where a Sidon set has all nonzero ordered differences distinct (equivalently pair sums unique). This optimal constant remains OPEN.

## A small example

The four-element set B={0,1,3,5} has positive differences {1,2,3,4,5}, so B−B={0,±1,±2,±3,±4,±5} and |B−B|=11. It is NOT Sidon: 3−1=5−3=2. By contrast {0,1,3,7} has six distinct positive differences and |B−B|=13, and it is Sidon. Thus the local ≥11 condition is strictly weaker than every four points being Sidon.

## What is known

The source records Erdős–Sós c≥1/2, Gyárfás–Lehel 1995 1/2+1/(141·76)≤c≤3/5, and Ma–Tang February 2026 improved 9/17≤c≤4/7 (arXiv:2602.23282). These are bounds on the BEST universal c, not a proof of its exact value.
