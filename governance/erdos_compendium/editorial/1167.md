## The question in plain language

[Erdős Problem #1167](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1167). Can a partition relation on subsets of a power-set-sized cardinal be stepped back down to the original cardinal?

$$
2^\lambda\to(\kappa_\alpha+1)_{\alpha<\gamma}^{r+1}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \lambda\to(\kappa_\alpha)_{\alpha<\gamma}^{r}?
$$

For finite r≥2, lambda infinite cardinal, gamma ordinal of color indices, kappa_alpha cardinals, arrow μ→(κ_alpha)_{alpha<gamma}^r means every gamma-coloring of r-subsets admits a homogeneous subset size κ_alpha in SOME color alpha. Negative stepping-up asks 2^lambda→(κ_alpha+1)^{r+1} implies lambda→(κ_alpha)^r. Cardinal addition κ+1=κ for infinite κ (not ordinal successor). MAJOR SOURCE-FIDELITY ERROR: catalogue snippet omits gamma≥2 and all κ_alpha>r assumptions from original Erdős–Hajnal list. Literal unrestricted version is FALSE; properly constrained conjecture OPEN.

## A small example

For one color gamma=1, μ→(ν)^r_1 iff ν≤μ. Set lambda=aleph0, κ0=aleph1,r=2. The premise 2^aleph0≥aleph1 holds in ZFC and κ0+1=κ0, whereas conclusion aleph0≥aleph1 fails. This rigorous counterexample applies ONLY to omission of gamma≥2, not to the intended question. For finite κ, κ+1 is natural successor; for infinite κ it collapses.
