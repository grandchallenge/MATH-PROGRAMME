## The question in plain language

Let A be a set of positive integers, and r₂(n)=Σ_{a+b=n}1_A(a)1_A(b), counting ORDERED representations. Erdős–Turán asked whether some A and c>0 could satisfy the cumulative relation Σ_{n≤N}r₂(n)=cN+O(1). This is DISPROVED by Erdős–Fuchs; much smaller-than-N^(1/4) discrepancy is impossible under their hypotheses.

## A small example

For A={1,2,3}, the ordered two-sum counts for n=2,3,4,5,6 are 1,2,3,2,1, totaling 9. For A=all positive integers, r₂(n)=n−1 for n≥2, so Σ_{n≤N}r₂(n)=N(N−1)/2, quadratic rather than linear. Such finite/examples with wrong growth do not by themselves rule out a carefully chosen infinite set.

## What is known

Erdős–Fuchs [ErFu56] proved that Σ_{n≤N}r₂(n)=cN+o(N^(1/4)/(log N)^(1/2)) is impossible for c>0. Jurkat (unpublished) and Montgomery–Vaughan [MoVa90] improved the boundary to impossibility of error o(N^(1/4)). Since O(1) is o(N^(1/4)/sqrt(log N)), the original bounded-error conjecture is decisively false. This concerns cumulative ordered sums, NOT the pointwise statement r₂(n)=c+O(1). Full original analytic proofs were not independently reproduced.
