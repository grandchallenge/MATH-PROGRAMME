## The question in plain language

[Erdős Problem #1178](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1178) asks the following. The maintained status is **OPEN**.

For $r,e\ge3$ let $\mathcal F_{r,d,e}$ consist of all $r$‑uniform hypergraphs with $d$ vertices and $e$ edges. Let $d_r(e)$ be the least $d$ with $\mathrm{ex}_r(n,\mathcal F_{r,d,e})=o(n^2)$. Brown–Erdős–Sós conjecture

$$
d_r(e)=(r-2)e+3.
$$

The $o(n^2)$ is an asymptotic bound on extremal edge count, not a finite exact identity. The family forbids $e$ edges supported on at most $d$ vertices, subject to the source's isolated-vertex convention.

## A small example

At $(r,e,d)=(3,3,6)$, a universe of $n=4,5,6$ vertices has the property that any three distinct triples are supported on at most six vertices. Thus it can have at most **two** triples while avoiding $\mathcal F_{3,6,3}$; two triples are allowed. Hence $\mathrm{ex}_3(n,\mathcal F)=2$ for those three finite sizes. This does not prove a limit as $n\to\infty$.

## What is known

The general problem remains **OPEN** at https://www.erdosproblems.com/1178. BES73 showed $d_r(e)\ge(r-2)e+3$; Ruzsa–Szemerédi [RuSz78] proved $d_3(3)=6$, and Erdős–Frankl–Rödl [EFR86] resolved $d_r(3)$ for all $r\ge3$. Sárközy–Selkow [SaSe05] proved upper $(r-2)e+2+\lfloor\log_2e\rfloor$. Conlon–Gishboliner–Levanzov–Shapira [CGLS23] obtained $d_3(e)\le e+O(\log e/\log\log e)$. None solves all $(r,e)$.
