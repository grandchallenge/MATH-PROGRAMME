## The question in plain language

A K_r-free graph (no complete r-vertex subgraph) on n vertices with average degree t is conjectured to have an independent vertex set of size at least c_r n log(t)/t, in the asymptotic regime t sufficiently large. Independence means no edges between any two selected vertices. The positive constant may depend on r but not n,t.

K_r-free means no copy of the complete r-vertex graph, not merely an upper bound on degree. The conjectured independent-set size is on the order of n log(t)/t for large average degree t, with a constant depending on r. The extra loglog(t) denominator in Shearer's general bound is a genuine, presently unresolved loss for larger r.

## A small example

A 5-cycle C5 is triangle-free (r=3), has n=5, average degree t=2 and independent number alpha=2 (non-adjacent vertices such as 0 and 2). K_r-free is GLOBAL absence of a clique, not a condition on just local degrees. Lower expression log(t) is an asymptotic target, not an exact 5-vertex inequality with unspecified c_r.

This finite fixture tests the conventions. It does not certify any of the asymptotic or all-graphs assertions.

## What is known

Maintained Erdős #802 (directly read via public search, 2026-10-14) reports original Ajtai–Erdős–Komlós–Szemerédi [AEKS81] bound n loglog(t+1)/t; Shearer [Sh95] n log(t)/(t loglog(t+1)); Ajtai–Komlós–Szemerédi [AKS80] proves sought bound for r=3; Alon [Al96b] proves bound under stronger neighborhood chromatic-number <=r-2 hypothesis. General r remains OPEN. Original four papers not fully proof-checked; no external status promotion.

The distinctions between established partial theorems, original open parts, and disproved universal claims have been preserved. Historical publications are attributed, rather than presented as new GCL mathematics.

The target bound for fixed $r$ and sufficiently large average degree $t$ is

$$

\alpha(G)\gg_r \frac{n\log t}{t}.

$$

The established general-r bound attributed to Shearer instead has an extra logarithm of a logarithm in the denominator. The $r=3$ result does not automatically extend to all larger forbidden cliques.
