## The question in plain language

For a graph needing ℵ₁ colours, can one find a SINGLE edge lying on a cycle of EVERY sufficiently large length? [Erdős #737](https://www.erdosproblems.com/737) asks for the same edge to work across all large cycle lengths. The answer is PROVED by Thomassen, not merely a statement about existence of some cycle at each length.

## A small example

In the complete graph K₄, every edge lies in a triangle and a 4-cycle, and no cycle longer than four exists. This example illustrates an edge shared across lengths but cannot establish anything about ℵ₁-chromatic graphs.

## What is known

Erdős–Hajnal–Shelah [EHS74] proved that every ℵ₁-chromatic graph contains cycles of all sufficiently large lengths. Thomassen [Th83] established the stronger fixed-edge statement. The quantifier orders differ: ∃e ∀large ℓ versus ∀large ℓ ∃some cycle whose edge can depend on ℓ. The current catalogue credits the former result to Thomassen. Exact primary 1983 proof pages not replayed.
