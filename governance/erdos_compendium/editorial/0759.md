## The question in plain language

Let S_g be the orientable surface of genus g, and z(S_g) the largest cochromatic number of a graph embeddable on S_g, where each cochromatic class induces a clique or independent set. Erdős–Gimbel asked for its asymptotic growth. Gimbel and Thomassen (1997) proved z(S_g)=Θ(√g/log g) as g tends to infinity; thus #759 is SOLVED on the order-of-growth level.

## A small example

The sphere S_0 supports planar graphs. Any planar graph has a proper four-colouring, each independent colour class also a valid cochromatic class, so z(S_0)≤4. This finite-genus example does not determine the coefficient of √g/log g or supply a precise formula at small genus. Orientable genus must not be confused with number of vertices.

## What is known

Gimbel [Gi86] established the upper O(√g) and lower Ω(√g/log g). Gimbel and Thomassen [GiTh97] tightened the upper to O(√g/log g), matching the lower within constants. The maintained source uses asymptotic notation 'asymp', not a numerical leading constant, and does not claim a limit of z(S_g)log g/√g. Original Gimbel–Thomassen proof and surface-embedding hypotheses not independently reconstructed.
