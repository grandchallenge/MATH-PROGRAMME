## The question in plain language

[Erdős Problem #1170](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1170) is classified **OPEN** in the maintained catalogue.

Ask whether it is **consistent with ZFC** that
$$
\omega_2\to(\alpha)_2^2 \quad\text{for every ordinal }\alpha<\omega_2.
$$
The arrow concerns **order type** $\alpha$, not merely cardinality at most $\aleph_1$. The same model of set theory must satisfy the property for **all** smaller ordinals simultaneously. Relative consistency requires a model/forcing construction from stated consistency assumptions; a theorem applying to each $\alpha$ in separate models is insufficient.

## A small example

The six-vertex finite Ramsey threshold illustrates how the *same coloring* must provide some prescribed homogeneous set: every two-coloring of $K_6$ has a monochromatic three-set, whereas $K_5$ admits one without. This exact phase-20 computation has no power to construct a forcing model where the uncountable ordinal arrow holds for all $\alpha<\omega_2$.

## What is known

The maintained catalogue cites Laver [La82] and Foreman–Hajnal [FoHa03], who established **weaker relative-consistency** partition statements with one distinguished target of the form an expression in $\omega_1$ plus one and an arbitrary smaller target. Those hypotheses and conclusions are **not** the all-smaller-ordinals symmetric property requested here. In particular, $\omega_1\cdot2+1$ and $\omega_1^2+1$ should not be conflated when transcribing the historical results. The full problem remains **OPEN**.
