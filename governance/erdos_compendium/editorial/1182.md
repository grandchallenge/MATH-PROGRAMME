## The question in plain language

[Erdős Problem #1182](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1182) asks the following. The maintained status is **OPEN — FINAL SUBQUESTION DISPROVED**.

Let $R(K_3,G)$ be the ordinary two-edge-color Ramsey number, red triangle versus blue copy of $G$. Define $f(n)$ as the **maximum edge count among connected $n$‑vertex graphs** $G$ satisfying $R(K_3,G)=2n-1$. Define $F(n)$ as the **largest edge threshold** such that **every connected $n$‑vertex graph** with at most $F(n)$ edges satisfies this equality. Distinguish existential maximum $f(n)$ from the worst-case threshold $F(n)$, so trivially $F(n)\le f(n)$ under relevant existence conventions. In particular, the question asks if $F(n)/n\to\infty$, which is already answered **NO** by a linear bound.

## A small example

For $n=3$, the three-vertex path $P_3$ has $R(K_3,P_3)=5=2n-1$. Exhaustive enumeration of all $2^6$ red/blue edge colorings of $K_4$ finds one with neither red triangle nor blue two-edge path; all $2^{10}$ colorings of $K_5$ have one of those structures. This verifies the small tree case, **not** any asymptotic estimate of $f(n)$ or $F(n)$.

## What is known

https://www.erdosproblems.com/1182 remains **OPEN** for determining the best orders and constants of $f(n),F(n)$, attributed to Burr–Erdős–Faudree–Rousseau–Schelp [BEFRS80]. Chvátal gives $R(K_3,T_n)=2n-1$ for trees. BEFRS80 established $(17n+1)/15\le F(n)\le(27/4+o(1))n(\log n)^2$ and $n^{3/2}(\log n)^{1/2}\ll f(n)\ll n^{5/3}(\log n)^{2/3}$, with context for $n\ge4$. **Brandt [Br96] proves $F(n)\le84n$**, decisively refuting the final superlinear-ratio speculation. Do not claim the entire graph extremal problem is solved.
