## The question in plain language

[Erdős Problem #1154](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1154). Can algebraically closed-under-addition-and-multiplication subsets of the real line have every possible fractal dimension?

$$
\dim_{\mathrm H}(F)=\alpha,\qquad F\subseteq\mathbb R,\quad F\text{ a subfield or subring}.
$$

For every alpha∈[0,1], ask if there exists a subring OR subfield F⊆R of Hausdorff dimension exactly alpha. This is an existence question for each alpha, not the same as existence of Borel or analytic fields. State carefully the set-theoretic conclusion: a model of ZFC where the assertion holds shows it is NOT DISPROVABLE from ZFC (assuming consistency), but does not show ZFC-independence without a model of failure.

## A small example

Q is a countable subfield of R and has Hausdorff dimension 0; R has dimension 1. Endpoints alpha=0,1 are exact constructive examples, but reveal nothing about intermediate dimensions or definability.
