## The question in plain language

Suppose $A$ and $B$ are Sidon sets of integers from 1 to $N$ whose sets of differences have no common nonzero element. Let $f(N)$ be the largest possible cardinality of a Sidon set in that interval. [Erdős Problem #43](https://www.erdosproblems.com/43) contains **two separate questions**.

First, must the numbers of unordered pairs of different elements satisfy

$$
\binom{|A|}{2}+\binom{|B|}{2}
\le \binom{f(N)}{2}+O(1)?
$$

Second, when $|A|=|B|$, can this bound be improved by a fixed positive fractional saving asymptotically? The maintained registry reports **negative answers to both questions**, but they have different proof routes.

## A small example

For the Sidon set $A=\{1,2,4\}$, there are three unordered pairs of distinct elements and three positive differences. A singleton $B=\{3\}$ has no nonzero differences, so it is automatically compatible with $A$. The two pair counts add to $3+0=3$.

This finite example cannot disprove a bound with an unspecified universal additive constant. The first conjecture fails only if its excess can become arbitrarily large.

## What is known

The negative answer to the **first** question follows from the general statement for any fixed $M$ reported for [Problem #42](https://www.erdosproblems.com/42). Choose $A$ with the maximal possible size $f(N)$. For each fixed $M$, #42 supplies, at sufficiently large $N$, a compatible $B$ with $M$ elements. If the first inequality held with one fixed constant $C$, it would require

$$
\binom M2\le C
$$

for every $M$, a contradiction. This implication is elementary **given** the deep #42 theorem; it is not an independent verification of that theorem.

The public registry attributes a negative answer to the **equal-cardinality** question to Kevin Barreto. A related upper bound was discussed by Terence Tao. The first-question contradiction by itself does not resolve the second question, so their mathematical evidence must remain separate.

## What remains

Both original bounds are reported disproved. GCL's separate work is to source-check the general #42 proof for the first part and independently inspect Barreto's equal-size construction for the second, preserving external attribution and exact quantifiers.
