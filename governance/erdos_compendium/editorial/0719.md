## The question in plain language

For $r\ge2$, define the extremal function

$$
t_r(n)=\mathrm{ex}_r(n,K_{r+1}^r).
$$

It is the maximum number of r-edges in an n-vertex r-uniform hypergraph containing no complete $K_{r+1}^r$. Erdős–Sauer ask whether EVERY n-vertex r-uniform hypergraph G can be covered by at most $t_r(n)$ copies of $K_r^r$ (single r-edges) and $K_{r+1}^r$ (complete (r+1)-vertex cliques), with no two chosen copies sharing an r-edge. This is OPEN. 'No sharing an r-edge' is an edge-disjoint packing condition, not merely different vertex sets.

## A small example

For r=2, $G=K_4$ has six edges. One triangle covers three edges and the other three edges become singleton $K_2$ copies: four pieces, equal to $\mathrm{ex}(4,K_3)=4$. For $G=K_5$, two edge-disjoint triangles (e.g. on {1,2,3} and {1,4,5}) cover six edges and the four remaining edges give six pieces, equal to $\mathrm{ex}(5,K_3)=6$. These are finite certificates, not a general packing theorem.

## What is known

If G has e r-edges and we choose t pairwise r-edge-disjoint copies of $K_{r+1}^r$, each replaces r+1 singleton edge pieces by ONE block. The total number of pieces is $e-rt$. Therefore the requested decomposition is equivalent (for the chosen G) to finding at least $\lceil(e-t_r(n))/r\rceil$ edge-disjoint complete (r+1)-vertex r-hypergraphs whenever $e>t_r(n)$. This reduction is elementary and source-faithful; the existence of such a packing is the hard point. The maintained Erdős #719 still says OPEN, a conjecture of Erdős and Sauer.
