## The question in plain language

For N≥2, Erdős asks whether N can always be expressed as the quotient of two products of k consecutive positive integers, with k≥2 and with the upper interval completely later than the lower: N=Π_{i=1}^k(m+i)/Π_{i=1}^k(n+i), m≥n+k. This remains OPEN. Squares are a particularly important case. One cannot silently allow k=1 or overlapping intervals.

## A small example

An exact positive witness for N=9 is k=3,n=11,m=25: (26·27·28)/(12·13·14)=19656/2184=9. In contrast, N=4 has NO representation with k=2. Writing x=m+1 and y=n+1, the required equation x(x+1)=4y(y+1) has no integer solution: 2y(2y+1) is too small and (2y+1)(2y+2) too large. This excludes only length two, not all k.
