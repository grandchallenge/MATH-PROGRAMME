## The question in plain language

The $n$ dimensional hypercube $Q_n$ has one vertex for each binary string of length $n$. Two vertices are adjacent if their strings differ in exactly one coordinate. The cube has $2^n$ vertices and $n2^{n-1}$ edges. Every four-cycle uses two distinct coordinate directions.

Let $f(n)$ be the maximum number of edges in a subgraph of $Q_n$ containing no four-cycle. Erdős asks whether

$$
f(n)\le\left(\frac12+o(1)\right)n2^{n-1}.
$$

Thus the conjecture says that the extremal **edge density within the cube** tends to one half. The asymptotic quantifier is essential.

## A small example

$Q_2=C_4$ has four edges. Deleting one edge destroys its only four-cycle, so $f(2)=3$, which is a fraction $3/4$ of all cube edges. This finite example does not refute the proposed limiting density.

In $Q_3$, six distinct square faces arise by fixing one coordinate and varying the other two. A square-free edge set may use at most three edges from each face.

## What is known

The conjecture remains **OPEN**. Erdős's constructions have square-free cube density at least $1/2+c/n$ for a positive constant $c$. Brass, Harborth and Nienborg improved this to $1/2+c/\sqrt n$. These lower bounds are consistent with a limiting density of one half.

Balogh, Hu, Lidický and Liu, [*Upper bounds on the size of 4- and 6-cycle-free subgraphs of the hypercube*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.0209), prove that the density of $C_4$ free cube subgraphs is at most $0.6068$, using adapted flag-algebra methods. Their primary arXiv abstract was inspected. The maintained catalogue also reports Baber's subsequent $0.60318$ upper bound. Neither figure proves the conjectured one-half limit, and the complete flag-algebra certificates have not been independently replayed.

## What remains

A bounded task is to compute exact maxima for $n=2,3,4$. Use one binary variable per cube edge; constrain each square face to contain at most three selected edges. Acceptance: provide a feasible square-free edge set and a matching exhaustive or integer-programming upper-bound certificate for each dimension. Stop after finite checks without interpreting them as asymptotic evidence.
