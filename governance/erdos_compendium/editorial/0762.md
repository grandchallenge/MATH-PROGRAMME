## The question in plain language

For a graph G with no K5 and cochromatic number ζ(G)≥4, Erdős, Gimbel and Straight conjectured χ(G)≤ζ(G)+2, where ζ is the minimum partition into induced cliques or independent sets. The conjecture is DISPROVED by Raphael Steiner [St24b], with a graph having clique number ω=4, ζ=4 and χ=7. The threshold is violated as 7>4+2=6.

## A small example

The four-vertex complete graph K4 has ω(K4)=4 and χ(K4)=4, but ζ(K4)=1 because its entire vertex set induces one clique. It does NOT satisfy the conjecture's ζ≥4 hypothesis; thus K4 is not a counterexample. The Steiner graph with ω=4, ζ=4 and χ=7 supplies the actual counterexample, but its large graph and parameter certificates are externally attributed rather than independently checked here.

## What is known

The maintained page credits Erdős–Gimbel–Straight with χ(G)≤ζ(G)+f(t) for K_t-free graphs, with f(t) dependent on t, not necessarily f(5)=2. Steiner [St24b] refutes this sharper f(5)=2. A February 2026 forum reports a formalized counterexample aided by Aristotle and ChatGPT, involving a finite base check of an 11-vertex graph. Follow-up comments in June 2026 discuss replacing `native_decide` with `decide` and a large explicit counterexample (1177 vertices, 5400 edges). GCL's pinned formal statement exists but its exact axiom closure and compilation were not replayed; do not certify merely from externally reported Lean.
