## The question in plain language

Does **every permutation of the positive integers** contain a monotone four-term arithmetic progression? This is a subsequence $a_i,a_j,a_k,a_l$ whose indices satisfy $i<j<k<l$ and whose values form an arithmetic progression in either direction, with **nonzero** common difference.

Erdős and Graham asked whether the answer was yes. To refute this, one must construct one permutation of **all** positive integers containing **no** such four-term subsequence. A long finite prefix avoiding progressions is not enough.

## A small example

In the finite list $3,1,2,4$, the set of numbers $1,2,3,4$ is an arithmetic progression, but it appears in the order $3,1,2,4$, so it is not monotone. A candidate infinite construction must prevent this for **all** possible four-term progressions, including negative common differences and progressions whose indices are far apart.

For a finite prefix of an injective sequence, enumerate all index quadruples $i<j<k<l$ and reject if $a_i+a_k=2a_j$ and $a_j+a_l=2a_k$ with the entries distinct. The two equalities encode a four-term progression without floating-point arithmetic.

## What is known

**DISPROVED in September 2026.** Boon Suan Ho, [*A 4AP-free permutation of the positive integers*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12780), submitted **11 September 2026**, constructs a permutation with no monotone four-term arithmetic progression. The author's [Lean 4 formalization and executable Python implementation](https://github.com/boonsuan/4ap) explicitly verify the existence theorem and the permutation's bijectivity.

An independent later construction and Lean formalization appeared on **14 September 2026**, and its [publication note](https://github.com/coleski/erdos196) recognizes Ho's priority. Conjectures.io separately reports a verified counterexample for the equivalent nonnegative-integer formulation, but its Lean verification and review are **external**, not GCL protected admission.

The maintained [Erdős #196](https://www.erdosproblems.com/196) page and pinned August 2025 task classify the question **OPEN** because they have not incorporated this September 2026 development. This is a dated **status drift**, not permission to rewrite the protected source lock. The original 1977 Davis–Entringer–Graham–Simmons results proved three-term necessity but only avoided five-term progressions.
