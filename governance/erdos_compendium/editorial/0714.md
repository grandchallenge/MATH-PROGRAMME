## The question in plain language

For r≥2 let $K_{r,r}$ denote the complete bipartite graph with r vertices in each part. Erdős asks whether $\mathrm{ex}(n,K_{r,r})$ is bounded below by a constant times n^(2−1/r) for every fixed r (as n→∞), matching the classical Kövári–Sós–Turán upper exponent. The maintained page remains OPEN for general r, not for the known r=2 and r=3 instances.

## A small example

$K_{2,2}$ is exactly a four-cycle $C_4$. A graph containing no $C_4$ has any two vertices with at most one common neighbour. Hence $\sum_v \binom{\deg(v)}{2}\le\binom{n}{2}$. Convexity then gives an $O(n^{3/2})$ edge upper bound. For n=5 the five-cycle $C_5$ has five edges and no 4-cycle, providing an exact but non-sharp small lower fixture.

## What is known

Kövári–Sós–Turán 1954 proved $\mathrm{ex}(n,K_{r,r})=O_r(n^{2-1/r})$. Known matching exponents hold for r=2, where $\mathrm{ex}(n,C_4)=(1/2+o(1))n^{3/2}$, and for r=3 by Brown 1966 and independently Erdős–Rényi–Sós 1966. These constructions do not extend as an unconditional general r≥4 theorem. The source page was edited 2026-01-23; original KST54, Brown and ERS paper proofs were not independently read in this tranche.
