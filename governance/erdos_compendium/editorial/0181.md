## The question in plain language

The $n$ dimensional hypercube graph $Q_n$ has the $2^n$ binary words of length $n$ as vertices; two words are adjacent if they differ in **exactly one coordinate**. Its number of edges is $n2^{n-1}$.

The ordinary two-colour Ramsey number $R(Q_n)$ is the smallest $N$ for which every red-blue colouring of the edges of $K_N$ contains a monochromatic subgraph isomorphic to $Q_n$. Burr and Erdős conjectured that

$$
R(Q_n)\le C\,2^n
$$

with an absolute constant $C$ independent of $n$. This is a linear bound in the **number of vertices of the cube**, not linear in its dimension $n$.

## A small example

The two-dimensional cube $Q_2$ is a four-cycle. It has four vertices and four edges, unlike $K_4$, which has six edges. For the three-dimensional cube, each of eight vertices has degree three, giving $3\cdot8/2=12$ edges. A graph copy need not be **induced**, so additional edges among its cube vertices do not disqualify it as a monochromatic subgraph.

The immediate upper estimate follows by embedding $Q_n$ into the complete graph on the same $2^n$ vertices and applying the ordinary clique Ramsey number: $R(Q_n)\le R(K_{2^n})$. This grows much faster than the conjectured $C2^n$.

## What is known

The linear conjecture remains **OPEN**. Konstantin Tikhomirov, [*A remark on the Ramsey number of the hypercube*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.14568), 2022, showed an exponential improvement on the trivial clique bound:

$$
R(Q_n)\ll 2^{(2-c)n}
$$

for an absolute $c>0$, with $c\approx0.03656$ permissible. This is a major reduction in the exponent of two, but $2^{(2-c)n}$ is still asymptotically far above $2^n$.

Erdős noted in 1993 that even whether $R(Q_n)/2^n$ tends to infinity had been uncertain. The [maintained #181](https://www.erdosproblems.com/181) continues to classify the desired linear bound OPEN, not proved by Tikhomirov.

## What remains

A bounded finite fixture constructs $Q_n$ for $n=1,2,3,4$ and checks vertex count $2^n$, degree $n$, and edge count $n2^{n-1}$. For $Q_2$, verify that a candidate monochromatic four-cycle is accepted even when the four chosen vertices have extra edges in the ambient complete graph. Stop before interpreting small Ramsey computations as a proof of the universal constant $C$.
