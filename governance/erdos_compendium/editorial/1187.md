## The question in plain language

[Erdős Problem #1187](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1187) is **SOLVED — MIXED YES/NO** in the maintained catalogue.

For $k\ge3$ and **every finite coloring of the integers**, question (i) asks for arbitrarily long **monochromatic progressions whose terms are all prime**. Question (ii) asks whether there must be monochromatic integer APs with **prime common difference**. These differ: prime TERMS versus prime STEP. The second can be false even though the first is true.

## A small example

The residue check verifies prime differences 2,3,5,7,11,13: each is nonzero modulo 4. In the two-color 0,1 / 2,3 scheme every length-3 AP with any tested prime difference changes color. The modular argument extends to all primes, but checking finitely many steps does not prove the Green–Tao positive-density theorem.

## What is known

The maintained https://www.erdosproblems.com/1187 reports (i) **YES** from Green–Tao [GrTa08]'s theorem that positive relative density subsets of the primes contain arbitrarily long APs. One prime color class under any finite coloring has positive relative upper density. For (ii), color integers by residues modulo 4. A prime step is never 0 modulo 4, hence not even a monochromatic pair with prime difference exists in that four-coloring. Two-color residues 0,1 red versus 2,3 blue prevent 3-term prime-step APs.
