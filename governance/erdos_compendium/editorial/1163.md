## A small example

In S3 the six actual subgroups have orders 1,2,2,2,3,6. Uniformly picking one of these six gives order probabilities P(1)=1/6,P(2)=1/2,P(3)=1/6,P(6)=1/6. Picking a random conjugacy class of subgroups has a different law (the three 2-subgroups belong to one class). This exact finite example demonstrates the necessary missing measure.
