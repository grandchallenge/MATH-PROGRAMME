## The question in plain language

For a complex polynomial $p(z)$, its unit **lemniscate** is the locus of points with $|p(z)|=1$. It can consist of one or several closed curves, with complicated shapes and sometimes singular crossings. Erdős, Herzog and Piranian asked: among monic polynomials of a fixed degree $n$, is the **total arclength** of that locus largest for

$$
p(z)=z^n-1?
$$

“Monic” means that the coefficient of $z^n$ equals one. Without this normalization, multiplying a polynomial by a constant changes the level set and its geometry.

## A small example

For $p(z)=z^n$, the condition $|p(z)|=1$ is simply $|z|=1$; its lemniscate is the unit circle and has length $2\pi$ for **every** $n$. By contrast, the petals of $|z^n-1|=1$ have total length asymptotic to $2n+4\log2$, much longer for large $n$. The latter value does not follow from counting the number of petals alone: the length of each must be integrated.

The conjecture is an *exact finite-degree extremal assertion*, not only a leading-order estimate.

## What is known

**PROVED for all sufficiently large degrees; the all-degree formulation remains unconfirmed.** Terence Tao's [*The maximal length of the Erdős–Herzog–Piranian lemniscate in high degree*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12455), submitted 13 December 2025, proves that for every sufficiently large $n$, the maximizing lemniscate is the one for $z^n-1$. Tao's author [exposition](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2025/12/15/the-maximal-length-of-the-erdos-herzog-piranian-lemniscate-in-high-degree/) explains the exact relation to the older conjecture.

Earlier Eremenko and Hayman proved the case $n=2$; Fryntov and Nazarov had already shown the asymptotic extremal length

$$
\sup_{p\text{ monic, }\deg p=n}\ell(\{|p|=1\})=2n+O(n^{7/8}).
$$

Thus the leading asymptotic scale is settled, but it is not logically valid to infer every intermediate degree from a theorem that requires sufficiently large $n$ and one isolated small case. The maintained catalogue labels #114 “falsifiable/open” for this reason. The 2025 source is a preprint and its full proof is not independently reproduced here.

## GCL's current state

The original issue contains an authenticated [RESULT/1 source report](https://github.com/grandchallenge/MATH-PROGRAMME/issues/1383#issuecomment-6162498293). This is unadjudicated source evidence only, not independent mathematical certification or editorial admission.

## What remains

A bounded successor is to compute the arclength of the degree-one lemniscate $|z-a|=1$ and show translation leaves length unchanged. Then audit the available $n=2$ source and distinguish exact finite degree from “all sufficiently large.” Acceptance: unit-circle length $2\pi$ independently checked, the correct translation-invariance statement, and no extrapolation from the large-degree theorem.
