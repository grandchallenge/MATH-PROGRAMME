## The question in plain language

[Erdős Problem #1166](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1166). How many different lattice sites have ever been the random walk's most-visited site by time n?

$$U_n=\bigcup_{k=0}^nF(k),\quad |U_n|\ll(\log n)^2\text{ eventually a.s.}$$

For the same simple planar random walk and favorites F(k), ask whether union of all DISTINCT sites ever favorite by time n, U_n=∪_{k≤n}F(k), satisfies |U_n|≤(log n)^{O(1)} a.s. eventually. This differs from |F(n)| at a single time. Maximum local time T_n=max_x f_n(x) enters proof.

## A small example

Exact enumeration of all 4096 six-step paths gives maximum 7 distinct ever-favorite sites by n6 and maximum ratio |U6|/T6=7. This can exceed eventual constant3 due to early >3 favorites; it does NOT contradict eventual a.s. polylog theorem.
