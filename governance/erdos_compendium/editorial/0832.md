## The question in plain language

For ordinary graphs a large chromatic number forces many edges. Erdős asked for an analogous sharp threshold for uniform hypergraphs. Alon's counterexamples defeat the proposed general rule, but not the remaining three-uniform case at sufficiently large chromatic number.

For a fixed r>=3 and sufficiently large chromatic number k, the proposed edge-minimal r-uniform hypergraph is the complete r-graph on (r-1)(k-1)+1 vertices. The statement is false for all r>=4, but the large-k r=3 case remains OPEN. Chromatic number means vertex colors with no monochromatic hyperedge; the finite r=k=3 Fano exception does not settle r=3 for large k.

## A small example

For r=k=3, the seven triples of the Fano plane have chromatic number 3 but only seven edges; the complete 3-graph on (r-1)(k-1)+1=5 vertices has 10 edges. Exhaustively enumerating 128 two-colorings finds zero valid Fano colorings. Explicit seven-edge witness does not disprove eventual large-k r=3 statement.

This finite calculation is not an asymptotic or all-parameter proof.

## What is known

Maintained /latex/832 directly read 2026-10-14. Alon [Al85] *Hypergraphs with high chromatic number*, Graphs and Combinatorics 1985 disproves all r>=4, with for sufficiently large r and k at least Cr an upper construction at most (7/8)^r times proposed complete-graph edge count. Akolzin–Shabanov [AkSh16] bound minimum number of edges for chromatic number greater than k by constants times (r/log r)k^r and (r^3 log r)k^r, not the same threshold. Cherkashin–Petrov [ChPe20] prove fixed-r normalized limit exists. All full primary proof texts require independent review.

For r=3,k=3 the proposed complete hypergraph has ten edges: all three-subsets of five points. The seven Fano triples already require three colors and therefore refute this small-k instance. That does not settle the remaining r=3 question with k large. Alon's 1985 results disprove the general statement for r>=4. Follow-up results on chromatic number STRICTLY GREATER than k need indexing care.
