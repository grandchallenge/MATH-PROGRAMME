## The question in plain language

In a group $G$, two elements commute if $xy=yx$. Suppose no subset of more than $n$ elements can be **pairwise noncommuting**. Equivalently, every collection of more than $n$ elements contains two distinct elements that commute.

Let $a(G)$ be the smallest number of *abelian subgroups* whose union is $G$, and put

$$
h(n)=\sup_{\omega(G)\le n}a(G),
$$

where $\omega(G)$ is the largest number of pairwise noncommuting elements. Erdős #117 asks for the growth of $h(n)$. The covering sets must be **subgroups** and must cover all elements, not merely a list of selected test elements.

## A small example

If $\omega(G)\le1$, every pair of distinct elements commutes, and $G$ itself is abelian, so $a(G)=1$. This gives $h(1)=1$. For any group, each subset consisting of pairwise commuting elements generates an abelian subgroup; hence covering by abelian subgroups relates to colouring the graph whose adjacent vertices **do not** commute.

The whole group may be infinite even when its noncommutativity parameter is bounded. Finiteness of $G$ is **not** a hypothesis in the original problem.

## What is known

Pyber's [1987 paper](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s2-35.2.287), *Journal of the London Mathematical Society* 35, 287–295, gives exponential upper and lower scales: absolute constants $c_2>c_1>1$ exist with $c_1^n<h(n)<c_2^n$ in the historical parameter convention. Its publisher abstract explicitly establishes the related upper bound $|G:Z(G)|\le c^n$.

A **new, unadjudicated 20 August 2026 preprint** by Guillaume Lecomte, [*Sharp Asymptotics for Abelian Covers of Groups with Bounded Noncommutativity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20507), claims

$$
\log_2 h(n)=\frac n2+
O\bigl(\sqrt n\,(\log(n+2))^3\bigr),
\qquad
h(n)^{1/n}\longrightarrow\sqrt2.
$$

It attributes a matching lower bound to extraspecial $2$ groups and an upper bound to isoclinism reduction, alternating commutator forms, and Sylow/Fitting analysis. Its explicit section 2 relates noncommuting graphs to abelian covers and discusses reduction of groups of bounded noncommutativity to finite stem representatives.

**This is a research claim, not an independently accepted solution.** GCL inspected the source abstract, the reduction statement, and the proof architecture but did not verify every central-series estimate or the uniform error term. The maintained [Erdős #117](https://www.erdosproblems.com/117), last edited January 2026, remains **OPEN** and does not incorporate the August preprint. Its “open” status is historically accurate but must be read alongside this unresolved new claim.

## What remains

An immediate bounded successor is to verify the claimed graph-theoretic equivalence $a(G)=\max(1,\chi(\Gamma_G))$ for finite groups such as $S_3$ and $D_8$, and to check the passage to quotients by the centre. Then independently referee the preprint's sharp bound for extraspecial $2$ groups and the finite $p$ group upper-bound lemma. Acceptance for a status upgrade requires source-fidelity, valid uniform hypotheses and independent proof-level review, not a preprint abstract alone.
