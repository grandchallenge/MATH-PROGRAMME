## The question in plain language

Let $A_1,A_2,\ldots$ be a countable family of **infinite subsets of the positive integers**, each written in increasing order as $A_i=\{a_{i1}<a_{i2}<\cdots\}$. Assign each integer a sign $f(n)\in\{-1,1\}$.

Erdős asked whether there is **one** signing such that, for every fixed integer $d\ge1$, the initial sums along the first $d$ sets stay bounded, uniformly in how far each set is traversed:

$$
\sup_{\substack{1\le i\le d\\m\ge1}}
\left|\sum_{j=1}^m f(a_{ij})\right|\le C(d).
$$

The constant may depend on $d$, but not on the prefix length $m$, and the signs are shared among all $A_i$.

## A small example

Take $A_1$ to be the even positive integers and $A_2$ the odd positive integers. Giving consecutive members of **each** set alternating signs makes every prefix sum of both enumerations have absolute value at most one. This example has no overlap, so it does not capture the challenge: when sets overlap, the same integer must keep the same sign in every set.

On any finite truncation one can compare the maximum prefix discrepancy over the first $d$ sets. That computation cannot itself establish one infinite signing for infinitely many constraints.

## What is known

**PROVED.** József Beck solved Erdős's question in [Be81]. His later work, *A discrepancy problem: balancing infinite dimensional vectors* (2017), gives an explicit parameter dependence

$$
C(d)\ll_\varepsilon d^{4+\varepsilon}
$$

for each fixed $\varepsilon>0$. This is distinct from Erdős #177, where the polynomial exponent attributed to Beck for all fixed-difference progressions is $8+\varepsilon$.

The [maintained #178](https://www.erdosproblems.com/178) reports that an external formalization of Beck's result was produced in 2026 and labels the problem **PROVED (LEAN)**. The public discussion attributes the automated formalization to Aristotle. A published report of formalization does not establish that GCL has independently built the exact Lean snapshot, checked its allowed axioms or matched it to its protected provider.
