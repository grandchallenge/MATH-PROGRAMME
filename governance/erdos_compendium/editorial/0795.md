## The question in plain language

Let g(n) be maximum cardinality of A subset [1,n] with distinct products over every two different SUBSETS of A, each original element included at most once; empty product=1. Determine second-order term beyond pi(n).

**Primary-source improvement:** Raghavan, Acta Mathematica Hungarica 177 (2025), 363–377, DOI 10.1007/s10474-025-01578-4, Theorem 1.3: the published O(N^(5/12)) remainder is stronger than the initial arXiv O(N^(5/12+o(1))) remainder. The prime-and-prime-square construction supplies the elementary sharp lower term.

## A small example

Set A={2,3,4,9}, comprised of primes and prime squares, has sixteen distinct subset products: prime exponents (0,1,2,3) for each prime 2 and 3 are uniquely encoded by including p and/or p²; in contrast {2,3,6} is invalid because 2*3=6. Presence of 1 also forces collision with empty product.

The calculation illustrates the exact definitions and hypotheses. No single finite example establishes the reported asymptotic assertion.
