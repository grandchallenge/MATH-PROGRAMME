## The question in plain language

The Ramsey number $R(3,k)$ is the smallest $N$ such that every red-blue colouring of $K_N$ contains either a red triangle or a blue complete graph on $k$ vertices. Equivalently, every triangle-free graph on at least $R(3,k)$ vertices has an independent set of size $k$.

Erdős asked for a **sharp asymptotic formula**. It is known that $R(3,k)$ grows on the scale $k^2/\log k$, but the leading constant is not yet settled:

$$
R(3,k)\sim c\frac{k^2}{\log k}\quad\text{for which constant }c?
$$

The goal is to determine the coefficient, not simply the exponent of $k$.

## A small example

The five-cycle $C_5$ has no triangle and has independence number two. Therefore $R(3,3)>5$. Classical elementary Ramsey theory also gives $R(3,3)=6$, consistent with the graph interpretation. A lower-bound construction is a triangle-free graph with unusually **small** independence number; it gives a counterexample to forcing an independent $k$ set at that order.

## What is known

The precise constant remains **OPEN**. Kim proved in 1995 that $R(3,k)\gg k^2/\log k$, originally with a small constant. Shearer's upper analysis and earlier Ajtai–Komlós–Szemerédi work give

$$
R(3,k)\le(1+o(1))\frac{k^2}{\log k}.
$$

Bohman–Keevash and Pontiveros–Griffiths–Morris established lower constant $1/4$. Campos–Jenssen–Michelen–Sahasrabudhe later improved it to $1/3$, and Hefty–Horn–King–Pfender, [*Improving $R(3,k)$ in just two bites*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19718), improve it to **$1/2$**:

$$
\left(\frac12+o(1)\right)\frac{k^2}{\log k}
\le R(3,k)
\le(1+o(1))\frac{k^2}{\log k}.
$$

The maintained [Erdős #165](https://www.erdosproblems.com/165), edited March 2026, identifies one-half as the conjectured actual constant. The 2025 preprint's full probabilistic proof has not been independently reconstructed; the sharp upper constant remains one and is not already one-half.

## What remains

A bounded successor enumerates all graphs on at most seven vertices and verifies $R(3,3)=6$ by exhaustive red-blue edge colourings. Acceptance: identify the $C_5$ lower witness, show every six-vertex graph has a triangle or independent triple, and retain the distinction between finite Ramsey certificates and asymptotic constants. A proof-level successor would independently review the 2025 triangle-free process and its coefficient before attempting the remaining upper bound.
