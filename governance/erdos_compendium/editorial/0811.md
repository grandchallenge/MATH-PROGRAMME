## The question in plain language

A completely balanced m-edge-coloring of K_n, where m=e(H) and n≡1 (mod m), has exactly (n-1)/m edges of EACH color at EACH vertex. Classify those graphs H for which every such balanced coloring for all sufficiently large eligible n contains a RAINBOW H (all m edges with distinct colors). The classification remains OPEN; the assertion that EVERY H works has been DISPROVED.

The category of target graphs with the balanced rainbow property remains to be classified. In particular, 2023 published work constructs balanced six-colorings of complete graphs on 13^k vertices with no rainbow K4. Thus universal success for every target is false, while the C6 and general classification questions remain live.

## A small example

For n=7 and m=3, color edge {i,j} of K7 by cyclic distance min((i-j) mod7,(j-i) mod7) in {1,2,3}. Each vertex sees exactly TWO edges of each color, so the coloring is balanced: (n-1)/m=2. This is a small balanced coloring, not proof of existence/absence of rainbow H for arbitrarily large n.

This finite fixture tests the conventions. It does not certify any of the asymptotic or all-graphs assertions.

## What is known

Maintained #811 remains OPEN as a classification question, but the [Clemen–Wagner primary published abstract](https://doi.org/10.37236/11965) directly proves the K4 counterexample and Clemen–Wagner, *Balanced Edge-Colorings Avoiding Rainbow Cliques of Size Four*, Electronic J Combinatorics 30(3) (2023) P3.17 directly published abstract shows balanced 6-colorings of K_(13^k) without a rainbow K4 for each k. Axenovich–Clemen, *Rainbow subgraphs in edge-colored complete graphs: Answering two questions by Erdős and Tuza*, J Graph Theory 106 (2024) 57–66 DOI 10.1002/jgt.23063, gives more clique families. Original journal abstract and full theorem 3.3 on Wiley page checked. Their constructions DO NOT classify all H or settle C6, and should not convert OPEN to wholly DISPROVED.

The distinctions between established partial theorems, original open parts, and disproved universal claims have been preserved. Historical publications are attributed, rather than presented as new GCL mathematics.

If the target graph has $m$ edges, a balanced $m$ coloring of $K_n$ requires

$$
n\equiv1\pmod m,\qquad
d_i(v)=\frac{n-1}{m}
$$

for every vertex $v$ and each color $i$. The 2023 paper constructs a balanced six-coloring of $K_{13^k}$ with no rainbow $K_4$, for all positive integers $k$. This refutes the all-target assertion, not the open classification of all possible targets.
