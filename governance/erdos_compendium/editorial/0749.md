## The question in plain language

For every ε>0, does there exist A⊆positive integers with LOWER asymptotic density of the sumset A+A at least 1−ε, while its ordered two-sum representation counts r_A(t)=|{(a,b)∈A²:a+b=t}| remain bounded by a constant depending only on ε? The maintained #749 remains OPEN for LOWER density. The analogous UPPER-density question was proved in 2026 by Aron Bhalla with assistance from GPT-5.4, but that does NOT imply the lower-density claim.

## A small example

For a finite Sidon-like set A={1,3,7}, all unordered pair sums (repetitions allowed) are distinct; hence the ordered r_A(t) is at most two for every integer t, but A+A is finite and has BOTH lower and upper density zero. This demonstrates bounded representations alone do not guarantee dense sumsets, and a finite example cannot test the infinite lower-density requirement.

## What is known

The protected FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/749.lean encodes the LOWER-density formulation and has `answer(sorry)` and `by sorry`; it is not a certified solution. The maintained source explicitly credits Bhalla with an UPPER-density construction giving upper density≥1−ε and r_A(t)=O(1/ε) for each ε. In a different remark Erdős and Sárközy conjectured a limitation on upper density for bounded 1_A*1_{−A}, involving DIFFERENCES not sums; it must not be substituted for r_A of positive sums. The original Erdős [Er94b,p.263] passage remains to be checked for upper-versus-lower intent.
