## The question in plain language

[Erdős Problem #1186](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1186) is **OPEN — MANDATORY CRITICAL** in the maintained catalogue.

Among all two-colorings of $[n]$, count nontrivial monochromatic $k$‑term APs, each counted **once** by its starting point and **positive** difference. Let $\delta_k$ measure the least asymptotic guaranteed count, normalized by $n^2$; the formal definition needs a $\liminf$ or a proven limit of finite minima. This is distinct from the **cyclic finite-field** parameter $\widetilde\delta_k$, which counts APs under modular conventions.

## A small example

Exact enumeration of all $2^n$ binary colorings on $[n]$ for $n=8,10,12$ finds minimum monochromatic 3-AP counts **0, 1, 2**, respectively; the receipt retains minimizing bitmasks, total 3-AP counts and normalized exact fractions. A separate cyclic-prime-field enumeration at $p=5,7,11$ counts **unordered** 3-element modular APs. None of these small minima proves an asymptotic constant; notably the cyclic and interval normalization differ.

## What is known

The catalogue https://www.erdosproblems.com/1186 keeps sharp $\delta_k$ **OPEN** [Er80,p.93]. Van der Waerden implies $\delta_k\gg_k1$, random binary coloring upper $\delta_k\le1/((k-1)2^k)$. Parrilo–Robertson–Saracino [PRS08] prove $1675/32768\le\delta_3\le117/2192$ (about 0.0511–0.0534); the upper bound is conjectured sharp. For cyclic prime fields, $\widetilde\delta_3=1/8$ and Wolf [Wo10], Lu–Peng [LuPe12] give $7/192\le\widetilde\delta_4\le17/300$, under their counting convention. These are source-attributed, not GCL-independent SDP certificates.
