## The question in plain language

[Erdős Problem #1206](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1206) has maintained status **OPEN — RECENT PARTIAL CUBIC PROGRESS** at this audit.

Among the **first $N$ positive cubes** $\{1^3,2^3,\dots,N^3\}$, ask for a **Sidon subset of cardinality $\gg N$**, uniformly in $N$. Equivalently, does there exist a positive-density subset $A\subset\mathbb N$ such that $\{a^3:a\in A\}$ has **unique unordered pair sums, repetitions included**? Careful: a positive density set of indices is stronger than just a family of $N$‑dependent large subsets unless one builds a compatible infinite limit. The source also asks related extremal questions for higher powers and arbitrary finite index sets.

## A small example

The familiar taxicab identity

$$
1^3+12^3=9^3+10^3=1729
$$

shows the first twelve cubes are **not** collectively Sidon. Exhaustive inspection of all $2^{15}$ subsets of the first fifteen cubes finds a **Sidon subset of size 14** (removing one of the cubes participating in the unique low-range collision), with all unordered sums including repetition checked. This finite near-full fraction provides **no** asymptotic lower bound linear in $N$.

## What is known

The maintained https://www.erdosproblems.com/1206 keeps the **positive-proportion cubic-Sidon** questions OPEN [Er80,p.109]. Gabdullin–Konyagin [GaKo24] obtained a Sidon interval of consecutive cubes of **$cN^{1/2}$** near $N$. Garaev–Garayev–Konyagin [GGK26] improved the index-interval length to **$N^{4/7-o(1)}$ infinitely often**, and for fourth powers to $N^{3/5}$ for all $N$. These are stronger-than-trivial but **sublinear**; no positive-density or linear-sized construction is certified. References #324 and #773 concern distinct power variants.
