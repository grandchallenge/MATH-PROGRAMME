## What is known

A prime p divides binom(2n,n) iff adding n+n in base p has at least one carry (Kummer's theorem). Equivalently v_p(binomial)>0 by Legendre. This offers exact finite support enumeration, and the pair (87,88) can be directly checked without materializing a new theorem.
