## The question in plain language

For positive integers n≠m, can the two central binomial coefficients $\binom{2n}{n}$ and $\binom{2m}{m}$ have exactly the SAME SET of prime divisors for infinitely many distinct ordered pairs? The maintained #730 now reports a solution by GPT Pro prompted by Price, with quantitative lower bounds for neighboring central-binomial prime-support coincidences. ## What is known

A prime p divides $\binom{2n}{n}$ iff adding n+n in base p has at least one carry (Kummer's theorem). Equivalently $v_p(\binom{2n}{n})>0$ by Legendre. This offers exact finite support enumeration, and the pair (87,88) can be directly checked without materializing a new theorem.
