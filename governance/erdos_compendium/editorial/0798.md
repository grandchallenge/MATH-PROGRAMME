## The question in plain language

t(n) is minimum number t of selected lattice points from grid [n]x[n] such that the infinite lines determined by ALL pairs of DISTINCT selected points cover every grid point. This is a self-generated line covering problem.

The key is to preserve the order of the quantifiers and the exact distinction between a finite example and a theorem about arbitrary large inputs. Replacing an extremum over every admissible set by an extremum over one convenient set would change the problem.

## A small example

For 2x2 grid, selecting all 4 grid points covers everything. No set of 3 works: its three lines are the three side or diagonal lines joining the three selected corners, none containing the fourth corner of a nondegenerate square. Hence t(2)=4, a finite check with distinct-point nondegenerate lines.

The calculation illustrates the exact definitions and hypotheses. No single finite example establishes the reported asymptotic assertion.
