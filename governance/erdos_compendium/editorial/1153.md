## The question in plain language

[Erdős Problem #1153](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1153). How large must interpolation amplification become on an arbitrary fixed subinterval, even if its interpolation nodes are chosen adversarially?

$$\max_{x\in[a,b]}\sum_{i=1}^{n}|\ell_i(x)|\ge\frac{2}{\pi}\log n-O_{a,b}(1).$$

For n distinct nodes x_i∈[-1,1], let l_i(x) be Lagrange basis and L(x)=sum|l_i(x)|. For any FIXED nondegenerate interval [a,b]⊆[-1,1], does max_{x∈[a,b]} L(x) ≥ (2/pi)log n - O_{a,b}(1) uniformly over node choices? A fixed interval is distinct from a single fixed evaluation point as in open #1132.

## A small example

Nodes -1,0,1 have L(1/2)=1/8+3/4+3/8=5/4 and max_{x∈[0,1]}L(x)=5/4. Bounded n=3 gives no sharp asymptotic theorem.
