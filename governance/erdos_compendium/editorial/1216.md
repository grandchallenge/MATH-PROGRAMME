## The question in plain language

[Erdős Problem #1216](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1216) currently has source status **DISPROVED — DIRECTED RAMSEY LOWER IMPROVED**.

For a tournament (orientation of every edge of $K_n$), let $f(n)$ be the **largest $t$ guaranteed in every tournament** such that it contains a **transitive subtournament** on $t$ vertices, equivalently a tournament with no directed triangle. The proposed exact formula was $f(n)=\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor+1$. An improved lower bound **strictly exceeding this value** for sufficiently large $n$ disproves the proposed equality; it does not determine the true exact $f(n)$ for all $n$.

## A small example

Exhaustive enumeration of **every** tournament on $n=3,4,5$ vertices (respectively $2^3,2^6,2^{10}$ orientations) computes, for each, its largest transitive subtournament and then minimizes over tournaments. The resulting exact guaranteed sizes are included in phase25 receipt. This small enumeration checks the basic definition; it cannot certify Reid–Parker's general improved inequality.

## What is known

Stearns [St59] proved $f(n)\ge\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor+1$ via a vertex of out-degree at least $(n-1)/2$, while Erdős–Moser [ErMo64] gave upper $f(n)\le2\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor+1$. Reid–Parker [RePa70] **DISPROVED exact equality for $n\ge14$**, proving a stronger lower bound $\lfloor\log_2n+4-\log_27\rfloor$. Sánchez-Flores [Sa94],[Sa98b] improved bounds for $n\ge55$, then $n\ge32$. Maintained https://www.erdosproblems.com/1216 reports $f(7)=3$, consistent with the original equality at some small values. Source proofs remain independently unreviewed.
