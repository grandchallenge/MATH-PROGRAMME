## The question in plain language

[Erdős Problem #1158](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1158). How dense can a uniform hypergraph be without containing the complete multipartite hypergraph whose edges choose one vertex from each class?

$$\operatorname{ex}_t(n,K_t(r))\ge n^{\,t-r^{1-t}-o(1)}.$$

K_t(r) is complete t-partite t-uniform hypergraph with r vertices in EACH of t classes, containing r^t transversal t-edges. Let ex_t(n,K_t(r)) be largest t-edges of an n-vertex t-graph avoiding that configuration. Ask for ALL integers t,r≥2 whether ex_t(n,K_t(r))≥n^{t-r^{1-t}-o(1)} as n→∞. Interpret -o(1) as an exponent asymptotic and distinguish an unspecified multiplicative constant and the upper bound exponent.

## A small example

For t=r=2, K_2(2)=K2,2=C4. Exhaustive 64 graph search on 4 labelled vertices finds ex_2(4,C4)=4. The proposed exponent is 2-2^{-1}=3/2. A single n=4 extremal count is not an asymptotic lower bound.

## What is known

https://www.erdosproblems.com/1158 OPEN [Er64f],[Va99,3.65]. Erdős derived n^{t-O(r^{1-t})}≤ex_t(n,K_t(r)) ≪ n^{t-r^{1-t}}. The sharp lower exponent proposed is reported known only for graph case t=2 with r=2 or 3. For graph t=2,r=2 this is C4-free graph extremal n^{3/2}; related #714. No general t≥3 theorem claimed.
