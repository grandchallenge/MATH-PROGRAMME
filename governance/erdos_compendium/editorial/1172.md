## The question in plain language

[Erdős Problem #1172](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1172) is classified **OPEN** in the maintained catalogue.

This entry is an **omnibus collection**, not one yes/no assertion. Erdős and Hajnal ask three positive two-color ordinal partition relations **assuming GCH**:

$$
\omega_3\to(\omega_2,\omega_1+2)^2,\quad
\omega_3\to(\omega_2+\omega_1,\omega_2+\omega)^2,\quad
\omega_2\to(\omega_1^{\omega+2}+2,\omega_1+2)^2.
$$

They separately ask whether $\omega_2\to(\omega_1+\omega)_2^2$ is **consistent with GCH**, or even $\omega_2\to(\xi)_2^2$ for all $\xi<\omega_2$. Do not confuse ordinal exponentiation, product and addition with cardinal arithmetic, or a theorem under GCH with a model satisfying GCH and an additional partition property.
