## The question in plain language

F(n) is maximum cardinality A subset [1,n] with no a divides bc when a,b,c in A, a != b and a != c; b=c is ALLOWED. The question asks whether F(n)=pi(n)+(C+o(1)) n^(2/3)/(log n)^2 and determines C.

**Proof-fidelity hold:** the precise constant 27/2 is reported by the maintained catalogue from a GPT-assisted proof, but its full proof and coefficient have not been independently reproduced here. The catalogue status PROVED is not GCL mathematical certification.

## A small example

A={2,3,4} is invalid: with a=4 and b=c=2, 4 divides 2*2 although a differs from both factors. Set of distinct primes <= n is admissible: if a is prime and differs from each prime b,c it cannot divide bc. Check n=4 gives admissible {2,3}. A={1} is admissible vacuously but cannot coexist with another member since 1 divides every product; vertex convention matters.

The calculation illustrates the exact definitions and hypotheses. No single finite example establishes the reported asymptotic assertion.
