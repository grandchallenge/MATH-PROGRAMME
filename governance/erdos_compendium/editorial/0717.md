## The question in plain language

For a graph G on n vertices with chromatic number χ(G), define σ(G) as the largest k for which G contains a topological K_k (a subdivision of the complete graph). Erdős and Fajtlowicz conjectured χ(G)≤C sqrt(n) σ(G)/log n for a universal C and sufficiently large n. The maintained source marks PROVED by Fox, Lee and Sudakov in 2013. This is not the original stronger Hajós conjecture χ(G)≤σ(G), which is false in general.

## A small example

For G=K_4, χ(G)=4 and σ(G)=4. A subdivision of K_k needs at least k branch vertices, so σ(K_4)=4. This one graph satisfies the theorem's qualitative dependence, but the asymptotic factor sqrt(n)/log n and the universal constant cannot be tested from a single small graph.

## What is known

PRIMARY AUTHOR abstract directly available: Jacob Fox, Choongbum Lee and Benny Sudakov, 'Chromatic number, clique subdivisions, and the conjectures of Hajós and Erdős–Fajtlowicz', Combinatorica 33 (2013), 181–197, DOI 10.1007/s00493-013-2853-x; author arXiv:1107.1920. It establishes the precise O(sqrt n/log n) bound for χ/σ; earlier Erdős–Fajtlowicz random graph lower bound shows sharpness up to constants. Catlin counterexamples invalidate the stronger Hajós bound, not the weaker proved statement. The exact original 1981 pages and whole published proof not independently read.
