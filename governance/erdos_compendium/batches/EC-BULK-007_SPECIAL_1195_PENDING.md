# EC-BULK-007 — #1195 integer-ratio-free infinite-measure set

**2026 Suan/GPT integral criterion PRIMARY PROOF FIDELITY PENDING.**

The maintained source https://www.erdosproblems.com/1195 states that for nondecreasing unbounded F on [1,infinity), there is a measurable S of infinite Lebesgue measure avoiding any integer ratio between distinct elements and satisfying measure(S intersect (0,x)) >= F(x) if and only if integral_1^infinity F(x)/x² dx is finite.

Verify published construction and necessity, especially whether F must be regular, whether inequality must hold for all or sufficiently large x, and the exact interpretation of ratio x/y as *any* positive integer. The norm here is Lebesgue **measure**, not a cardinality count.

Earlier Haight and Szemerédi proved existence of infinite-measure sets without integer ratios. Phase23 verifies only a finite interval example and two elementary integrals, not the general construction.
