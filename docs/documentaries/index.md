## About these editions

The files are the original compiled publications, unchanged. Their byte sizes and SHA-256 checksums agree with the previously protected [publication manifest](ARTIFACT_MANIFEST.json). The [PDF release register](PDF_RELEASES.json) records the public download location and identity of each file. The original manifest records their availability at admission; the later release register records their subsequent public publication.

The books are not announcements of new proofs. The books introduce important mathematics; they do **not** announce solutions to the open conjectures discussed. Illustrations are not mathematical proofs, and historical reading editions may predate later results. The Poincaré volume reconstructs an established theorem. The Navier–Stokes volume predates the [September–October 2026 developments](../MILLENNIUM_PRIZE_STATUS.md) and should be read alongside that updated account.

**The web editions and their illustrated plates are under reconstruction.** They have been removed from the main Books menu, but no archival source or original URL has been deleted.
