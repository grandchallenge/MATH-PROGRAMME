# The Shape of a Sphere

## A gentle illustrated guide to the Poincaré theorem

A lay journey from loops and three-manifolds through Ricci flow, controlled surgery, finite extinction, and backward reconstruction. The technical appendix preserves the Hamilton–Perelman source boundary and the distinction between pictorial analogy and topology.

## How to read this volume

Read the illustrated sections as orientation and memory. Read the formal definitions, theorem statements, scope qualifications, source notes, and technical appendix as the mathematical authority. Where an image compresses dimensions, substitutes a lower-dimensional analogy, or dramatizes an infinite process, the surrounding prose identifies the compression explicitly.

## Claim boundary

The monograph reconstructs the classical Hamilton–Perelman route. It makes no new-proof, novelty, priority, or independent-complete-verification claim.
