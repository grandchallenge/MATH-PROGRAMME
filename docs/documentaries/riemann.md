## How to read this volume

The musical and celestial plates provide memory and orientation. Analytic continuation, the completed xi-function, zero taxonomy, explicit formulas, equivalent criteria, computation protocols, and the technical appendix govern the mathematics.

## Claim boundary

Symmetry, extensive numerical verification, zero-density theorems, random-matrix agreement, prime-error heuristics, and spectral analogies do not prove that every nontrivial zero lies on the critical line.
