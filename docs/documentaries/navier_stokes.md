## How to read this volume

The fluid imagery supplies intuition. The whole-space and periodic formulations, solution classes, continuation criteria, scaling statements, partial-regularity theorems, campaign artifacts, and technical appendix govern the mathematics.

## Claim boundary

Energy bounds, numerical simulations, weak existence, small-data results, two-dimensional regularity, partial regularity, and progress in the `NS-CI-001` critical-integrability campaign are not promoted into a proof of global smoothness in three dimensions.
