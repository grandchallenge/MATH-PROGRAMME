# The River and the Storm

## Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness

A documentary treatment of velocity, pressure, viscosity, incompressibility, energy, vorticity, vortex stretching, critical scaling, weak solutions, partial regularity, and the exact three-dimensional smoothness question.

## How to read this volume

The fluid imagery supplies intuition. The whole-space and periodic formulations, solution classes, continuation criteria, scaling statements, partial-regularity theorems, and technical appendix govern the mathematics.

## Claim boundary

Energy bounds, numerical simulations, weak existence, small-data results, two-dimensional regularity, and partial regularity are not promoted into a proof of global smoothness in three dimensions.
