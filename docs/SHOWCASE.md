The aim is not to sound impressive. The aim is to make mathematical work cumulative, inspectable, and difficult to fool.

## The central transformation

| Stage | Governing question | Required artifact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curiosity | What might be worth studying? | Lead note |
| Reconstruction | What was actually asked? | Source map |
| Campaign | What exact obligation can be attacked? | Work Package |
| Evidence | What computation or proof attempt exists? | Claim ledger |
| Handoff | What can now be checked? | Certification packet |
| Certification | What crossed the proof boundary? | Checked artifact |

## The first cross-pillar lane

<div class="certificate-lane" aria-label="Algebraic witness to certificate lane">
  <span>Polynomial obligation</span><b>→</b><span>Witness search</span><b>→</b><span>Explicit certificate</span><b>→</b><span>Exact replay</span><b>→</b><span>Local lemma</span>

This is deliberately not a promise that Gröbner bases solve open problems. It is a disciplined way to use symbolic algebra without walking into worst-case complexity traps.

## Review posture

> I know what is proved, what is computed, what is conjectural, what failed, and what must happen next.
