## The manifest

<div class="manifest-rule">
  <span>Governing rule</span>
  <p>Route each polynomial application through a narrow obligation, explicit excluded inference, bounded method, and replayable certificate.</p>
</div>

Every lane below has a first fixture. None is activated merely because its
source object can be translated into polynomials.

<div class="manifest-foundation" aria-label="Checked application foundations">
  <div><span>Foundation 01</span><strong>Ideal membership</strong><p>Inequation encoding, exact rational replay, six rejected mutations.</p></div>
  <div><span>Foundation 02</span><strong>Radical membership</strong><p>Exponent-bearing witness, model-class boundary, retained countermodel.</p></div>
  <div><span>Selected next</span><strong>Automated geometry</strong><p>One coordinate theorem, one nondegeneracy condition, one source-language certificate.</p></div>
</div>

## Six application lanes

<div class="application-lanes">
  <article class="application-lane">
    <header><span>01 · queued</span><h2>Diophantine systems</h2></header>
    <div class="application-lane__body">
      <dl>
        <div><dt>Local obligation</dt><dd>Derive eliminants and algebraic branches before exact arithmetic reconstruction.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Boundary</dt><dd>Algebraic solutions do not by themselves prove integer or rational solvability.</dd></div>
      </dl>
      <p class="application-lane__fixture"><span>First fixture</span>Solve <code>x + y = 5</code>, <code>xy = 6</code>; lift both branches and certify integrality.</p>
    </div>
  </article>

  <article class="application-lane application-lane--next">
    <header><span>02 · next fixture</span><h2>Automated geometry</h2></header>
    <div class="application-lane__body">
      <dl>
        <div><dt>Local obligation</dt><dd>Compile a coordinate theorem into ideal or radical membership with nondegeneracy visible.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Boundary</dt><dd>An algebraic identity does not certify that coordinates represent exactly the intended configurations.</dd></div>
      </dl>
      <p class="application-lane__fixture"><span>First fixture</span>Certify a small coordinate theorem with one excluded degeneracy and a human-readable semantic bridge.</p>
    </div>
  </article>

  <article class="application-lane">
    <header><span>03 · queued</span><h2>Signal and image systems</h2></header>
    <div class="application-lane__body">
      <dl>
        <div><dt>Local obligation</dt><dd>Establish algebraic identifiability and enumerate exact reconstruction branches.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Boundary</dt><dd>Identifiability does not establish numerical stability, noise robustness, or perceptual quality.</dd></div>
      </dl>
      <p class="application-lane__fixture"><span>First fixture</span>Recover a two-tap exact signal from a noiseless convolution model and expose non-identifiable branches.</p>
    </div>
  </article>

  <article class="application-lane">
    <header><span>04 · queued</span><h2>Robotics</h2></header>
    <div class="application-lane__body">
      <dl>
        <div><dt>Local obligation</dt><dd>Eliminate auxiliary variables and isolate feasible real configurations.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Boundary</dt><dd>Complex solutions do not establish real reachability, collision freedom, or joint-limit feasibility.</dd></div>
      </dl>
      <p class="application-lane__fixture"><span>First fixture</span>Solve a planar two-link target with exact real-root isolation and joint-limit filtering.</p>
    </div>
  </article>

  <article class="application-lane">
    <header><span>05 · queued</span><h2>Sudoku and finite constraints</h2></header>
    <div class="application-lane__body">
      <dl>
        <div><dt>Local obligation</dt><dd>Certify existence, inconsistency, or uniqueness for one bounded instance.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Boundary</dt><dd>A polynomial encoding must still be compared with SAT and specialized combinatorial solvers.</dd></div>
      </dl>
      <p class="application-lane__fixture"><span>First fixture</span>Encode a 4 × 4 Sudoku, recover its solution, and certify uniqueness independently.</p>
    </div>
  </article>

  <article class="application-lane">
    <header><span>06 · queued</span><h2>Reconstruction and missing data</h2></header>
    <div class="application-lane__body">
      <dl>
        <div><dt>Local obligation</dt><dd>Determine completion branches and whether missing values are identifiable.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Boundary</dt><dd>Algebraic completion does not establish scientific, causal, historical, or palaeontological truth.</dd></div>
      </dl>
      <p class="application-lane__fixture"><span>First fixture</span>Complete one missing coordinate in an exact model and certify uniqueness or branching.</p>
    </div>
  </article>
</div>

## What “powerful” means here

<div class="power-boundary">
  <div><span>Power</span><p>One exact language can express elimination, implication, branching, finite quotient structure, and reconstruction across unrelated domains.</p></div>
  <div><span>Boundary</span><p>No automatic tractability, reality, integrality, stability, uniqueness, scientific interpretation, or theorem-level certification follows from polynomial form alone.</p></div>
</div>
