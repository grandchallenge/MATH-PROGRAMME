# GCL-TCS-00 1.0.0 compatibility statement

## Semantic compatibility

`GCL-TCS-00/1.0.0` is intended to preserve the substantive semantics of the reviewed `0.1.0` charter. Sections 6–17 are inherited by exact historical blob identity. Sections 1–5 and 18–20 retain their prior substantive rules while updating document control, version-bound examples, and the exact promotion/admission transition.

## Machine compatibility

The version-1 schemas preserve the existing field names, authority-state shapes, claim vocabulary, gate-record shape, exception-record shape, and conformance dimensions. They intentionally change version constants and schema identifiers from `0.1.0` to `1.0.0`. A strict `0.1.0` validator therefore will reject a version-1 declaration, as intended.

## Authority compatibility

The version-1 release candidate does not supersede or widen the authority of `0.1.0` until G9. Existing bounded-candidate-pilot authority remains attached to the historical admitted candidate. A later G9 PASS must state the exact supersession and rollback relationship.

## Known residual limitations

The release does not establish formal ASD-STE100 conformance. Representative false-positive and false-negative rates remain incompletely quantified. P04 exact environment reconstruction remains limited by the historical mutable runner. Operator and reviewer burden remains partly proxy-measured. These limitations must remain explicit in the new G8 review.
