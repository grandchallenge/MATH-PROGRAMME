# Grand Challenge Mathematics Programme

**A public front door for accountable mathematical understanding.**

MATH-PROGRAMME organizes open-problem work into three cooperating rooms: a foundry for discovery, a campaign room for disciplined attack, and an assay office for certification.

## The three rooms

| Room | Pillar | Work | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundry | MATHFORGE | source maps, examples, finite screens, conjecture candidates, symbolic witnesses, risk flags | promising, not proved |
| Campaign room | MATHSOLVE | Work Packages, theorem spines, reductions, failed routes, exact screens, certification handoffs | disciplined, not certified |
| Assay office | MATHCERT | Lean/equivalent proofs, exact replay, interval certificates, SAT/SMT artifacts, claim ledgers | certified or rejected |

```text
questions -> campaigns -> certificates
   |             |              |
   v             v              v
MATHFORGE -> MATHSOLVE -> MATHCERT
```

## What makes this Grand Challenge style

The programme does not confuse beautiful exposition with proof, nor formal syntax with communicated understanding. Every serious artifact must show:

- the object in view;
- the obstruction in focus;
- the claim boundary visible;
- the next move unmistakable.

> No theorem without a spine. No computation without a ledger. No conjecture without a map. No proof without a reader.
