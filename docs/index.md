<section class="gc-section">
  <div class="gc-section__heading">
    <p class="gc-kicker">A legible research discipline</p>
    <h2>Every artifact must reveal four things.</h2>
  </div>
  <ol class="principle-list">
    <li><span>01</span><strong>The object</strong><p>What, exactly, is under study?</p></li>
    <li><span>02</span><strong>The obstruction</strong><p>Where does genuine difficulty live?</p></li>
    <li><span>03</span><strong>The boundary</strong><p>What is proved, computed, conjectural, or unresolved?</p></li>
    <li><span>04</span><strong>The next move</strong><p>What concrete obligation advances the programme?</p></li>
  </ol>
</section>
