- demonstration-spine and certified-spine terminology under `CMDG-C08`;
- preservation of `CMDG-C03` through `CMDG-C06` as later stage gates;
- continuation of the MATHFORGE → MATHSOLVE → MATHCERT authority split;
- explicit metatheory/object-theory and foundational-concordance discipline;
- the ratified natural-number concordance, Euclid bridge, V0/V1/V2, and Condensed Mathematics implementation route.
