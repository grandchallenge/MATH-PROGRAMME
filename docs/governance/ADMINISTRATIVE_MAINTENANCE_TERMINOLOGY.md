# Administrative Maintenance Terminology Registry

**Registry extension:** `MP-ADMIN-TERMS-001`  
**Parent registry:** `docs/AGENT_COUNCIL_TERMINOLOGY_REGISTRY.md`  
**Decision lineage:** ADR-0016  
**Current interpretation:** amended by `MP-STREAMLINED-EXECUTION-001`

This controlled extension defines the maintenance vocabulary currently used by MATH-PROGRAMME. Terms that described the original accelerated pilot remain historical; they no longer create routine countdowns or approval gates.

| Term | Canonical meaning | Boundary and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| Core Clarity | The condition in which authority, material identity, current state, validating route, supporting evidence, permitted next action, and prohibited claims are explicit without inference. | Does not assert that the governed mathematics is correct or complete. |
| Material evidence closure | The governed changed bytes together with the directly consumed protected artifacts, validators, schemas, workflows, toolchain pins, policy bytes, authority, and claim scope that can affect the conclusion. | Unrelated repository-head movement is outside the closure. |
| Material change | A change that alters authority, lifecycle, route, claim, promotion, accepted evidence, prohibited use, consumed provider content, required workflow semantics, or another input in the material evidence closure. | Repository-head movement alone is not material when relevant consumed identities and semantics are unchanged. |
| Nonmaterial change | A change that preserves the material evidence closure, such as an unrelated protected commit, byte-identical synchronization, mirror repair, or typographic edit outside the governed conclusion. | Classification requires evidence; genuine ambiguity fails closed to the smallest defensible affected set. |
| Delegated routine disposition | Standing authority to complete bounded administrative, documentation, engineering, workflow, maintenance, routing, synchronization, and campaign-execution work through protected merge and readback once affected checks pass. | Does not authorize mathematical certification, source-semantic adjudication, constitutional expansion, security-sensitive weakening, or external claim promotion. |
| Concurrent protected development | Development in which protected `main` may advance without forcing every open candidate to update or rebase. | Candidate remains valid only while mergeable and its relevant dependencies, material closure, scope, and authority are unchanged. |
| Impact-routed CI | Selection of only the policy shards, formal lanes, or computational replays affected by the material transition, with stable aggregate required contexts where needed. | Does not exempt material changes from their substantive checks; scheduled/manual full sentinels remain available. |
| Protected evidence reuse | Completion of a required context by verifying that previously protected evidence remains valid for an unchanged material identity. | It is not caching a mathematical conclusion across changed inputs. |
| Event-triggered material propagation | Immediate update of materially affected cross-repository contracts after a governed change. | “Propagation” does not mean branch rebasing or repository-head synchronization when consumed artifacts are unchanged. |
| Workflow coverage | The recorded combination of capability, trigger, required-context role, affected-input rule, execution or protected-reuse evidence, owner, repair route, and last verified material identity. | A workflow file, historical green run, or monolithic full-suite execution alone is insufficient. |
| Canonical tracker | The designated issue or navigation surface that points to protected authority, current lifecycle and route state, next obligation, claim boundary, and review trigger. | It cannot create protected state. |
| Issue mirror | A mutable issue, pull request body, or comment that explains or navigates protected authority. | It cannot change lifecycle, route, certification, or claim state. |
| Specialist review | Independent non-author scrutiny required by a substantive mathematical, source-semantic, constitutional, security-sensitive, or external-claim boundary. | Not a generic requirement for routine bounded administration. |
| Protected readback | Verification after merge that the intended artifact and authority state are present on protected state. | It is evidence, not a second approval cycle. |
| Administrative waiver | A typed, scoped, expiring exception with owner, evidence, approver, prohibited uses, and repair obligation. | It cannot authorize claim promotion; critical waiver classes remain subject to their reserved authority. |
| Emergency override | A temporary bounded control used only to restore availability, respond to a security incident, or restore CI operability. | It cannot promote, certify, delete evidence, or leave repository protection weakened. |
| Maintenance-burden circuit breaker | The principle that administrative machinery is narrowed, consolidated, automated, or retired when recurring cost exceeds the material risk it reduces. | It does not suspend unrelated campaigns because a calendar interval elapsed. |
| Campaign-level fail closed | Suspension of the affected promotion or transition when a critical required capability or evidence boundary is materially missing or stale. | Does not automatically suspend unrelated campaigns. |
| Protected-merge activation | The rule that an artifact whose authority requires protected admission becomes operative only through the applicable protected merge/readback condition. | A branch, issue, comment, or review alone cannot create protected authority. |
| Final cross-repository closure | A state in which materially affected provider/consumer contracts and protected identities agree and required readback is complete. | Does not require ceremonial repinning of unchanged artifacts and does not promote mathematical or external claims. |

## Historical terms

The following ADR-0016 terms remain useful for reading historical records but no longer impose a current routine timer:

- **Accelerated maintenance time scale** — the original Human Steward rule multiplying proposed pilot durations by `0.1`.
- **Accelerated pilot** — the original nine-day maintenance pilot beginning at PR #184 protected admission.
- **Tracker refresh clock** — the original `PT7H12M` issue-mirror target.
- **INTELLECT Phase A buy-in / Phase B protected adoption** — the original two-stage INTELLECT admission sequence.

Current cadence is governed by `docs/governance/AGENT_CADENCE_OPERATING_DESIGN.md`; current routine execution by `docs/governance/STREAMLINED_EXECUTION_AMENDMENT.md`; current CI evidence by `docs/WORKFLOW_COVERAGE.md`.

## Change control

A term in this extension may change through a governed change that identifies the affected control, material consistency checks, applicable review evidence, supersession boundary, and claim boundary. Routine terminology/editorial maintenance uses standing delegated authority; a change that alters constitutional authority, promotion semantics, or a substantive claim boundary receives the specialist review appropriate to that material change.
