# Frontier Advancement Gate

The Frontier Advancement Gate separates safe evidence transport from mathematical research scheduling.

A completed `GCL-CONTRIBUTION-RESULT/1` does not itself authorize a successor. `Next residual` is evidence supplied by the contributor.

A successor requires a protected disposition: `ADVANCE_FRONTIER`, `INDEPENDENT_VERIFY`, `FORMALIZE`, `ADVERSARIAL_ATTACK`, `SOURCE_BLOCKED`, `REFUTED`, `CLOSED_NO_SUCCESSOR`, or `CAMPAIGN_TERMINAL`.

The default independent replay budget for a bounded deterministic claim is one. Additional replay requires a named reason such as discrepancy, material artifact change, certification, or deliberate adversarial challenge.

`claim_effect: NONE` normally closes the operational contribution without creating a mathematically equivalent successor.
