# Agent-cadence operating design

Status: **design candidate; not active authority**

This document replaces the proposed nine-day programme activation in pull
request #493. It does not activate a schedule, start a clock, authorize a
merge, expand automation authority, or change any mathematical, certification,
publication, production, constitutional, or commercial boundary.

## Decision

Do not build a second programme-wide execution control plane merely to compress
the former 90-day plan into nine days.

The exact P9D candidate at
`4abe7653e74a3a1fd16e8b9e72e5c61646718241` was returned for revision by all
ten Council offices. Its fixed clock required new activation, lineage, state,
dependency, cutoff, resource, retirement, and custody machinery. That cost and
complexity are not justified when the programme already has narrower controls
for the properties that matter.

The P9D activation proposal, transform manifest, activation schema, and special
validator are therefore withdrawn. Their adverse review history remains in
`grandchallenge/INTELLECT` matter
`GI-COUNCIL-AGENT-CADENCE-OPERATING-PLAN-001` and must not be represented as an
approval.

## Operating rule

Programme work proceeds campaign by campaign, not under one global countdown.

1. Existing constitutional, programme, repository, and certification authority
   remains unchanged.
2. Each campaign names its own protected predecessor, exact candidate, bounded
   question, checks, review requirements, terminal condition, and nonclaims.
3. Every repository workflow passes mandatory execution routing. Work requiring
   unattended persistence uses an admitted persistent controller; a
   conversational agent is never the sole long-running controller.
4. Multi-session or asynchronous governed work uses the programme's admitted
   durable checkpoint control when that control is protected and active.
5. A changed candidate invalidates old checks, diagnostics, reviews, and
   approvals. Evidence is always rebound to the exact current head.
6. A campaign may end in success, a bounded negative result, a characterized
   blocker, supersession, or withdrawal. Time pressure never upgrades evidence
   or claims.
7. Human action is requested only for authority actually reserved to a human.
   Silence, elapsed time, automation, or a prior disposition never supplies
   that authority.

## Capacity and priority

The programme does not invent unmeasured global quotas. When available
controller, CI, or independent-review capacity is insufficient, new dispatches
stop and already-started work remains at its last durable state.

Priority is deterministic:

1. integrity, protection, and recovery defects;
2. completion or safe parking of already-active bounded transactions;
3. independently reviewable campaign candidates;
4. new exploratory work.

Maintenance automation must coalesce obsolete generated candidates instead of
accumulating a queue of stale branches.

## Completion and recovery

There is no programme-wide `T0`, observation cutoff, or terminal packet.
Completion is established separately for each governed work item by its exact
terminal evidence and protected readback. A replacement agent reconstructs the
current position from repository and GitHub state, not from conversation.

If a work item lacks enough durable state for cold recovery, it pauses at the
last proven transition until a bounded checkpoint is created. This document
does not itself create or approve such a checkpoint.

## Council findings

The Council's nine repair conditions remain valid findings about the withdrawn
P9D architecture. Conditions 1 through 8 are not claimed complete and are not
silently waived; the architecture that required them is no longer proposed for
activation. This redesigned candidate satisfies only the procedural direction
of condition 9: it is new bytes requiring fresh validation and fresh review,
with no carry-forward of the former candidate's checks, reviews, mergeability,
authorization, activation, or deadline.

## Adoption boundary

Merging this design would record the withdrawal and replacement design only.
It would not activate a new programme regime. Any future proposal to create a
global timed plan or to alter authority must be tabled as a separate exact
candidate under the then-current governance process.
