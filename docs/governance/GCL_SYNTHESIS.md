# GCL Synthesis Pilot

`GCL-SYNTHESIS-WP00` Tranche 1 is a bounded transfer-analysis control.

It binds every transfer claim to exact protected source artifacts, requires explicit source and target assumptions, preserves non-transferability, records contradictions and duplication without prose harmonization, and produces deterministic report and review-packet projections.

The pilot contains exactly four transfer records:

- three accepted bounded governance transfers;
- one rejected cross-domain analogy.

It also contains exactly four contradiction or duplication records covering Git/GitHub versus optional AETHER workspace semantics, candidate evidence versus realizability and product claims, portfolio versus synthesis responsibilities, and reuse of truth-spine identity controls.

Accepted transfers authorize only their recorded validator or review consequence. They do not activate downstream work packages. Rejected analogies carry no executable authority.

The review packet names Axiomatist, Cartographer, Verifier, Adversary, Formalist, Amanuensis, Referee, and Human Steward. Candidate rows are questions, not approvals.

This pilot does not prove mathematics, establish source-target equivalence, issue certification, authorize publication, determine novelty or patentability, validate products, or create commercial authority.
