# Domain 04 · Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer

**Campaign ID:** `BSD-001`  
**Mathematical status:** open conjecture  
**Canonical field:** elliptic curves over `Q`  
**Governance:** `ADR-0005`

## Canonical challenge

The campaign separates three nearby obligations that must not be conflated:

- equality of Mordell–Weil rank and complex analytic rank;
- finiteness of the Tate–Shafarevich group;
- the strong leading-term formula with all normalization data explicit.

A parity statement, family result, one-prime theorem, finite computation, or `p`-adic formula does not by itself establish the universal complex conjecture.

## Programme posture

WP00–WP04 have completed source normalization, false-proof analysis, theorem-ledger construction, computational/formal substrate review, and restricted-target selection. The selected target `BSD-R2-A1` remains unproved. No WP05 or theorem-strengthening stage is inferred from promotion of the documentary stack.
