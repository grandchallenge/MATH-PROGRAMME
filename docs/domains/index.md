# Programme Domains

<p class="page-deck">The public catalogue of governed mathematical campaigns. Each entry identifies the current claim boundary, canonical repository artifact, and permitted next movement.</p>

The programme has three mathematical execution pillars—MATHFORGE, MATHSOLVE, and MATHCERT—plus the MATH-PROGRAMME governance and integration layer. Domain state is recorded in `DOMAIN_REGISTRY.yaml`; detailed claims remain authoritative in the linked master plans, Work Packages, ledgers, reviews, and certificates.

## Reading rule

A domain landing page is an orientation artifact, not a theorem ledger. Before relying on a mathematical statement, follow its canonical master plan to the relevant claim ledger, review record, proof, source, or certificate route.

The programme never infers theorem status from domain activity, document count, Referee disposition, or public visibility.
