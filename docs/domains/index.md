# Mathematical Estate

<p class="page-deck">The standing catalogue of governed mathematical domains. It records scope and canonical entry points; it is not a priority list and does not imply current activity.</p>

## How to use the estate

The estate answers a different question from [Current Work](../CURRENT_WORK.md).

- **Current Work** shows material obligations now in motion.
- **Mathematical Estate** shows the standing domains that the programme preserves.
- **Results and Exemplars** shows bounded outputs that crossed a declared checking or publication boundary.

A domain can remain in the estate while no active front is running. An active front can also cross domain or programme boundaries.

## Domain authority

`DOMAIN_REGISTRY.yaml` records the machine-readable domain estate. Each public domain page points to its canonical repository entry.

The public landing page is an orientation artifact. It is not the theorem ledger. Before relying on a mathematical statement, follow the domain page to the relevant source record, Work Package, claim ledger, proof, review, or certificate.

## Priority is a separate axis

The estate does not rank domains by importance, difficulty, or current attention.

For example, the current BSD work is one active front inside a standing domain. OZ order-7 certificate work is active programme research but is not represented by one of the eight historical domain slots. VGSE has moved from a bounded mathematical certification result into a separate engineering-discovery lane.

This separation prevents a durable taxonomy from becoming a stale description of present work.

## Claim boundary

The programme does not infer theorem status from:

- presence in the domain catalogue;
- document count;
- issue activity;
- repository merge;
- CI success;
- Referee or governance disposition;
- documentary publication;
- public visibility.

Mathematical support remains attached to the exact claim and its governed evidence route.
