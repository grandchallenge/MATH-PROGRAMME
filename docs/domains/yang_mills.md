# Domain 06 · Yang–Mills Existence and Mass Gap

**Campaign ID:** `YM-001`  
**Mathematical status:** open problem  
**Programme state:** WP00 source-normalization dossier merged  
**Governance:** `ADR-0009`

## Canonical challenge

For every compact simple gauge group, construct a nontrivial four-dimensional quantum Yang–Mills theory satisfying an accepted axiomatic floor and prove a strictly positive finite physical mass gap.

Existence and mass gap are separate but jointly required theorem trunks. A fixed lattice model, finite-volume gap, perturbative expansion, numerical spectrum, confinement heuristic, or area-law result does not by itself solve the Clay problem.

## Programme posture

`YM-WP00` fixes the official statement, Wightman and Osterwalder–Schrader floor, quantifiers, regulator and reconstruction interfaces, gap normalization, exclusion ledger, and formal proposition schemas. It is a source-normalized non-solution artifact.

The next documentary stages are the false-proof atlas and source-normalized theorem ledger. Mechanism generation and numerical experimentation remain gated by those controls.

The programme has not constructed four-dimensional quantum Yang–Mills theory, proved a mass gap, confinement, or an area law, or established that an existing lattice, perturbative, stochastic, numerical, or gauge-fixed construction satisfies the complete theorem target.
