**Millennium status, 20 October 2026:** Clay still lists the general Hodge conjecture as **Unsolved**. This is a research claim subject to assessment, not a proof of the general conjecture or a GCL-certified result. [Full comparison](../MILLENNIUM_PRIZE_STATUS.md).

## The question in everyday language

Many geometric shapes can be described using algebraic equations. The Hodge conjecture asks whether certain features discovered through more general geometric methods can always be explained using algebraic pieces of the shape.

This is a question about a precise class of complex algebraic varieties and rational-number coefficients. Changing those conditions can turn the conjecture into a different statement.

GCL has focused on identifying the exact mathematical formulation and comparing sources. **What remains uncertain:** no new case of the Hodge conjecture has been proved by this programme.

## Canonical challenge

For every smooth projective complex variety `X` and codimension `p`, determine whether every rational Hodge class of type `(p,p)` lies in the rational span of algebraic cycle classes.

The campaign preserves the rational Hodge conjecture exactly. Integral variants, generalized Hodge conjectures, analytic substitutes, and numerical evidence are separate statements.

## Programme posture

The authorized work is source, normalization, and equivalence control. A reformulation is accepted only when the ambient category, coefficient field, cycle-class map, and quantifiers are preserved. No theorem-strengthening claim is made.
