    <section class="minderling-panels" id="flow">
      <article class="minderling-panel minderling-panel--dark">
        <h2>Why these companions?</h2>
        <p>Complex proof programmes need memorable roles. The Minderlings help readers onboard faster, communicate precisely, and notice when a claim tries to cross the wrong boundary.</p>
        <ul><li>Shared language</li><li>Clear responsibilities</li><li>Fewer handoff errors</li><li>Stronger verification habits</li></ul>
      </article>
      <article class="minderling-panel" id="doctrine">
        <h2>Doctrine in four questions</h2>
        <ol>
          <li>What is proved?</li>
          <li>What is checked?</li>
          <li>What remains open?</li>
          <li>What requires external verification?</li>
        </ol>
        <p>See the <a href="GRAND_CHALLENGE_PEDAGOGY_STANDARD.md">Pedagogy Standard</a> for the full reader contract.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="minderling-panel minderling-panel--flow">
        <h2>Stack flow at a glance</h2>
        <p><strong>MATHFORGE</strong> emits candidate ore and artifacts.</p>
        <p><strong>MATHSOLVE</strong> turns artifacts into Work Packages, obligations, and tactics.</p>
        <p><strong>MATHCERT</strong> checks certificates, claim ledgers, and replay routes.</p>
      </article>
    </section>

    <section class="minderling-deployment" id="deployment">
      <div>
        <p class="minderling-kicker">Release-train deployment</p>
        <h2>Use them as mnemonic operating roles, not authorities.</h2>
        <p>The Minderlings are a reader aid and coordination device. They do not certify mathematics, replace reviewers, or create theorem status. Their purpose is to help people mentally organize the programme under the <a href="GRAND_CHALLENGE_PEDAGOGY_STANDARD.md">Grand Challenge Pedagogy Standard</a> and the <a href="FOUNDATION_AWARE_MATH_PROGRAMME.md">Foundation-Aware Doctrine</a>.</p>
      </div>
