# GCL-ERDOS3

GCL-ERDOS3 is the standalone successor research campaign promoted from the OPENMATH-2026 H7 corpus.

Its purpose is dual: advance Erdős Problem 3 and validate frontier-governed research execution. Operational contribution handling remains automated, but successor selection is controlled by the mathematical dependency frontier.

Current protected Solve frontier:
- D3 arithmetic-progression truncation: formalized.
- D4 frequent-long-AP / every-finite-length bridge: formalized.
- D5 finite-deletion, tail, and residue-fibre lemmas: staged, not yet proof-receipted.
- scale-local reciprocal-mass bridge: open.
- bridge into the D4 conclusion interface: open.

Initial work is E3-F01, E3-B01, E3-A01, and E3-S01. These are sibling attacks on named frontier nodes, not a numbered replay chain.
