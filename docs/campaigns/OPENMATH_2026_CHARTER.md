# OPENMATH-2026 — bounded six-hill sprint campaign

**Programme tracker:** `grandchallenge/MATH-PROGRAMME#1072`  
**Event window:** 2026-09-27 through 2026-10-02  
**Initial state:** `EXTERNAL_HILL_LOCK_PENDING`  
**Authority:** bounded campaign execution under existing MATH-PROGRAMME / MATHFORGE / MATHSOLVE / MATHCERT doctrine

## Purpose

Use the OpenMath 2026 competition as a live field test of the Grand Challenge mathematics stack.

The campaign objective is not merely to produce a competition submission. It is to test whether a versioned external mathematical target can be transformed into a governed parallel research graph that preserves source identity, failed routes, useful intermediate mathematics, formal artifacts, replay evidence, and claim boundaries.

The canonical research flow is:

```text
external OpenMath hill
        |
        v
MATHFORGE source/status lock
        |
        v
MATHSOLVE obligation graph
        |
        +--> bounded internal routes
        |
        +--> zero-context CEI contributions
        |
        v
GCL adjudication
        |
        v
MATHCERT replay/formal checking
        |
        v
competition-ready artifact
```

## Required per-hill source lock

Before `OM26-Hn` advances beyond intake, MATHFORGE must record:

1. exact AutoLab hill/list identity and version where exposed;
2. exact mathematical statement as presented by the competition;
3. Ulam/UnsolvedMath identifier and difficulty rating where applicable;
4. primary or authoritative source statement;
5. dated open-status triage;
6. known-equivalence and statement-fidelity hazards;
7. evaluator/checker identity and approved formal environment when available;
8. artifact and provenance requirements;
9. exact competition-rule version affecting the claim;
10. a non-certification statement.

If these cannot be established, the hill remains `SOURCE_LOCK_BLOCKED`.

## Thin sprint profile

OpenMath is a one-week campaign. Administrative weight must protect material epistemic boundaries without turning into research latency.

Mandatory during the sprint:

- exact source/hill lock;
- claim ledger;
- proof-obligation decomposition;
- route/failure ledger;
- durable resumable state;
- formal environment lock;
- provenance/tool disclosure;
- independent adjudication before Programme promotion;
- MATHCERT handoff for claim-bearing outputs.

Deferred unless needed for mathematical work:

- monograph-quality pedagogy;
- full programme-wide synthesis;
- nonessential documentary polish;
- post-campaign migration and archival consolidation.

This profile changes no pillar authority.

## Parallel work model

A locked hill should be decomposed into independently useful obligations rather than copied wholesale to multiple agents.

Preferred graph:

```text
semantic/source lock
        |
        +--> formalization prerequisites
        +--> normal-form / reduction route
        +--> special-case / restricted theorem route
        +--> exact-computational route
        +--> falsification / counterexample route
        +--> independent CEI contribution route
        |
        v
candidate claim graph
        |
        v
adjudication
        |
        v
MATHCERT
```

Artificial fragmentation into cosmetic corollaries is prohibited.

## CEI use

The Controlled Epistemic Interface may be used to obtain zero-context contributions once a hill or sub-obligation can be fully specified in one bounded dispatch.

A CEI return is evidence received, not mathematics accepted.

The intake path must preserve:

- exact dispatch identity;
- exact returned bytes;
- contributor/source identity;
- receipt and digest;
- `canonical_claim_effect=false` on intake;
- later separate adjudication.

## Success conditions

This campaign may succeed through any combination of:

- a competition-accepted formal result;
- a useful independently replayable formalization;
- a new restricted theorem;
- a decisive counterexample;
- a meaningful reduction;
- a reusable library contribution;
- a route-eliminating negative result;
- a formally characterized blocker;
- a high-quality source/status correction.

A competition score is not itself a MATHCERT disposition.

## Claim boundary

This campaign does not by its existence establish:

- correctness of any OpenMath submission;
- novelty or priority;
- that any listed problem remains open beyond its dated source audit;
- semantic fidelity of a machine-checked artifact;
- independence of an AI contribution;
- certification;
- publication or promotion authority.

The existing invariant remains controlling:

> No Grand Intellect mathematics without a Solve route. No Solve route without lineage. No mathematical claim without a Cert handoff. No promotion by implication, omission, or repository accident.
