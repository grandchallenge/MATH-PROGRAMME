## Role of this document

This handoff is a continuity map for the successor operation tracked by #788. It is not authority, evidence, review, certification, promotion, or a substitute for protected repository state. Re-fetch controlling artifacts before acting.

## Purpose

Convert the completed GHOS bounded GCL-TCS/GCL-POS pilot from a strong one-time exemplar into a calibrated, low-friction, self-monitoring and maintainable institutional learning loop.

This operation does not reopen `GHOS-ESTATE-ROLLOUT-001`, promote GCL-TCS/POS to v1.0, create a new approval bureaucracy, or create mathematical/certification/publication/constitutional/production/commercial authority.

## Controlling execution doctrine

`MP-STREAMLINED-EXECUTION-001` controls routine execution.

For bounded administrative, documentation, engineering, maintenance, routing, synchronization, evidence-indexing, pilot-record and implementation work, standing delegated authority is sufficient after affected material-closure checks pass.

Routine sequence:

`classify material closure -> run affected checks -> exercise delegated disposition -> protected merge -> protected readback`

There is no generic Human Steward approval stage and no generic Referee approval stage. Do not request approval because a commit changed, `main` advanced, a template contains G8/G9 fields, or the work merely feels consequential.

Require Human Steward, Referee, independent specialist or other reserved disposition only when the controlling instrument materially reserves the transition: for example substantive mathematical/certification authority, constitutional expansion, security-sensitive weakening/controller transition, external claim promotion, or an actual candidate-standard promotion gate.

Where separation of duties is materially required, independence is determined by the governing instrument and the relationship between author, reviewer, artifact and claim. Do not manufacture independence by changing role labels. Do not assume every G8 must be human; do not assume every separate agent is independent.

Evidence binds to a material closure, not indiscriminately to repository-head freshness. Unrelated movement of protected `main` does not invalidate materially unchanged evidence. Do not manufacture rebases, synchronization merges, CI reruns or re-approvals merely to refresh numerical ancestry.

CI must remain proportional. Unknown material impact fails closed into the smallest defensible affected set; it does not automatically imply a full-estate replay. Expensive replay/full sentinels run on relevant material changes, explicit dispatch, or deliberately low-frequency assurance schedules.

## Domain-authority firewall

GCL-TCS evaluates communication, provenance, review, claim-boundary and promotion-control properties. It does not silently inherit domain authority.

A mathematical pilot does not prove or certify mathematics and does not replace MATHCERT/proof/replay evidence. An experimental pilot does not establish scientific truth beyond its underlying evidence. A software pilot does not create production/security authority. A governance pilot does not expand constitutional authority. A public pilot may simplify exposition but cannot strengthen the source claim and may remain unpublished if publication authority is reserved.

Apply `MATH_CORE_INTEGRITY.md` to mathematical pilots.

## Governing optimization criterion

When two procedures provide equal protection of the material boundary, choose the one with fewer gates, fewer reruns, fewer actors, and less persistent machinery.
